How to browse and compare artwork

Current artwork is the default. Pick a category, region, or appearance, then narrow further only if needed. The search also recognizes exact asset IDs, artwork types, tags, and area names.

Game selection uses the renderer's current choice for the selected state. Procedural shows the code-drawn version, even where a sprite normally takes precedence. Sprite shows a registered or retained image, when one exists. Compare puts both side by side.

Studio close-up enlarges the art for inspection; 100%, 150%, and 200% are zoom settings for isolated artwork, not proof of its appearance inside a full game scene. Day/night adds a study tint; it does not reproduce every environmental light or Pixi effect.

Open How this artwork is defined to see the actual renderer input, documented design decisions, source files and matching code lines. The showroom is read-only. Copy a link or note to discuss an asset without changing the game.

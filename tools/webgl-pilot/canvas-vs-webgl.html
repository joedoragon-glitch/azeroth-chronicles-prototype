Azeroth Chronicles: Canvas Only vs WebGL Only

Just two graphics paths. Both use actual Azeroth's approved world, textures, roads, buildings, character art, lighting and camera. No synthetic shapes and no hybrid layering during measured frames.

Important limitation: the WebGL-only frame loop renders a prepared real-world texture. That complete world artwork is baked once by the original game renderer during preparation. WebGL then moves and displays it without calling Canvas during its measured frame. This is not a fully native WebGL recreation of animated enemies, evolving combat effects, or every original procedural drawing command. Don't compare its frame time to live Canvas as equivalent gameplay workloads; the report exposes that difference rather than calling it a win.

Ready. Neither path reads nor writes your campaign saves.

## Multi-phase execution: classify blockers, defer safely, keep moving (31 October 2026)

**Standing owner direction — applies project-wide to every multi-phase workflow**, including regions/interiors, combat and companions, AI/navigation, Pixi/WebGL graphics, performance, menus, saves, audio, QA and future workstreams. The owner regularly works across chats and may be AFK. Within the **already authorized scope**, the implementer is responsible for assessing test failures and discovered issues **without waiting for the owner to request triage**. A minor, unrelated or later-phase concern must not automatically strand an otherwise completed phase. Neither speed nor an impressive green status justifies overlooking real quality defects or silently changing owner requirements.

**Default behavior: do the work, not assign the owner homework.** When the owner gives a clear authorized task, deliver the result using normal independent engineering judgment. Do not introduce extra owner checklists, mandatory audits, new approval ceremonies, repeated clarifications, or a separate PR/issue for each tiny finding. Use the smallest sufficient source review and focused validation; bundle related corrections with their owning phase. The owner's attention is for genuinely missing creative decisions, consequential trade-offs, changed scope or explicit approval gates—not routine debugging, test interpretation or project administration. A small amount of tracked noncritical debt is preferable to blocking useful work, **provided it has no material impact on the current required outcome**.

**Classify the evidence against the actual phase exit contract before repeatedly debugging:**

- **BLOCK NOW** if a proven or plausibly material failure undermines the current phase's required outcome, a direct owner instruction or a necessary prerequisite for the next phase; a relevant original regression fails without a defensible independent explanation; saves/security/licensing/game stability, crucial audio/combat warnings, current budget limits or player-facing quality are seriously at risk; or the intended implementation would scale a known design/quality defect. An unexplained relevant failure cannot be marked passed by running a narrower substitute test. Consent gates for broad testing, merging, deployment, device verification and owner acceptance remain binding.
- **DEFER WITH OWNERSHIP** if a focused assessment establishes that the issue is *independent of the present phase's required acceptance*: e.g. a demonstrated unrelated pre-existing failure, an explicitly later-phase browser/device check, noncritical polish, a bounded improvement or an unproven theoretical risk with no current blocking evidence. The phase may be marked **GO with a documented deferral**, not “every test passed” or “no problems.” Defer only within the owner's explicitly permitted quality and acceptance boundaries.
- **INVESTIGATE BRIEFLY** when impact or causality is unknown. Inspect exact logs/commit/base, reproduce with the smallest relevant test, compare original behavior, and determine whether current acceptance is compromised. A failed relevant test with unresolved impact remains a **BLOCK**; limited time alone is not permission to waive it. Do not rerun unchanged successful CI indefinitely or launch a full suite without permission.

**Handle deferrals proportionately, not ceremonially:** Fix trivial independent items in the current authorized work when inexpensive. For a small nonblocking note, use **one short entry in the existing phase issue/PR**, not a new issue, PR, document, checklist or approval request. Open **one scoped follow-up issue** only when substantive independent remediation really warrants tracking beyond the current phase; open a **separate draft PR** only when there is a concrete independent implementation to propose. For such actionable follow-ups, identify what is known versus hypothetical, why the current phase can still pass, the next responsible action and the **revisit-before dependency or phase** (when there is one). Group related items, keep the debt list deliberately small, and don't create tickets just to document that a harmless concern exists. Recheck only before affected dependent gates; close or address a finding before it becomes a genuine prerequisite. Any deferral that changes an owner's explicit quality or acceptance requirement still needs their decision.

**Owner freedom is a delivery requirement, not a convenience.** The owner is **not** a CI watcher, routine bug triager, or session babysitter. Within an authorized active work session, take ownership of diagnosis, implementation, scoped checks, decision-making and GitHub status. If a relevant check is still running, make useful independent progress and record its exact pending URL; do **not** require the owner to stay online or repeatedly poll the same workflow as a substitute for engineering judgment. Once all useful authorized steps in the current session are complete, leave a short, reliable resume record stating what passed, what remains pending and the next actionable task. Do not imply that execution continues automatically after the conversation ends. In another authorized work session, verify pending outcomes before relying on them. A material true blocker still holds the affected work and must be reported accurately.

**When working AFK or across chats:** Continue other authorized independent steps; make a source-grounded GO / HOLD / GO-with-deferrals decision and update the existing GitHub issue, docs, tested SHA and handoff. Ask the owner only for creative authority, consequential risk acceptance, changed scope or consent-controlled work. **Never claim invisible background execution, an unperformed perceptual test, a completed merge/deploy or device verification.** A separately requested chat/phase boundary still applies. This classification policy does not authorize skipping sound-design/visual/experience quality review merely because it is not machine-testable.

## Standing documentation ownership — part of every change (28 October 2026)

**Engineering responsibility, not an extra request from the owner:** Whoever implements an approved game change must keep its affected documentation accurate *while the work is happening*. This applies to planning, designs, game code, art, menus, balance, saves, tests, releases and interrupted work. Do not defer documentation to an occasional repository-wide cleanup, and do not ask the owner to manage this process.

- **Reuse before creating.** Search the existing current guide, design contract and feature PR/issue before adding a Markdown file. Prefer editing the established owner document; create a separate record only if it carries genuinely distinct design evidence, acceptance results or a necessary handoff. Avoid competing "current" status pages and repeated facts.
- **Maintain every touched document continuously.** At each meaningful implementation change or checkpoint, reconcile *all documents created or edited during that feature* plus any affected maintained instructions (including player-facing English/Spanish guidance). If an approach is revised, update the active document and clearly mark the old plan as historical/superseded, keeping its original evidence intact. A feature that generated ten documents is responsible for checking all ten, not just the latest handoff.
- **Make the status truthful.** Separate **planned**, **implemented**, **tested** (with exact test/head evidence), **merged**, **published**, and **device-verified**. A draft's successful CI is not a release; publishing does not establish that an installed phone or Chromebook received or accepted it. Do not write speculative future status as fact.
- **Reconcile before completion or handoff.** Before calling a stage complete, review the changed behavior against current source and approved decisions, check the affected documentation for contradictory figures, obsolete instructions, missing links and misleading "next steps", and update the owning PR/issue's short resume record. If documentation is still pending, disclose it as unfinished work; do not report an unreconciled feature as fully complete.
- **Close the release-status loop.** When merge/publication is separately authorized, verify the actual GitHub merge and publishing outcomes and reconcile the maintained [development-state ledger](docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATE.md) and affected current entrypoints in the appropriate authorized change; do not silently declare a draft shipped or rely on stale historical preambles. Use source and owner-approved design as authority for gameplay facts, not a document timestamp or test assumption.
- **Keep the workflow lightweight.** No duplicate documentation registry, new mandatory reports or review ceremony, broad test matrix, automated rewriting of historical records, game-behavior edits, or extra release permission is implied. Documentation is part of the feature's normal definition of done; [the engineering change workflow](docs/MECHANIC_DEVELOPMENT.md) provides practical checks for mechanics, menus, audio and other scoped changes.

- **Pre-publication docs gate:** The existing focused workflow runs the lightweight offline `scripts/docs-release-consistency.cjs` check before its build can publish. Every build-version bump must update the active `docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATE.md` source-version marker and visible build line **in the same implementation PR**. Keep README, this policy and the documentation index free of independent 'latest build' numbers. Game-facing runtime changes must include an affected existing feature/player documentation update in the same PR, even without a version bump. Automation checks documentation presence and current-version/status consistency; PR review checks semantic accuracy. Never predeclare a candidate published before the exact GitHub Pages run succeeds; after an authorized publication, reconcile its verified receipt in the existing Development state. This does not introduce a broad test suite, extra workflow, automatic merge or device-verification claim.

## Active operating rules — current owner consent applies

The rules here govern authorized work today. Dated historical tasks and approvals above do not independently reauthorize older features, broad tests, merges or deployments.

- The multi-file Campaign application is canonical. Never require a self-contained HTML release or impose obsolete single-file limits.
- **Branding for every new chat and asset:** The **official public game title** is **So I Became the Dark Lord's Problem** (the English spelling is canonical). **SIBDLP** is only the friendly shorthand; never shorten it to **SIBDL** or imply the hero is the Dark Lord. **Azeroth Chronicles** is the historical/internal project and repository name, never the game's current public-facing title. Before creating or naming main menus, title screens, logos, splash art, generated images, videos, artwork with text, UI labels, PWA/install metadata, credits, promotional assets or player-facing documentation, read `docs/BRAND_IDENTITY.md` and use the exact public title if a title is needed. Do not render `Azeroth Chronicles` on any new player-facing asset or use a dual-title/"formerly Azeroth Chronicles" presentation. Technical filenames, GitHub URLs, source identifiers, old provenance and historical development documents retain their established names for compatibility; those do not authorize using the retired name on player-facing material. Plain character/environment sprites should not add an unnecessary title or watermark. These requirements apply regardless of which chat calls this the "Azeroth Chronicles" project.

- Read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATE.md` and relevant gameplay decisions before changing a subsystem. Preserve unfinished authored maps and the procedural sprite canon.
- New visible content and lore follow `docs/DESIGN_TO_SPRITE_WORKFLOW.md`: retain the accepted procedural design, approval scope and per-design handoff so a later sprite request in any chat can resume from that canon. Reconcile assets for characters, hero classes, monsters, companions, NPCs, locations and objects; preserve stable IDs and record replacement/animation requirements. The implementing agent owns these records and technical work.
- Sprite edits requested in chat follow the lookup/edit/replacement workflow in `docs/DESIGN_TO_SPRITE_WORKFLOW.md`; retain rollback sources and update affected clips/variants together. - Sprite adaptation to 150% follows `docs/SPRITE_ORIGINAL_MASTER_EXPORT_POLICY.md`: export from the untouched generator output of the accepted revision, audit older padded/resized intermediates, carry placement metadata rather than lossy pixels, and review each result in the game. Progression/training/equipment belong in progression.js, snapshots/validation/migration in save.js, and specialist/expedition menus in menus.js with injected shell services. Domain modules must not import engine.js or introduce browser state.

- Use the owning module instead of expanding `app.js` or `engine.js` indiscriminately. New runtime files belong in the asset inventory and generated entry graph.
- After runtime changes, update the package version and generated assets together when applicable, then use the smallest relevant focused checks, format verification and reproducible build checks. `npm test` and broad browser/game matrices are not automatic requirements: obtain the owner's separate explicit authorization before comprehensive testing. Documentation-only changes do not require a runtime version bump or PWA regeneration. Preserve results already valid for unchanged source.

## A. Single combat contract to teach in-game

The hero begins with **self-learned Skill 1 Rank 1**, which existing rescued specialists can upgrade. The same skill rank powers: **automatic three-beat basic attacks**, an **immediate single-target Tap technique**, and a **deliberate charged Hold technique**.

Automatic attacks are not a ninth button or an independent training tree. Their cadence is independent of activated Skill 1. **New owner-directed asymmetric cooldown behavior:** all Skills **1–8** have separate **normal and charged readiness**. Charged starts **both** timers; Tap starts **only normal** and never restarts an existing charged lock. **Tap returns after its normal cooldown even when the longer charged timer is still active**. Charged requires **both** ready. Each skill slot remains independent, with no universal hero recovery. Full rules: [asymmetric cooldown contract](ASYMMETRIC_TAP_CHARGED_COOLDOWNS_20261024.md).

**Important starting change on this PR's future implementation:** Skill 1 automatic basic **0.85s**; activated normal Tap **3s**; activated Charged **8s Paladin / 7s Mage / 8s Ranger**, all before Cooldown Training and Slow. The hero continues basic attacks while the activated slot is busy or a charge is preparing when combat rules permit. Preserve the current ~0.65s charge hold; quick tap <0.20s; middle release cancellation; input queue choices during cooldown (normal tap and short hold for charged, with genuine charge preparation **after both normal and charged cooldowns complete**), and immediate normal Tap allowed after **normal** cooldown alone. The existing Paladin Shield Stance exception (normal Skill 3 starts recovery when lowered) remains.

## C. Original visual and sonic signature for each new charged action

### Paladin — Radiant Cross (working name)

**Visible progression:** charge raises sword and forms an original **holy angular cross-glyph**, not any borrowed game icon. On legal release a cross of light appears **centered at the cast origin**, rotates one axis toward selected enemy, and extends to its four bounded arms (**190–230 per arm**, **70–102 full width** by rank). Its **first hit pulse is at 0.00s**, **second hit at 0.35s**; mark the second pulse as a distinct re-illumination or inward/outward light sweep. The impact area remains legible above dark/detailed terrain and **never visually passes through an impassable wall while still dealing damage**. A very brief fading glow after the last hit may be decorative only. Unique targets receive **no more than one hit per pulse**; 8 distinct targets across whole cast, not an enlarged radial circle.

**Sound design:** (1) restrained, ascending sacred/metallic preparation that uses existing charge-UI stages; (2) one resonant blade/holy-release accent, (3) **two clearly separated, soft luminous impact accents** synchronized to the two actual hit pulses, not one per enemy body; (4) short quiet tail without looping or crowd noise buildup. A rejected cast makes only a rejection cue; no second success accent if the second pulse is cancelled by legal zone/state change. Avoid Ragnarok-specific imitation.

**Keep apart from** the normal Paladin Skill 1 holy projectile, Shield Bash, Judgment, radial Holy Area/Oath Strike and Sanctuary Oath support wards.

### Mage — Wide Arcane Beam (working name)

**Visible progression:** normal Tap uses the **thin** prior charged Mage Arcane Beam with immediate departure; charged preparation visibly accumulates a much denser core. On release one **straight, broad arcane column** appears from the Mage to the true terrain-limited end; damaging full width is **96–128** by rank, effective reach **440–480**. It **continues through every eligible enemy**, even after the primary enemy, without homing/branching/extra blasts. Show a readable bright center and bounded translucent edges that match actual collision; avoid opaque screen-wide bloom or overlaid art that obscures enemy warnings. One hit **per foe per cast**, no numerical foe count cap. Decorative beam dissolution cannot re-damage enemies.

**Sound design:** distinguish the existing thin beam's crisp shot from a **single fuller low-to-high charging sweep**, one strong spatially coherent arcane-release/energy-wash, and a restrained single contact layer; **do not play one enormous crack per intersected enemy**. Stop all charge audio cleanly on cancel, pause, death, menu/zone transition. Never duplicate Skill 6's slowing Focused Beam language.

**Keep apart from** Mage Frost Burst (Slow), Force Wave (push), Arcane Chainstorm (seeking leaps), Astral Cataclysm (circular splash/vulnerability), and Skill 6 Focused Beam (slowing focused attack).

### Ranger — Hawk Interception (working name)

**Visible progression:** on release, a **brief recognizable original bird silhouette** launches near the Ranger, **tracks the chosen living enemy**, and turns smoothly while obeying **finite flight speed 650 units/s**, **actual cumulative travel 560–620** and **maximum lifetime 1.25s**. The flight must look like a directed hunting bird, **not** another arrow/projectile, permanent tameable pet or summon; it may disappear gracefully on expiration without an impact. On **actual legal contact**, show a small talon/feather impact, **one damage hit** and (only if a real eligible retreat was stopped) a distinct clear **RETREAT INTERRUPTED** cue/brief marker. In English/Spanish the control message must distinguish a successful catch from a missed/expired pursuit. Do **not** show a stun icon or immobilize an enemy; this is a **short same-retreat denial**. Do not chase into scripted returns, boss phase relocation, walls, other zones or safe cities.

**Sound design:** (1) quick bow/hunt call or soft wing launch that does not imitate copyrighted bird recordings; (2) short airy swish matching actual movement; (3) one tactile strike on **contact only**; (4) a brighter tiny per-target tactical chime **only when an active retreat is actually interrupted**. Expiration and blocked terrain give at most a subdued harmless fade, not a hit accent. Do not produce 11 pet/companion sound sources by treating the hawk as a recruit.

**Keep apart from** Ranger Arrow Volley/Rain, Piercing Arrow/Slow, Repelling Shot/push, hunter multi-arrow ultimates and pet companion skills.

# Fort defenses, veteran Ranger audit and passage recovery — dev.109

Joel requested defended-area range and damage reduction, garrison shots visibly passing above their own walls, then enemy-only ranged fire over reasonable obstacles throughout the game, particularly interiors. He also requested an audit of Expedition IV strike protection/covering Slow. His level-17 solo Mage screenshot showed 57 crowns against the Frontier's 180-crown onward fare, both specialists rescued and depleted income sources; he approved working off the fare shortfall.

## Result

- Enemies physically inside defended areas take 40% less incoming damage. Ranged units gain 30% range on top of ordinary targeting reach. These are position-derived benefits, end immediately on leaving, and never accumulate as permanent stats. The established explicit piercing bypass remains valid.
- Coverage: thirteen regional creature holds, five field-boss compounds, three Crown command/final-fortress parcels, Old Signal Keep, Abyss Bastion and Citadel of Ashes. Stronghold centers follow existing stable wall indices; field compounds follow their current migrated mini coordinates. Interior protection follows authored walkable rooms, never void. Unfortified ruins, Treasury rooms and ordinary houses do not acquire a universal fort buff merely by being indoors.
- Garrison basic aiming/acquisition, launch and actual projectile collision share an own-fort wall allowance. Friendly shots and all physical movement keep their original collision. Other forts' walls, tall props, cliffs, water and room boundaries still block fire. Interior fort walls can be fired over, but room void is never traversable by shots.
- All enemy ranged units can fire over authored low fences/barricades, crates/supplies, carts, logs and stumps. Low crypt/archive screens and the Treasury roost shelf have the same exception. Doors, thresholds, mining bulkheads, tall pillars, trees and buildings remain solid outside their own fortified area. Decorative nonblocking furniture already allowed shots; this adds the missing allowance for solid low cover.
- Launch permissions belong to that shot and zone. A shot launched outside a fort cannot acquire its rights by entering; a legally launched arrow can finish after its shooter leaves. Future range/defense immediately reflect the unit's new position. Projectiles remain transient on save/load/travel.
- Native arrows follow an elevated curve with their tips aligned to its slope, a restrained ground shadow and descending contact. A small shield by the enemy health plate marks fort protection. No wall, arm, body, boss warning, skill radius or aim-duration artwork is replaced. Geometry, damage, timing and fixed-direction dodge behavior remain authoritative; the height is presentation, not a second 3D simulation.

## Expedition IV audit

The existing Soldier Power Strike correctly grants a self-only 20% ward for two seconds after a successful strike. It preserves a stronger ward and shares the existing mitigation layer. The Ranger's ready Triple Shot is an eight-second skill: when fired within 150 units, only its first arrow applies one second of Slow on real contact. Misses do not Slow. Rank, skill/global recovery, Silence, sight and charge restrictions apply.

The newer cautious-regroup behavior could return before the Ranger's older close fallback branch. Regrouping now attempts the same ready covering shot against a close pursuing enemy before moving away; withdrawal continues that frame. Basic covering fire is retained during recovery. Recall still cancels combat and returns defensively; gathering priorities remain. Existing living/restored Rangers use current rules without resting, replacing or rehiring them. The training menu now states which troop receives which benefit and the exact conditions.

## Work for passage

At a paid onward transport, after both of that region's captive specialists are rescued, an unaffordable fare offers **Work for passage**. It spends the player's current crowns and abstracts helping the crew to settle the exact shortfall; it completes the normal travel transaction and grants the usual return ticket. For the reported Frontier case: pay 57, work off 123, arrive in Dark Crown.

No spendable gold, EXP, debt, resource node, companion, daily counter, interest or new persistent unlock is introduced. There is no real-time chore or clock skip. Rescue progress makes it available in existing v4 saves; there is no one-use limit that could recreate the trap after a later return/death. Affordable fares, free returns, recovery waivers, developer travel and other prices remain. Destination failure restores coins, progress and tickets atomically. Newly completed unrelated quest rewards still pay normally; this feature does not suppress legitimate income.

## 5 November 2026 — PixiJS fortified health-plate parity (separate draft follow-up)

Owner's Greenwood Vale screenshot reports no shield beside Skeleton Watch defenders and green hostile health bars in the PixiJS/WebGL mode. Current native fort ownership already includes `skeleton-watch` at the authored Vale compound (position-derived, never a permanent enemy flag). Existing combat rules still grant 40% incoming damage reduction, 30% ranged basic/detection reach and projectile speed only to live hostile actors physically inside a recognized fort. Outlying skeletons remain unfortified. The authored perimeter requires actual blocking stronghold walls; an apparently fenced but missing/destroyed layout is not grounds for inventing an always-on buff.

Source diagnosis: the Pixi overlay passed raw `zone.enemies` objects to shared `renderer.js::healthPlate`; the latter checked `e.renderKind === 'enemy'`, which is populated for Canvas presented entities but not for those raw Pixi-overlay actors. This suppressed the shield and incorrectly painted hostile HP bars ally-green. The draft correction passes explicit enemy/ally/hero presentation kinds and leaves `Campaign.enemyFort`, damage, AI, navigation, collision, saves and world geometry intact. Canvas uses its unchanged default presentation kind.

**Status link:** [PR #805](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/805) is the initial draft investigation; the reconciled integration PR records the final checks and merge/publication outcomes. Focused source and native-painter checks do not establish physical iPhone acceptance. No new fort or stat increase is authorized or implemented.

## 5 November 2026 — 160-unit global outdoor pursuit margin (PR #817; supersedes its 40-unit draft)

The owner selected **160 world units**, replacing an earlier, unmerged 40-unit proposal. This is deliberately **global**, not a Greenwood-only exception. All thirteen authored creature strongholds in the five outdoor regions (Greenwood Vale, Mire March, Ironroot Highlands, Ashen Frontier, Dark Crown), all five authored field-boss sites **when they actually have defensive walls** (four are walled in the deterministic fresh-world fixture; the fifth is not eligible without real walls), and all three authored Dark Crown outdoor defense parcels use the same `rules.balance.fortifications.outdoorLeeway = 160`. Rectangular forts expand by 160 world units on each edge. Ruin and habitat rings extend each wall-relative polygon vertex radially by 160 units; oblique edges have a slightly smaller perpendicular offset. Stronghold centers continue to follow live, potentially relocated wall indices.

This builds on **[PR #811](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/811)**, already merged and published: the enemy-red health plates and fortress shields in PixiJS/WebGL2 and the Canvas presentation use the same position-dependent `enemyFort()` across *all* regions. PR #811 remains merged; no separate regional Pixi fix or modification to that historical PR is needed. PR #817 supplies the region-wide 160-unit gameplay boundary and extends the shared painter contract to check every outdoor region.

The existing **320 world-unit unfortified ranged basic** and **416-unit fortified basic**, 40% incoming damage reduction, 30% projectile speed increase and own-fort wall firing permissions are untouched. Protection persists only while an enemy physically occupies the expanded outdoor area, and ends immediately beyond it. This does not extend fort interiors, modify actual walls, pathfinding or collision, create a timed status, alter any of the four playable classes, add CPU-expensive per-frame geometry, or change v4 saves. It grants tactical movement room around established defenses rather than permanent immunity for pursuing enemies.

Focused regression coverage must check all regional/outdoor catalogs, a real Skeleton Watch defender inside/outside the new margin, projectile permissions, save restoration, and unchanged dungeon/Treasury room boundaries. Pixi and Canvas shield/color parity are tested region by region. **At the time of authoring this candidate**, PR #817 is proposed for review, not merged, deployed, or physically verified on the owner's iPhone. Exact validation and future publication evidence belong to PR #817 and the live development-state ledger.

## 5 November 2026 — Actual-wall perimeter mismatch found during owner's Skeleton Watch playtest (separate unmerged correction)

The owner observed skeletons beside the reconstructed visible palisade/stone fence without a shield indicator. Current main already has **160 units** of global outdoor protected leeway; draft #819's proposed further 40 units is based on older source and must not simply be stacked. A code audit found the Greenwood reconstructed `greenwoodWall.a/b` segments do **not** correspond to the generic ten-point ring indices used in `fortAreas()`. Other regional strongholds and interior room footprints stay on their current rules. Focused checks are added for direct wall-bound bounds, restoration, protected ranged fire and loss of protection outside the boundary. **Code committed; exact-head tests, merge, publication and owner's actual-device acceptance remain unverified.** The older #819 addition requires an explicit integration decision based on the new actual-wall measurements.

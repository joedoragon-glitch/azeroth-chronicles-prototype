## Current measurement interpretation: improved tooling with remaining limits

The original profiler measures `game.tick()`, total party/enemy-stage timing, audio update, event dispatch, periodic HUD and synchronous rendering. **Dev.169 adds evidence that the old audit could not assess**: per-surface Pixi/Canvas renderer timing (with surface-specific sample counts), current graphics-backend identification, optional crowd diagnostics, emergency state/counters, and Auto FPS adaptive-skip coupling tests.

Do not over-correct by claiming all old measurement problems are fixed, or by declaring that the new tooling is the same as dev.166. Known distinctions remain:

- `runtime.report().fps` is legacy drawn-callback cadence; `frameProfile.callbackFps` is active callback rate, **not guaranteed completed game ticks during an emergency hold**; `frameProfile.displayedFps` is issued world draws, **not physical-screen presentation**.
- `frameProfile.adaptiveSkippedDraws` is a rolling active-window count, whereas `frameSkip.skippedDraws` has a longer session lifetime. Renderer counters and phase samples may include menu/idle draws outside the 180-active-callback window.
- `companionAIMs` includes all of `updateParty()` rather than narrowly isolated AI reasoning. `enemyAIMs` covers broad enemy updates. AI timing is nested within simulation; independently computed 95th-percentile stage values are **not additive**.
- Pixi/Canvas stage measurements are **synchronous JavaScript call timings**, not GPU rendering completion or input-to-photon latency.
- Saved milliseconds from an intentionally suppressed drawing are hypothetical; no direct counterfactual drawing was executed.

## Audit disposition and next evidence

**Established by newer evidence:** successful dev.169 focused integration and release; corrected Auto FPS safety interaction; covered emergency and crash-recovery edge cases; tested collision equivalence; additional, more specific profiler/renderer/crowd observation fields.

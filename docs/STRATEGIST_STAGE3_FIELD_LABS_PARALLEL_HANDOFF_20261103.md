## 3. Nonnegotiable owner design contract

- **Exactly one Lab per outdoor region** only when that region already contains a **player-placed Barracks**, including an unfinished `progress < 4` Barracks; **the Lab is a separate building**, not an automatic Basic→Full Barracks conversion, not a global free singleton, and not a replacement for ordinary Barracks or tribute. Five region-appropriate appearances. A **Strategist can create/manage/upgrade** Labs. Build quote ~50 crowns is an **unapproved placeholder**; all prices, timings and yield rates remain balance candidates.
- Any Lab can centrally **manage all previously established Labs across regions** while the active hero is the Strategist; **one per region**, stacking legitimate production. While the hero is in another region, remote Labs produce at a **lower, positive** rate, not zero. Production happens only on **active, unpaused simulated game time**; **never while the app is closed, paused, or by retroactive offline catch-up**.
- **A real trained worker is mandatory.** Eligible owned *idle* companions include legitimate inactive reserves, qualifying pets and lawfully Charmed creatures with verified ownership/capture provenance; eligibility must not be faked based on appearance/type alone. A one-time affordable **paid job-training qualification persists** across reassignment and lawful Succession. Do not force either starting Soldier or Archer to abandon the initial combat party; do not assume a free reserve. An actor cannot simultaneously build, recover, join an expedition, fight actively, or staff an incompatible job.
- Each trained worker has a player-selected **repeating Water, Wood, Mineral or Fire** assignment. Source eligibility comes from real authored, active world objects/terrain (Mineral = rock/stone/ore, **not dirt**, and never renderer paint or protected houses). Source sampling is **nondepleting while the source remains valid**; the worker must have an **actual physically traversable route**, unlike the Ogre's material *combat* casts which only need nearby clear line of sight. Real transit, ordinary enemy danger, staffing/storage and processing cap output; no instant teleportation, invented nodes, invulnerable laborers or automatically scheduled per-frame pathfinding.
- **Neri separately teaches Advanced Alchemy ranks** that increase **worker capacity, storage and output**. Individual paid Neri skills 4–8 and separate reagent preparation lessons are **not** the same purchase; do not make Labs grant combat skills or auto-complete Neri rescue. Physical facility upgrades add machinery; **the windmill upgrade is available without requiring Neri's paid training**, though lawful Barracks/Lab construction still applies.
- A **small visibly turning windmill** powers a **staffed** conversion machine that spends **surplus authentic gathered material samples** to make **finite Acid Bottles**. It does **not** harvest Wind or create Wind Catalysts, run without input, create free bottles or consume zero worker capacity. Other legitimate samples can be processed into **Water Reagent, Wood Essence, Mineral Catalyst, Fire Elixir** with consistent finite inventory. The correct matching product can later replace a missing natural skill source with **identical potency** after separate relevant education; the Stage 3 economy must not unlock/execute those skills itself. **Acid is separately consumed**; one and the same shared Bottle inventory feeds Stage 1 automatic valid-third-dagger contact and Stage 2 manually learned Acid skill.
- Only the **Strategist manages the Lab network**; already-established staffed Labs may **continue passive lawful production after Succession** even while the heir is a different class. A successor's separate **chance-based automatic Acid Bottle throw** is **Stage 4**, not a reason to implement heir combat mechanics in Stage 3. Retain legitimate assignments, training, stock and provenance when restoration is authorized.
- Preserve the four existing playable heroes, their training and saves, Barracks build/upgrade/recovery, original tribute, source geography and current routes. Do not create a fifth playable option just to expose Labs.

1. **Shared stock conflict, already concrete:** Stage 1 E0 PR #780 introduces optional Ogre-only `s.strategistAcidBottles` in dormant code. Stage 3 must **read it as the prospective canonical Acid stock** after exact-head verification, not add `s.fieldLabAcid` or replace `save.js` from a stale branch. Atomic finite production/consumption receipts must avoid duplicate material spend on repeated ticks or restore.
2. **Inactive worker location vs actual trips:** Current `party.js` `activeLivingParty()` and `availableLabor()` support old field labor with visible active actors. Stage 3 extends eligibility to *owned inactive reserves* and more types, while preserving **real route/travel/danger**; no off-screen teleportation or guaranteed danger immunity. The new chat must design an authentic scheduled regional worker-trip representation, including remote-zone source changes, and prove it against current navigation/source systems before claiming live labor.
3. **Pathfinding cost and stale caches:** #770 authorizes at most 24 route *candidates* per query, **not** a guarantee that 24 separate region-scale A* solves are cheap. Budget node expansions/CPU time, batch route decisions across simulation steps where supported, invalidate only on real source/worker/zone changes, and profile worst case. Never scan all regions or run route searches/render ticks on every frame. Reuse authoritative source truth and reject fabricated Water from empty containers or inactive Fire.
4. **Temporal correctness:** `Campaign.tick` advances `s.time` only during valid unpaused simulation; use event/scheduled production intervals or a bounded aggregate advancement in that clock. No Date.now / background timers for economic catch-up. Verify exit/reenter region, low-FPS dt clamp, save+load, long away periods, early cancellation, death/Succession without duplicate production.
5. **Economic balance / gating:** One Lab per valid region, nonzero remote efficiency, windmill staffed, finite samples and capacity; one-time worker training cost. The numeric fee, bottle conversion recipes, cost, throughput and Neri rank values are **not ratified**. Produce tunable parameters in existing `rules.js` design (or isolated config when `rules.js` is a conflict), verify sensible economy without asserting phone-tested balance.
6. **UI/visual integrity:** Five region-specific Lab appearances plus windmill rotation are required for *eventual* delivered feature. **Do not imply a visual prototype is device accepted**. No misleading GUI controls in old-class runs; future management centralized from any Lab, even if Barracks unfinished, with explicit idle/assigned worker feedback.
7. **The v3.2 skill roster is current, unlike historic Stage 2 cards.** No hardcoded new advanced skill contracts in Stage 3. Neri's alchemy *capacity teaching* and reagent *substitution teaching* are economically connected but distinct from Neri's **five individually paid learned combat techniques**.

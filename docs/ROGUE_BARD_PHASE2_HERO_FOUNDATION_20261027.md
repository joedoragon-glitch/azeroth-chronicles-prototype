## Source integration

- Rules exports `rogueHeroFoundation` independent of the public `balance.classes` registry: initial HP 90 / dormant MP 60 / Power 18 / Armor 3 / Speed 300; talent coefficients Power +7, MP +2, HP +27, Speed +38. No normal-stat passive. All are candidate numerical details from earlier design review, not newly owner-finalized balance.
- The rogue-only automatic basic is 260 reach, every 0.85 simulated seconds (before talent CDR), one primary contact at 0.8 × (Power + 12) × existing rank/combo scaling, at most two 25%-strength nearby splash contacts, or four secondaries on third-hit finisher (at most five total). This is bounded, renderer-independent damage; no projectile actor or Charm roll. **Only a real primary hit may later attempt Charm.** Geometry and multipliers stay balance candidates until testing.
- **Owner Phase 2 correction:** The level-one Rogue gets her **three native Skill 1 forms immediately**: automatic bell-jingle short fan with three-step combo/third-hit finisher, regular tap (currently called Staccato Sting), and charged (currently called Fortissimo Crescendo). Both manual forms are combat-only bounded, spatially legal sonic bursts against up to 2 / 4 enemies; their same-footprint active living allies receive the positive Sharp Refrain **ordinary basic damage** bonus (+5% for 4s / +7% for 5s) and do not get extra special skill damage. Bonuses from this family never multiply or persist after save/reload. The Rogue's automatic basic uses its independent interval, tap has its own cooldown, and charged locks both release forms. Skills 2–8 still require their later implementation phase. Actual bell audio and art will use existing static/audio systems in Phase 7.
- **Name review:** The owner has reopened all sixteen prototype Skill names, wanting lore-specific, grounded names which convey the staff's suspended jingle-bell personality and both the enemy and ally outcomes, not grand-ballet or conventional orchestral names. **Do not freeze the currently implemented label strings**, but preserve working gameplay mechanics. No alternate names have been owner-approved yet. Critical chance/intensity integration is still later-phase work.
- Original three playable classes and their skill schedules remain untouched; Rogue continues to be test-only/dormant until valid Charm and tutorial are implemented.
- Endure changes a first legal lethal hit per life to 1 HP and protects the hero from subsequent HP hits for **2 seconds**. There is no auto-death on expiry, no speculative 90-second retrigger rule; after the window the next hit may kill. Per-life use persists across saves; transient active protection does not. Standard death/refuge begins a new life. These unapproved recovery details remain adjustable in later owner balance review.
- No new playable class entry or ordinary-game Succession count change is made in this phase. Phase 3/4 admission requires authentic Charm, the class-specific introductory Charm tutorial before Barracks tutorial, and Charmed-creature ordinary labor rights. The Rogue never builds Barracks herself.

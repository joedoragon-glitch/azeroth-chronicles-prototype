# Ash Sentinel — Marshal Veyra: military drillmaster and cosmetologist

Issue #244 · Revised by owner on 11 October 2026 · Draft only, no deployment.

**Identity.** Veyra is a woman from a humid coastal garrison town: a former road warden, a genuine practicing cosmetologist and the previous hero's companion responsible for turning assorted followers into a professional fighting force. She is neither a refugee rescuer, burned survivor nor secretly idealistic protector. After the hero became Dark Lord she imposed shared ranks, drills, equipment standards, discipline, officers and supply expectations on goblin skirmishers, ogre heavy fighters, human militias and other irregulars. She is proud to have created the Dark Lord's standing Crown army. Uniforms must be immaculate; architecture, complexion care, grooming and military efficiency all deserve rigorous aesthetic attention. She and the Stone Colossus are close friends who genuinely enjoy discussing proportion, materials and fine craftsmanship. She is loyal to the Dark Lord and afraid of politics, believing he can govern better than she could. Should he fall, she nevertheless has enough officer loyalty, command structure and military infrastructure to impose a military dictatorship that makes the realm a giant garrison. This is not an idealistic replacement government.

**Citadel.** The Citadel of Ashes is her regimental academy and the army's operational headquarters. Preserve its existing command, muster, barracks, ward rituals, forge, logistics, inner court, living soldiers, Tovan rescue and separate Dark Fortress throne. Multiracial Crown uniforms and actual goblin/ogre Crown soldier variants are **future visual/roster work**, not a change to the existing spawn species or combat system in this ticket. The Keeper's knowledge, Lifebinder's undead, Colossus's separate potential power and Dragon's flight forces remain distinct.

**Personal quarters.** A new eighth decorative station in the existing Citadel inner court is Veyra's vanity. The procedural drawing shows an upright mirror, cream/tint/perfume bottles, cosmetic brushes, a vivid textile and a pressed officer's garment. It is accessible as part of the existing room geometry and creates no walls, obstacles, traps, guards, rewards or interactions. The first-entry message mentions the quarters, and the existing Archive mentions her profession. Tropical color accents are an aesthetic detail, not an ethnic caricature.

**Current combat contract.** The five mechanics are intentional replacements, not cosmetic renames. Microdermabrasion is a nine-pellet canister fan with its combined raw damage bounded to one boss hit and its travel inside the visible warning. Dress Ranks now assigns nearby Crown soldiers actual marching orders to defensive posts; it never teleports them, and provides no defensive order without participating troops. Deep-Tissue Contouring shows a complete fixed five-leg route before one continuous multi-hit charge. Fractional Resurfacing produces a persistent four-by-four board that alternates warned and striking squares while Veyra performs other skills; impact squares remain visibly hot during their 0.45-second recovery. Volumize replenishes her soldiers under the existing caps and TRUE entourage rules. Normal, TRUE, save and Awakening identities remain unchanged.

**Compatibility and review.** Boss family remains `citadel`, title remains Ash Sentinel, 32 zero-reward guardians, 28 traps and fortress route topology remain. Authorial text and the new decorative station registration are in `src/prototype/narration.js` to keep performance-protected `data.js` and `rules.js` unchanged. The new procedurally drawn quarters are in `src/prototype/visuals.js`. The older full-render exact-pixel equivalence check will need its expected visual-change scope reconciled at integration; never silently relax the comparison or claim unchanged Citadel images. See `tests/ash-sentinel-identity.test.cjs` for focused invariants. No merge or deployment.

## Combat redesign · owner-directed, draft

Microdermabrasion fires a nine-fragment canister fan. Dress Ranks orders nearby Crown guards into a protective formation rather than placing ash lanes. Deep-Tissue Contouring shows one fixed five-leg clockwise path before Veyra charges along it, damaging on each leg. Fractional Resurfacing establishes an independent four-by-four chessboard: two squares warn for 1.45 seconds, strike once, rest 0.45 seconds, then the next pair warns. Its eight-pair cycle continues during other skills until the fight ends. Volumize is the shorter cosmetology-derived name for her existing soldier call.

This is an intentional mechanics change, not a text-only polish. Keep the draft isolated pending path, save, balance, device and differential rendering verification. Do not claim it matches the protected v0.9 simulation or pixel baseline.

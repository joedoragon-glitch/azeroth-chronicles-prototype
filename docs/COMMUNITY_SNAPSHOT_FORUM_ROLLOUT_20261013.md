## Presentation and promotional scope

The audience is the original forum community, not a broad acquisition campaign. Publish a personal, grateful progress update rather than a sales pitch. One concise thread is enough initially, with:

- the exact new title and informal **Community Snapshot — October 2026** label;
- a short explanation of what actually changed in the **verified** snapshot, grounded in the released build and free of unimplemented promises;
- one or two screenshots **captured from the actual snapshot**, not AI key art presented as gameplay (optional canonical concept art must be labeled as such);
- the verified public Pages desktop/phone link, simple controls and local-save/export warning;
- a small feedback request: device/browser, what felt good, where they got stuck and a reproducible bug/report where relevant;
- thanks to the forum where the idea began, with no claim that forum members have an exclusive build.

A trailer, countdown, mailing-list funnel, paid placement, formal beta registration and release-date graphics are **not required**. Revisit broad beta outreach only after its scope and timing are newly approved.

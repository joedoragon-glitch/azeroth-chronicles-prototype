**#12 final adjudication, in full:** The owner initially selected **Immobilization**, explicitly retained it when told the current combat action actually slows rather than roots, then requested three **single-word** remediation alternatives. After rejecting multiword border-control-sounding ideas (e.g. “Migration Control”), the owner chose **Fixation** and explicitly said **“Lock it.”** This is an actual replacement authorial decision, not merely a suggestion. **Fixation is the approved English label for the existing Goblin ranged Ringleader signature formerly `Slingmaster Split Screen`.** The move in `src/prototype/rules.js` has `effect: 'bind'`, `warning: 1.2`, `radius: 140`, `slowSeconds: 2.7`, `coefficient: 0.09`; `src/prototype/engine.js` resolves `bind` to a timed **Slow**, **not zero mobility**. The title “Fixation” comes from authentic contaminant fixation/stabilization processes: an apt Scornfang reclamation/remediation joke without promising a literal root. Retire the previous *semantic naming hold* on “Immobilization”; the **actual gameplay source** and any player-facing description still must truthfully explain Slow. ### Why the humor works — nonnegotiable owner design standard

1. **Actual master/actor first, species last.** Learn each specific enemy's established boss, captain, duties, office, occupation, geographical post and independence, *then* choose terminology. A monster can have more expertise than its species' anatomy, shape or elemental affinity.
2. **The core joke:** an authentic—sometimes obscure—**professional term** (or credible adaptation) from that actor's working life that can also be read aloud as a formidable, poetic or surprising **combat technique** by an uninformed player. Professional authenticity alone isn't enough; attack-like rhythm and the real effect matter. Avoid meaningless jargon, generic administrative labels, “Boss Name + Attack,” or repetitive species clichés. Some options may be everyday technical terms and still work.
3. **Multidisciplinary commanders:** Lifebinder isn't merely an anatomist; his combination of **mortuary science, thanatology, architectural conservation and adaptive reuse** is central. Veyra isn't merely a marshal; she also **practices cosmetology** and cares about aesthetic precision and workmanship. Mirejaw's hydrology, Garun's architecture/mining site, Scornfang's remediation, Ridge/Crag's finance and Ashen Warlord's occupation logistics have equally distinctive sources.
4. **Naming must be honest to the effect:** `bind` here is Slow, not full immobilization; `sweep` pushes, no independent explosions; older “volley” labels were misleading if there are no actual projectiles. A naming approval does **not** authorize new mechanics, a new faction, damage stats or arena geometry.
5. **Genuine real terms aren't blanket canon:** do not invent that a given creature studied a school, was trained by Mira, works for a given minister, or uses a historical profession merely because a name sounds interesting.

## Canon to protect — case-by-case allegiance and expertise

- **Lifebinder, Forest Crypt, Greenwood:** former Dark Lord adventuring companion, expert in **Mortuary Science, Thanatology, Architectural Conservation and Adaptive Reuse**. Binds fallen into **aware, enduring skeletons**, not resurrected living bodies. He **brought** his followers to Greenwood to convert an existing crypt into a partly completed winter residence; **they were not raised from that crypt's graves**. They are paid/conscious workers with their own concerns, relationships and limited freedom to decline; captive smith Borin works there. Naming may borrow from conservation, building supports, surveying, demolition, joints, bind maintenance, plus selected mortuary procedures. Canonical boss names **Excarnation, Stay Alive, Clerk of Works, Differential Settlement, Algor Mortis, Anastylosis, Disarticulation, Pressure Grouting, Reconstitution** already demonstrate the intended joke; protect them and Little Lifebinder's existing pet skills.
- **Thornfang and Mira:** Thornfang was a Crown wilderness scout and specialist in canine handling, patrols and tracking; Mira is his constrained paid instructor and rescues hero **Skill 2 / advanced companion skills**. **Mira trained Lifebinder skeletons is *not* canon**. Scornfang, his Goblin disciple, is Greenwood's **Superintendent of Reclamation**, responsible for decommissioning, abatement, salvage, inventory, receiving and estate works; his actual employed goblins may draw on site-remediation terminology.
- **Mirejaw / Direjaw / Drowned Keeper:** Mirejaw is a hydrology specialist; his followers can use river/channel-flow terminology. Direjaw is a distinct wetlands/Treasury captain, with silt, groundwater, land engineering themes. Dreadmaw and the Wraiths may like one another without a command relationship.
- **Ridge Tyrant / Crag Tyrant / Garun:** Ridge is **Master of Coin**, directing economic accounts, payroll and mineral trade; paid **archers** escort shipments across regions. Crag is his Treasury captain/disciple and guards cash/caches using paid Crown soldiers and armored direwolves. Garun, a former Ogre Bastion and master architect/engineer, designed roads, bridges, settlements, fortresses and even his fitted stone body. **His Ogre wardens defend Colossus Mine**, which he regards as workshop/refuge. Ridge's staff handle accounts, wages and ordinary production; that does **not** make every Ogre subordinate a financial officer or Crag's soldier. - **Ashen Warlord and Cinder Warlord:** Frontier occupied province under an actual **military governor**; Orc warbands operate roads, repair details, checkpoints, levies, guarded convoys and restricted crossing lanes. Cinder Warlord is the **roaming inspector/captain** enforcing those orders, *not* the governor. Naming should suggest logistics, inspections, traffic/route control and chain-of-command procedure as well as tactics—**not generic Orc bruisers**. Independent Ash Stalkers at their night hold are not automatically enlisted.
- **Veyrith:** loyal dragon promoting an **unfinished** air-force project at Abyss Bastion; don't imply completed operational air forces or manufactured histories.
- **Veyra:** marshal and former Crown road defender, trainer of Goblins, Ogres, Humans and others in combined drill, uniform care and equipment discipline; **also currently practices cosmetology** and prizes workmanship and proportion. Crown trained-soldier names may suggest cutting, sectioning, hair styling, refined aesthetics and disciplined positioning; do **not** assert all troops are literally certified cosmetologists or her personal attendants.

Dreadmaw, his disciple, directs agriculture/crops/livestock distribution and co-manages guarded Treasury/Inner Vault; Dark Lord has distinct royal troops. ## Owner's 27-answer implementation direction — 16 November 2026, later decision authority

The owner submitted 27 consolidated responses in the subsequent project chat **after** locking all 29 English names. This section governs how the established pilot and naming work should be implemented. It **does not reopen the 29-name map** or erase the earlier evidence. These are design directions, **not claims that code or real-world tests exist**.

### Canon, personality and presentation
- Lifebinder's aware Skeletons combine **skilled estate/worksite employees and competent defenders**, not faceless standard soldiers. Their techniques arise first from real architectural conservation/building craft **and also from internal estate defense drills**.
- **New authorial addition (not a previously established source fact):** Thornfang ensured captive instructor **Mira also trained the Skeleton defenders**. This supplements, rather than replaces, the Lifebinder's authority and the Skeletons' autonomy; it does not transfer Mira away from her established Thornfang/Millhaven story, retcon rescue/Skill 2, or establish that she commands the estate. Record this as new canon rather than citing older source as evidence.
- The same approved Skeleton technique names and gameplay presentation apply in Greenwood Vale and Forest Crypt. Boss/employee identity must be grounded in actual verified guard post/location, never inferred globally from species. Independent wildlife should evoke **habitat and environment**, not fabricated boss employment.
- Prefer professional characterization embedded *economically* in existing story situations. No new explanatory wall of text or first-use tutorial just to explain a technical joke.

### New technique quality, danger and charge model
- **No generic slash, ordinary arrow reskin, additional Slow or shove** as the principal benefit of any of the four new Skeleton abilities. Owner explicitly rejected further low-effort slow/push variations. Engineering has discretion to devise distinctive, implementable non-control mechanics, with clear real contact and player escape/counterplay.
- **Mortise Cut**: must express something distinct from an ordinary sword swing through its geometry, preparation, risk/reward, opening, reactive behavior or tactical use. Avoid a mere cut + damage/Slow/shove.
- **Datum Line**: must visibly/mechanically express a **survey datum / reference line**, not an ordinary arrow with another name; genuinely physical projectiles; should create meaningful positional pressure without Slow or knockback.
- Skeleton Guardians use these skills **sparingly when defending their posts** yet must be dangerous enough to matter. Investigate observed weakness before any separate stat buff; do not simply spam specials.
- **Double Shear normal**: two close angled cuts connected by a deliberate small pivot, but not a generic two-hit reskin. **Charged**: changes the attack angle in a way that punishes habitual lateral dodging, while retaining lawful counterplay.
- **Resection normal**: two closely directed, actual arrows, but distinctly designed; **charged** is permitted/encouraged to change its *function*, rather than only boosting damage, arrow count or radius. The same applies to **both new Ringleader charged forms**: materially different tactics, not a bigger copy, **no new Slow/shove**. This is not an order to redesign every existing boss or native Ringleader attack in other regions.
- Owner prefers **an existing-style casting bar** to another carpet of large ground telegraphs for the new charged techniques. Reuse existing visual and audio standards, with honest hit geometry and real dodge/cover counterplay. Do not suppress safety-critical warning altogether if a bar alone makes a new delayed area hit unreadable; resolve exact visual contract through focused testing.
- **Normal**: techniques rare enough to be memorable but genuinely dangerous; do not patronize or assume players lack genre knowledge. **Nightmare and advanced contexts**: veteran players deserve merciless, intelligent threats, including Greenwood captains/bosses, without illegal/unavoidable hits, accidental new level-1 progression gates, or rewriting existing unrelated boss kits. Cast coordination should not impose artificial courtesy delays on unrelated enemies; keep clear original admission/cooldown rules.
- **Charmed parity is a newly strengthened requirement**: Charmed Skeletons should not forget applicable ordinary and elite techniques simply by changing sides. Preserve the same names as hostile counterparts where the actual action matches, and retain the actor's verified professional identity. Design lawful reuse within existing Charm native/earned elite/heritage/mastery slots and existing rewards; **do not automatically add a purchased fourth slot, eliminate the Skill-2 gate, invent training fees, or change v4 identity**. If complete parity is technically incompatible with those rules, document the exact conflict and safe staged alternative rather than silently dropping this direction.

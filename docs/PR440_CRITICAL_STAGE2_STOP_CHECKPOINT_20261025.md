## Permanent design decisions

Party (hero, Soldier, Archer, eleven pets) and World (enemies, specialists, bosses) use **separate independent base 10% critical-chance domains**. Hero/Companion/World each have independently adjustable **critical-effect intensity Levels 1, 2, 3**, default Level 1. Chance and intensity are different. Sample **once per committed/released action**, not per projectile, area target, delayed pulse, five-second heal tick, frame, queue request or canceled charge. A critical release may still miss or meet normal immunity. Only enhance a real existing authored move consequence; no universal doubled damage, new arrows/targets/summons, broader geometry, reduced cooldowns, AI/telegraph bypass or per-tick critical observers. Tier figures are candidate balance, not owner-certified final values.

Owner decision, 12 October 2026: Frontier troops already visibly communicate Crown affiliation; leave their existing uniforms, names, species and ranks intact. Burned Hamlet should become a normal and comfortable village gradually, **without erasing its remaining ruins**.

This narrowly scoped draft uses the existing procedural `garden` and `laundry` visuals to create a small household corner near the existing Burned Hamlet recovery district. It does not add buildings, NPCs, economic production, gameplay systems, new military activity, or extra sites. Ash patches, damaged foundations, broken carts and active repairs remain.

Scenery is in the existing `regionalHandoffScenes.frontier` presentation-only hook (first-time version 1). On an older save that has no Frontier regional handoff, the two furnishings are placed deterministically and guarded by the existing clear-ground, NPC, service and road checks. No save schema revision is needed.

Do not reinterpret the dragon: Veyrith remains the Dark Lord's personal purple mount and the unfinished air-force project is separate from civilian dragon transport. Night Stalkers, local Orc and Raider territorial homes, their existing Crown affiliation, the Ashen Warlord and all battle/progression rules stay as on main.

**Release intent:** isolated draft only. Integrate once, as part of the owner-approved multi-region batch, with exact-head technical validation, mobile/desktop visual placement and save regression checks.

## Checked encounter behavior

The focused harness preserves authored walls, solid props and the initial resident enemies. It checks 27 configurations: eleven boss families in normal/TRUE forms and five authored captain profiles. Outdoor TRUE positions use the actual field spawn selector; Thornfang and Cindermaw retain their distinct patrol homes. Changing a spawn in future must also account for those homes, patrols and leash/reset logic.

- Every sampled boss position is body-clear. Crag Tyrant is the single blocked captain start.
- 108 boss skill/form groups produced 779 visible-target preparation probes. Ordinary one-point damage canceled none of the prepared warnings, and there were no preparation exceptions. These are preparation/interruption probes, not proof that every whole attack pattern has ideal playable room.
- Twenty-six body-clear-start pilots run twenty simulated seconds with an invulnerable stationary hero, existing residents and ordinary AI; every one starts and resolves attacks, without exceptions or ending in a return state. A separate six-second visible-target pilot covers Crag's obstructed start. Pilots are not damage/kill-time balance tests and do not promise every signature was selected.
- The sampled normal/TRUE boss summon groups and Dreadmaw's captain summon group deploy without terrain-blocked members, actor overlaps or zero clear short exit directions at their initial placement. This is one authored-state sample, not an all-seed, all-crowding or later-wave guarantee.
- Fourteen interior arrival points and all six troop positions per entrance are individually terrain-clear. The separate pairwise/hostile-visibility probe exposes the staging and immediate-acquisition findings above; the four revised Treasury arrivals have neither overlap nor initial visible acquisition in this fixture.

Source review confirms the general `safe()` helper validates its ordinary navigation clearance, rather than each requesting actor's actual radius or an arena's future skill footprint. A valid center therefore cannot certify a suitable boss/captain arena. Summon placement also has a final fallback that does not revalidate every normal separation condition; sampled deployments did not reach a broken result, so fallback safety is a follow-up risk rather than a reproduced failure.

Leash checks, refuge protection, explicit interrupts, Silence, death and encounter cleanup can cancel or pause actions for their existing reasons. Their presence is not evidence that ordinary attacks generically interrupt bosses or captains. No cadence, AI, summon cap, damage, immunity, fort protection or attack geometry is altered here.

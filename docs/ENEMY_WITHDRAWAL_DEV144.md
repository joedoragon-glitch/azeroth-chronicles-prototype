# Enemy withdrawal contributions — dev.144

Owner-directed incremental extension of the established Rogue withdrawal. Support healing has first refusal at each free decision. Existing support amounts, recipients, pulses, range, Silence and cooldown remain; a committed warning, aim or motion finishes before another action. Authored boss recovery is checked before a new Rogue decision.

Travel/escape can start existing ranged basics or compatible captain/boss/night skills while continuing the original route. Basic fire shares the unchanged native cadence, aiming, projectile and collision code. Ordinary ranged backpedaling also starts ready shots instead of indefinitely choosing movement alone. Melee basics cover only real contact; no offensive approach is added.

Selection filters existing plans, retaining native weights, eligibility, repeats, summons/caps, warning times, charge sequences and recovery. Charges, pounces, exposed openings, grabs/death rolls, forced sidesteps and Cindermaw's later compulsory finisher are excluded. Cindermaw can use eligible protection, health equalisation or reinforcements. Wraith Drain is available at its original reach; Stalker Pounce is excluded. An incompatible later boss combo rejects the entire attempt without spending readiness. Actor-centered warning geometry follows the actor; target-centered ground marks remain fixed. A committed covering action keeps its clock through arrival, changing targets or interruption of its support route. Native reset/death/leash/escape cleanup still wins.

No new skills, balance tables, health thresholds, damage, cooldown durations, persistent retreat mode, spawn/home changes or save schema. This does not guarantee survival or certify owner-device comfort/FPS.

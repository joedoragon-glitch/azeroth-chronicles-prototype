# Ordinary ranged-enemy healing buff — dev.181

Target: existing dev.180 main `2af8e97990f6d45b446149592334b9d31b2dfb34`. This update implements the cross-species healing audit: ordinary ranged healers treat ordinary monsters across species and packs, including while either actor is idle; an idle ordinary healer can approach a wounded idle ally. Ordinary-to-ordinary healing is full HP over three seconds on a seven-second cooldown. Boss/captain offensive AI, authored healing roles, normal player healing, enemy damage, and persistent saves are not altered.

| Cast and recipient | Before | Dev.181 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary ranged healer → ordinary enemy (including other species and ringleaders) | 5% of recipient max HP in 5s / 10s caster recovery | **Full HP (all missing HP at cast) in 3s / 7s caster recovery** |
| Ordinary ranged healer → boss/captain/field captain | 5% in 5s / 10s source eligibility | **5% in 5s / separate 10s leader eligibility per source** |
| Boss/captain healer → any eligible target | 5% in 5s / 10s caster recovery | Unchanged |

For an ordinary target at 40% HP, treatment restores the missing 60 percentage points linearly over three seconds, reaching 100% if no further damage occurs. Healing budget is determined at cast start; damage taken during pulses requires later treatment. Encounter scaling does not reduce the full-heal amount. No healing through walls or on dead/neutral/returning targets. Ordinary-to-ordinary support qualifies for any missing HP; the original 75%/90% eligibility thresholds remain for leader recipients and leader casters. Existing 350-unit range, five-second leader pulses, overlap/no-stacking behavior, scaling, Silence, Slow-based recovery, and area selection remain in force.

A runtime-only WeakMap keeps each ordinary healer's leader-target eligibility on the original ten-second cooldown despite a separate seven-second ordinary cast recovery. An ordinary-to-ordinary pulse receives a transient out-of-combat permission; leader-origin and leader-recipient pulses retain their previous combat requirement. An idle ordinary healer may close to within 350 world units of an idle wounded ordinary ally when an existing path is available, without being automatically aggroed. Both cooldowns decrement from the same recovered simulation time; leader casters retain their original interval. They reset on disengage and do not appear in v4 saves. Mixed casts give ordinary units the complete missing-HP budget while leaders retain the original amounts.

Tests: existing enemy-support-healing, enemy-area-healing, enemy-reach-regroup, enemy-withdrawal-actions, archive-mechanics-audit, Slow/save tests; new `tests/enemy-ordinary-heal-balance.test.cjs` covers all 12 species, mixed ordinary/leader area treatment, individual leader cooldown, unchanged leader sources, melee exclusion and save shape; `tests/enemy-cross-species-idle-healing.test.cjs` covers live idle/engaged healing pulses, moving into triage reach, mixed-species eligibility, thresholds, and interruption/safety boundaries. Device/feel balancing remains unverified.

## Engineering decisions on #770

1. Make the **existing dormant library** conservative: decorative region-overlay provenance defeats even a material-sounding artwork name; protect Crown district-owned layouts. Containers, ore carts and ambiguous industrial devices require explicit operating/contents flags. The old genuine woodland/shoreline/lava classification stays. Do not authorize unknown `crown-foundry-furnace`, `crown-glass-kiln`, `heated-washhouse`, `thermal-flue`, `crown-forgehouse`, `crown-civic-*` as material sources.
2. No world, class roster, rendering or save change. `strategist-materials.js` remains **dormant**, not in the browser loading graph. No per-frame work for Paladin, Mage, Ranger or Rogue. Future ability-event calls receive a Strategist-only bounded query; Field Lab calls require separately authenticated staffing plus a real path, not mere visibility.
3. Explicit `invalidate(game, zoneId)` is needed when a previously missing/empty/cold source becomes filled, heated or moved, and on restore/source removal. A cached candidate is independently rechecked against current active state before use; changes to position or previously unindexed source identity require invalidation. No unmanaged full-zone polling.
4. Preserve **32 sight probes**, **64 temporary hazard probes**, **24 candidate worker-route probes** and max 3,800-unit gather search. **24 route callbacks do not cap pathfinding CPU time.** The later Field Lab owner must supply a node/expansion/time budget, short-circuit stalled pathfinders, invalidate route caches on world changes, and measure p95/p99 CPU and allocation behavior on actual mobile hardware. A mock route or synthetic speed number cannot certify real gathering.
5. Optical casting may pass over nonwalkable water/lava when unobstructed, but buildings, partitions and authentic walls block sight. Lab gathering additionally requires authorized physically navigable worker travel. Parent-region outdoor lava/water **cannot leak into** `side-crown-foundry` or another interior.

## Engineering decisions on #770

1. Make the **existing dormant library** conservative: decorative region-overlay provenance defeats even a material-sounding artwork name; protect Crown district-owned layouts. Containers, ore carts and ambiguous industrial devices require explicit operating/contents flags. The old genuine woodland/shoreline/lava classification stays. Do not authorize unknown `crown-foundry-furnace`, `crown-glass-kiln`, `heated-washhouse`, `thermal-flue`, `crown-forgehouse`, `crown-civic-*` as material sources.
2. No world, class roster, rendering or save change. `strategist-materials.js` remains **dormant**, not in the browser loading graph. No per-frame work for Paladin, Mage, Ranger or Rogue. Future ability-event calls receive a Strategist-only bounded query; Field Lab calls require separately authenticated staffing plus a real path, not mere visibility.
3. Explicit `invalidate(game, zoneId)` is needed when a previously missing/empty/cold source becomes filled, heated or moved, and on restore/source removal. A cached candidate is independently rechecked against current active state before use; changes to position or previously unindexed source identity require invalidation. No unmanaged full-zone polling.
4. Preserve **32 sight probes**, **64 temporary hazard probes**, **24 candidate worker-route probes** and max 3,800-unit gather search. **24 route callbacks do not cap pathfinding CPU time.** The later Field Lab owner must supply a node/expansion/time budget, short-circuit stalled pathfinders, invalidate route caches on world changes, and measure p95/p99 CPU and allocation behavior on actual mobile hardware. A mock route or synthetic speed number cannot certify real gathering.
5. Optical casting may pass over nonwalkable water/lava when unobstructed, but buildings, partitions and authentic walls block sight. Lab gathering additionally requires authorized physically navigable worker travel. Parent-region outdoor lava/water **cannot leak into** `side-crown-foundry` or another interior.

## Stable environmental-source registration and change workflow — 4 November 2026

**The matrix above is a historical audited snapshot, not a second live world manifest.** Its contents should not be manually recopied whenever Greenwood, Crown or the other regions change visuals. A real source is located from the **current loaded zone and authoritative code-owned region definitions**, not a remembered coordinate or model name.

### Native owner contract

- Each authentic gameplay-bearing source has one physical anchor in the region's actual `zone.props`/terrain and may carry a stable `materialSourceKey` distinct from its `id`, `structure`, render asset, art style and coordinates. The region owner alone defines `rules.environmentalSources[zoneId][materialSourceKey]` with `material` and `requires` (one of `permanent`, `containsWater`, `containsOre`, `activeHeat`). The rule is **not** part of a user-authored save. Existing familiar structures are still eligible without new keys. An unknown key is rejected even when its sprite looks like a log or well. A decorative multi-part model should give the key only to its single genuine gameplay anchor.
- Repositioning a source changes its current physical position but **not its semantic resource key**. Replacing the art can change the underlying sprite and native prop ID, while the source owner carries the same logical key forward. Removal deletes the anchor; no remembered coordinate resurrects it. A real operating-state change (water/ore empties, heating stops, destructible material disappears) immediately revokes use. If a scene still has only painted scenery and no gameplay heat/water, its key must not be registered as a new source.
- On **source, ownership, terrain or sight blocker changes**, the owning world event invokes `StrategistMaterials.invalidate(game, zoneId)` or increments a local `zone.environmentSourceRevision`. This invalidates the lazy source/occlusion indexes on the next actual Strategist ability or authenticated Lab event, with **no** recurring render/tick scan. Pointer/array identity and length still trigger rebuilds; per-candidate physical-ID/key/grid-cell checks reject detectable stale props, but **not** every unannounced mutation. World owners must send the signal for same-length replacements, newly nearby sources and relocated solid walls.
- The classifier is the **single source-of-truth predicate** for material family; combat still independently enforces visible genuine sources within range, while Labs require native worker authorization, actual walkable *approach* positions and budgeted routes. The physical native `source.id` is intentionally unchanged for current integrations; preserving a stable `key` across replacements does **not** authorize an in-flight worker to skip re-verification of the replaced physical object or invent carried materials. A v4 restore regenerates the transient index from the restored native zone and current rules, never from a second save registry.

**Future focused CI rule (not installed in shared main yet):** after source and Lab owners integrate, changes to `world.js`, regional generation/rules, source declarations or state-event hooks, `navigation.js` (only for Lab routing), and `save.js` trigger focused semantic registry, movement/revision, optical line-of-sight, actual five-region Lab access and v4 restore tests. If regional teams change a genuinely exploitable resource's identity or ownership, they update **their authored declaration** and relevant narrow test, not a second Strategist coordinate spreadsheet. Continuous cross-PR polling is not running; reconciliation is an integration gate, not a background promise.

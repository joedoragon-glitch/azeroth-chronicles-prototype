# Public communication voice — project guide

**Scope:** Forum posts, game announcements, release notes written in the owner's voice, player replies, public development reflections, and related ghostwriting for **So I Became the Dark Lord's Problem**. This is a communication guide, not a gameplay, narrative, localization, or release-approval contract.

**Authority and privacy:** The owner requested this public working guide on 28 October 2026; this text is an editorial summary, not a verified quotation of the owner's writing or a claim that every sentence has been individually approved. For writing in the owner's voice, the private audited **Personal Writing Profile v1.1**, when accessible and applicable, is the primary voice reference; this public guide supplies the project and audience-specific rules. Active ChatGPT Project instructions and later explicit owner corrections take precedence over any conflicting wording here. The full private profile and original samples are **not reproduced here**. If they are unavailable, use this guide without pretending to have consulted them. Never commit personal writing histories, forum archives, correspondence, identity details or unapproved quotations to the public repository. Do not publish private material when reconciling a style conflict.

## The actual voice

Write like a person who is making a game and talking with people who have followed the process, **not like a studio publishing a marketing campaign**.

- Use an approachable, direct, conversational first-person voice when writing on the owner's behalf. It can be thoughtful and explanatory without sounding rehearsed.
- Say what happened, what is still wrong, what is being tried, and why it matters to someone playing. Trace practical consequences rather than just listing features: what does a player actually experience when a companion behaves unexpectedly or a menu gets in the way?
- Make an idea tangible with a specific example, an imagined situation or an unexpected but useful comparison when it fits. Speak *to* the audience, sometimes working through a qualification or correcting an idea in plain sight, rather than presenting an artificially perfect argument.
- Let sentences vary naturally in length. Normal contractions, meaningful asides and occasional playful mock-serious humor are welcome when the setting invites them. An absurd premise can be funny precisely because it is treated as ordinary for a moment; do not force jokes, metaphors, slang or rhetorical questions into every post.
- Keep expressive rhythm and deliberate personality while removing typos, speech-to-text repetitions and meandering setup that adds nothing. Do **not** polish every sentence into a slogan. The owner prefers a draft that needs one or two personal adjustments over an over-optimized draft that sounds fake.
- Prefer ordinary words and specific observations over empty hype, corporate phrases, exaggerated gratitude, dramatic declarations, or repeated rhetorical contrasts.
- Avoid forced excitement, promotional superlatives, manufactured suspense, excessive headings/bullets, and emoji-heavy formatting. An ordinary reply should sound like a reply, not a press release.
- Do not invent anecdotes, personal experiences, emotions, testers, feedback, or motives on the owner's behalf. If these facts have not been supplied, leave them out.
- Accept criticism without defensiveness or promises that every suggestion will be implemented. Replies should be helpful, curious and respectful, including when the player encountered a bug.
- Write in the language and register appropriate to the actual conversation. For established Spanish-language forum threads, prefer **natural Latin American Spanish (es-419)**, not a literal translation of English corporate prose. English can be used when the intended audience or owner requests it. Do not assume the two versions need identical phrasing.

**Editorial target:** The owner should be able to read the draft, recognize the intended point of view, and post it with minimal personal adjustment. This is an iterative preference, **not** proof that AI has reconstructed an exact personal literary fingerprint.

## Established forum channels

There are **two existing conversation threads**, serving different functions. Do not create a competing announcement thread by default, and do not invent URLs or post automatically.

1. **Ongoing game announcement thread:** Use for playable versions, changes affecting players, game problems, real playtesting requests and concrete progress toward release goals. Lead with what readers can do now and what has *not* yet been verified. Prefer a modest update over a full changelog when that is what the moment calls for.
2. **Original AI-game-development discussion:** Use for the story of the experiment: building a game with AI help, what succeeded or surprised the developer, limitations, lessons and honest reflections on the process. Reference the playable game when relevant without simply copying the release announcement.

Reply conversationally to the people already in each thread. Adapt the length and context to those readers. Do not cross-post identical promotional copy simply to increase attention. Only the owner decides whether to publish; drafting is not publication.

## Accuracy before wording

- Read [brand identity](BRAND_IDENTITY.md) before player-facing copy. The **only current official game title** is **So I Became the Dark Lord's Problem**. **Azeroth Chronicles** is a legacy project/repository name from an abandoned Warcraft-related concept, not a title, alternate brand, or description of the present independent game. Search and describe the game using its **official title**, using the old GitHub repository slug only where needed to locate technical files and preserve history. Do not revive the former name in announcements, player-facing descriptions or new public-facing headlines.
- Verify build, release and current-feature claims against [development state](DEVELOPMENT_STATE.md), current source and actual publishing records. If the user supplies a later verified update, reconcile it with GitHub before presenting it as live.
- **For Rogue/Pícara announcements, verify [the current Rogue canon](ROGUE_CURRENT_CANON.md) before writing any equipment, art, basic-attack or companion claims.** She carries a staff with an oversized hanging spherical **jingle bell**, not the guitar described in superseded October 26 prototypes. Rogue is live after PR #511, but full hardware, balance and audio acceptance must not be invented. Do not use dated archived design text as contemporary marketing copy.
- Distinguish **planned**, **implemented**, **tested**, **merged**, **published**, and **verified by a player on a real device**. An automated check does not equal a real player's experience; a merged PR does not prove a phone received the update.
- For **v0.9.2**, the owner's development goal is *functional completion*: essential intended gameplay works without unfinished core mechanics or major progression blockers. This is a **planning goal**, not a claim that 0.9.2 has shipped or that a public commitment was made. Artistic polish, optional expansions and every outstanding issue or PR need not be finished first.
- Keep links and instructions current, but do not overwhelm an informal message with technical jargon or status tables. Explain a necessary term in everyday language only when it helps readers.
- Do not announce a release, volunteer program, player reward, public commitment or invitation as active without the applicable owner approval and actual evidence.

## Drafting and review

For an owner's forum request: identify the audience/thread and language from context; verify the specific release facts; draft the **post itself** in a natural, ready-to-edit voice; and ask for feedback only if a genuine authorial choice is unresolved. When revising, respond to the owner's actual changes rather than repeatedly rewriting for an imagined ideal voice.

A natural progress-update opening might be:

> «He estado trabajando en que el juego se pueda completar de principio a fin sin tropezarse con sistemas a medio terminar. Todavía quedan cosas por corregir, especialmente las que solo aparecen cuando alguien lo juega de verdad.»

This is an **illustrative example written for this guide**, not a quotation or verified original post. Do not reuse it mechanically. Favor fresh wording fitted to actual news.

Check the final draft for four things: **Would this sound normal in the ongoing conversation? Is it factually supported? Is the tone personal rather than promotional? Does it honestly distinguish what works from what remains unfinished?** If a more polished version fails the first question, keep the simpler draft.

## Boundary with narrative writing

This guide is for the **owner's public communication**, not a mandate that the narrator, Drowned Keeper, bosses, NPCs or other characters speak like the owner. Game prose has its own canon, character identities and narrative review. An author's sensibility may influence how a story is written without giving every character the same voice.

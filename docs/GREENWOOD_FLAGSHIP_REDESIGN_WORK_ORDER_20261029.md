## Major additional rejection: wasted outer-map areas — mandatory first-class criterion

The owner explicitly reports that **areas near Greenwood's map edges remain poorly utilized**, despite prior successful solutions in Flooded Marches, Ironroot Highlands **and Frontier**. This is a **major failure** of the commissioned overhaul, not a cosmetic consideration. The 3200-side layout cannot be called successful merely for being bigger. The original Piedmont brief already required more **usable land rather than more edge pixels**; approved P02 requires meaningful extended contrasting farm/wood/estate circuits.

**Required process:** Map/measure the *actual current saved/fresh scene*, including original landscape features, roads, settlements, enemy homes, patrols, economic props, quest sites and usable travel. Divide the 3200-unit region into reproducible **center, four quadrants, edge bands/corners, traversable corridors and shoreline/crossing districts**; capture a high-level whole-region land-use overview and at least four edge-specific camera scenes. Quantify meaningful anchor distribution, traversable vs blocked land, prop/worksite concentration, expected route times and encounter spacing. Compare against the pre-reconstruction layout and proven Marches/Highlands/Frontier edge-use precedent. **No invented percentage target:** propose and document an evidence-based target after measuring actual geography, not a raw demand to fill every square.

**Redesign rule:** Reallocate suitable existing scenery *and where safe, complete compounds/sites* to purposeful underused ground **with their associated props, guard homes, roads, wayfinding and work function**. Give outer districts a reason to exist: viable wooded loops, agricultural reaches, rural businesses, maintained/abandoned occupation, river scenery, remote threats or deliberate quiet landscape. Respect ecological transitions, geography and map bounds. **Do not scatter unconnected props, teleport bosses merely for occupancy, dilute encounters into dull travel, shift established saved-player destinations without versioned migration, or enlarge the map again to hide poor allocation.** Keep wilderness and breathing room where it contributes to the experience.

**Edge pass/fail:** produce before/after region-wide density/land-use diagrams (label actual observed anchors, no fake scene data), fresh and v4-saved campaign routes including purposeful edge districts, recorded travel/encounter distributions and native 150% shots. Any systematically empty/visually generic outer band must have a documented purposeful composition or be improved before owner acceptance.

**Design from first principles.** The new Greenwood may thoroughly reorganize the geographical arrangement, settlement footprints and locations, the road network, distribution of woods and fields, river/pond systems, farm and mill economy, estate approach, landmark sites, camps and fortified compounds, and the use of the outer map. Do not inherit old town rings, exact coordinates, arbitrary road trees, campsite fence fragments or old site spacing as defaults. **Preserve the game's content and player progress, not obsolete placements.** Existing factions, named characters and identities, six quests and original rewards, two useful crossings, Scornfang/Thornfang/Lifebinder roles, occupied Goblin side cellars, campaign progression, defeated/rescued flags, companion and player-owned construction, saved discovery, 32 ordinary enemies and 640 finite crowns remain authoritative. **The present coordinates, exact landmark locations, visual scenery layouts, roads and fort perimeters are not canon.** Major sites may be moved together with their environmental dressing, NPCs, guard posts, patrol/home anchors, service and entry points and functional routes whenever the new plan warrants it. Re-establish a coherent geographical relationship among their functions, rather than simply shuffling isolated props.

**Save compatibility is an engineering challenge, never an artistic veto.** Map migrations must be explicit, versioned, idempotent and non-destructive: handle 2700-source legacy, 3200 already-migrated and genuinely played campaigns, hero/party positions, saved camps/Barracks, orders and progress. ## Environmental design plan: compose the **whole world**, not independent prop passes

1. **Regional concept and master spatial plan:** re-evaluate the integrated 3200 terrain as a mature-green Piedmont-like medieval fantasy river valley with village, hamlet/working orchard, western mill, woods with inhabited territorial sites, Crypt/Scornfang supply route, incomplete winter estate and authentic crossings. Study central/outer land use and compare full map alternatives. Keep 3200 as a measured baseline, **not a blind command to expand**.
2. **Terrain first:** river/catchment and shore shapes, varied low relief, forest margins/clearings, quiet/hostile transition and functional edge destinations. Draw shore/banks using actual collision sources. No fake lakes, impossible dam, straight generic terrain or purposeless excess space.
3. **Road network second:** distinguish **main cart thoroughfares**, farm/service lanes, faint forest pedestrian paths, maintained river crossings and guarded logistics. Use authored route spines and parcel goals, with navigation validated against them; do not let a 50-unit routing grid invent urban design or render every route as an equally wide road. Eliminate accidental parallel arterials, road triangles and dead-end strips. Maintain actual walkable width for hero/companions.
4. **Civic and productive districts:** author Millhaven's welcome/service frontages, open main street, homes, market, workshops, safe spawn, actual ways in/out. Design Orchard Hamlet, gardens, fruit growth stages, loading/cellar, rural transport as complementary household and economic spaces. Group details by their function; reserve necessary parcels before optional props. Establish natural negative space, plausible building orientation/scale and edge tie-ins.
5. **Hydraulic mill compound:** inspect real upstream and downstream source geography; connect the **same actual pond** to a restrained dam/weir, visible wetted flume/headrace, wheel and tailrace/outlet. If a channel/collision change is needed, use one narrow versioned design; no new water simulation, economy, extra pool or shoreline deception. Keep mill loading frontage accessible.
6. **Defended inhabited places:** rebuild the Goblin roadside camp with irregular *intentional* palisade, usable gate, sentries, clear work/domestic yard, escape/fighting circulation. Rework Scornfang's Treasury/bridgehead as distinct secured supply/logistics architecture with recognizable guard approaches, yard, fence logic and real crossing control. Audit other Goblin, Skeleton, Thornfang, Crypt, side-cellar and estate living/defensive sites for similar defects. **Frontier is the structural reference for forts, not a blueprint for Greenwood's culture.**
7. **Visual finishing at real viewing scale:** coherent prop density, hero/house scale, original accepted sprites and protected asset provenance, 150% phone/landscape/desktop frames (Canvas and supported Pixi/WebGL), first ten minutes and contrasting 30–60 minute routes. Work must independently reject sloppy scenes before asking the owner. Do not solve functional problems merely by drawing over the error.

**Important distinction:** Code presently routes roads in `src/prototype/world.js::roadNetwork()` using `route(..., {road:true})`, builds generic settlement patterns and runs later clearance/onboarding relocations. `src/prototype/visuals.js::roadGeometry()` paints generalized 35/29/23-width road layers. `src/prototype/greenwood.js` places the mill near the round terrain pond and a sluice independently; current tests verify vicinity/route clearance but **not a continuous hydraulic design**. Inspect latest files anew and fix at the correct authored source, not by changing only screenshots or weakening tests. Do not duplicate procedural engines or destroy preserved save-identity data.

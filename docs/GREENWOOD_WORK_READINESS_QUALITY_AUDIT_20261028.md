**Severity:** P0 = a potentially blocking defect or false-completion risk; P1 = important implementation/verification gap; P2 = hygiene/ambiguity. "Confirmed" means current source/doc evidence demonstrates the issue. "Risk" means failure is plausible but not shown in actual gameplay. "Unmeasured" is neither success nor failure.

## Practical acceptance / blocker policy

- **P0 BLOCKED:** inaccessible or drawn-through road/water/door; critical authored feature absent; new game start/quest/boss/rescue/payment broken; lost Barracks/companion/resource on a valid v4 old save; old Scornfang name on a relevant visible active surface; unsupported claims of a passing test/device or release.
- **P1 must resolve or explicitly expose before owner playtest:** major scene identity unconvincing, stale docs, drastically worse pacing/performance with evidence, repeated architectural proportions/occlusion, inaccessible normal route variants, unsupported source/art assumption.
- **P2 may be separately scheduled:** small ground clutter spacing, incidental leaf/grass density, noncritical sound/texture variation, minor color balance that does not affect readability.
- **Owner decision trigger:** an actual **new** story, gameplay, reward, named ownership or unapproved asset-production choice. Do **not** ask the owner to pick random coordinates, design collision algorithms, validate basic route clearance or reapprove all 50 artistic choices.
- **Release boundaries:** independent feature PR(s) stay draft; focused tests/build only per existing policy, no comprehensive all-game/browser suite without express approval, no merge to `main` or Pages publish without express approval, no real-device acceptance without user's device report.

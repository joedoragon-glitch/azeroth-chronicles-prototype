## 1. The actual class conception: acceptance authority

**Fantasy and loop:** A nimble but physically present Ogre fights with two rapid, relatively low-impact daggers at **90-world-unit ordinary melee range**, alongside a real starting **Soldier + Archer**. He is an opportunistic battlefield engineer/alchemist, not a wizard: analyze the actual enemy, body positions and shot lanes; use genuine available Water, Mineral, Fire and later Wood; work short physical leverage, force interception, flow and heat conversion; advance or withdraw deliberately; reward the one **real native ordinary dagger hit** and actual use of material. Inspiration is martial/Aikido-style timing and force reading, without directly importing fictional alchemy systems or free transmutation. The early three starting skills are **Water, Mineral, Fire**, already learned at New Game. The later five individually purchased after rescuing **Neri the Alchemist in Sunken Archive** introduce Wood, deeper Water, deeper Mineral, deeper Fire and Acid **in a proposed—not owner-fixed—lesson order**. Existing combat teachers train actual ranks separately from Neri's material knowledge and separately paid equivalent reagent substitution.

**Separately operated signature Reversal is NONNEGOTIABLE:** The player deliberately enters the real future impact footprint of one AUTHENTIC reversible enemy telegraph and manually responds before that specific release. Correctly reversed one-release damage and attached status effects are removed for **all party members the release would actually affect**, not just the Ogre; source-valid reflected harm only when legitimate. No arbitrary lifetime success quota and no invented damaging reflection of a status-only warning. It is **outside the eight learned slots**. Other concurrent warning instances or later beats are not canceled without their own legitimate reversal. The Ogre also has a conservative owner-only passive **Dodge**, plus a separate chance of real **enemy close-melee basic retaliation**; neither is itself the Reversal.

**Automatic dagger and Acid economy:** Three successful Ogre **ordinary** dagger contacts comprise the automatic rhythm. When stocked, **each eligible third beat automatically spends exactly ONE Acid Bottle for its native compact splash**; without stock, the beat is still a real ordinary dagger. The player cannot reserve the stock and skill effects cannot create phantom extra basics/crit rolls or additional free bottles. Learned Skill 8 manual Acid spends from THE SAME pool. Field Labs, worker routes, windmill surplus conversion and across-class Succession behavior are later development stages, not permission to implement a broader economy during this investigation.

**Authentic source contract:** The Ogre may cast material alchemy only from an actual nearby/current-zone material in line of sight; source **walkable-path reachability is NOT necessary for the Ogre** (a visible river across an impassable shore can be an actual Water source). Opaque walls block source sight. However, movement, targeting, melee contact and workpiece placement STILL require their own actual collision/line-of-action legality; visible source does NOT let the Ogre cross walls or hit behind them. Natural sources do not deplete. **Mineral means rock/stone/ore, NOT dirt**; no Wind. No fake world objects, added terrain, ordinary protected home harvesting or cosmetic painted Wetness. Only after the separate appropriate Neri education, one real matching Water Reagent, Mineral Catalyst, Fire Elixir or Wood Essence may replace missing source with **identical effect**, spending stock only on a valid committed action. Field Lab workers, unlike the Ogre's combat source checks, require **real traversable work routes**.

**Mineral choice A was confirmed by the owner:** One placed angled Mineral construct must genuinely intercept native **traveling physical projectiles from EITHER direction**, regardless of enemy/friendly source, including a Soldier/Archer encounter where an improperly placed face blocks the **friendly Archer's real arrow**. It is **NOT a Paladin ward, no reflection/ricochet, no movement obstacle, no fake projectile deletion unrelated to true geometry, no global per-render-frame projectile scan**. Owner requires measured bounded CPU/performance when engineering is authorized. Charged's moving-face implementation remains just a **designer hypothesis**.

**Do not copy other classes:** Paladin owns sustained defense/wards/taunt/rush; Mage owns ranged elemental-like beam, interrupt, vulnerability, chains and broad push; Ranger owns arrows, self/party healing, personal Haste versus companion Rally; Rogue Bard owns music stations, timed party boons, enemy wither/anti-heal, enemy cooldown interference and broad disruptive effects. It is permitted for some forms to be related and others more different; **"charged must always be a totally unrelated verb" is an overcorrection**. What matters is distinct useful choices at real dagger distance and real material/context cost.

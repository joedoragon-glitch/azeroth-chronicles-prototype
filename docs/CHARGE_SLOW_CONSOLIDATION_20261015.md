Imported paths include all eight hero skill slots/charged cooldowns; companion attacks, first/second/global skill timers, healing/mana support and Soldier survival; enemy basic/special/summon/healing timers; Lifebinder repair, Keeper sabotage, captain overtime, Cindermaw protection, Dreadmaw feeding and absolute Rogue/Royal readiness.

### Phase 1 review — what makes this a Strategist

**One-page identity contract.** The player approaches a close melee fight with forethought, not remote spellcasting. The Ogre can win a source-free encounter using dual daggers and an ordinary Soldier + Archer; a material source changes *how* the next few seconds unfold, never whether the character can function at all. Water repairs a compromised fighting line by cleansing a real enemy-imposed condition; Mineral changes the direction in which an authentic traveling shot can safely pass; Fire exploits contact and previously prepared material. Reversal is a fourth, separate player skill expression: choose to run **into** an actual warned strike rather than stand outside it, then counter that **one** release for the whole affected company.

**Difference from the four existing classes.** Against a slowed Archer by a river, the Ogre cleanses Slow but does not provide Ranger healing or the Paladin's damage reduction. At an exposed quarry approach, a correctly angled Mineral plate intercepts one shot and can also spoil the friendly Archer's shot; it is **not** a Paladin ward, immune state or terrain wall. On a boss warning, the Ogre's deliberate inside-footprint Reversal is **not** Mage's remote warning interruption. Unlike Rogue Bard, the Strategist neither Charms enemies nor performs musical support. His close-range vulnerability and dependence on authentic sources are real costs.

**Originality checks.** None of the six opening forms principally applies Slow, knockback, Stun, Silence, Weaken, an ordinary Heal-over-time, global damage buff, immunity or a ranged elemental bolt. A cast must produce a materially different next decision: *which ally needs cleansing*, *which actual shot lane is worth covering*, or *which nearby enemy/artifact can receive a finite thermal follow-up*. Water Trace and Mineral plate are short-lived **Ogre-made tactical artifacts**, not new natural terrain, harvestable materials or permanent world obstacles.

**Settled Mineral originality and performance guardrail (owner option A):** Angular interception deliberately turns **one oriented piece of authentic worked Mineral** into localized, two-sided projectile cover. The correct placement can protect the Ogre, but the wrong placement can block his Archer's shot; this drawback is part of the mechanic, not a defect to patch out. Its intended complexity is **small and predictable**: one short-lived construct per Ogre, one active normal stationary or charged mobile face with a single interception each, no numeric two-plate upgrade, and no always-on world-wide tracking or render-frame collision polling. Engineering will test, rather than assume, that this remains CPU-efficient.

**Scarcity is permitted.** Three simultaneous natural-source requirements are never assumed. Fire and Water synergy is an occasional opportunity, not the baseline rotation. When a particular material does not exist nearby, its skill is simply unavailable and the Ogre remains a viable dagger fighter. Any alpha showcase must describe where the existing world actually provides the material, without changing the world to suit the showcase.

## Phase 1 — identity and distinctiveness

The Ogre is a **battlefield engineer** and environmental opportunist, not an elemental Mage or a generic potion thrower. He reads actual authentic, nearby, **line-of-sight-visible** sources of water, solid mineral and active fire; modifies a small piece of the immediate fight; and operates in close reach where positional mistakes matter. His verbs are **Water = wash / route**, **Mineral = work / brace**, **Fire = convert / finish**, **Reversal = enter danger and redirect the source attack**, **daggers = sustained close pressure**. Water can support companions without becoming a Ranger heal; Mineral offers **physical directional projectile interception**, not a Paladin ward; Fire reacts with an earlier Ogre-made construct or primes a finite dagger contact rather than creating Burning, a huge AoE or enemy-style jet.

Principal exclusions: generic Slow, knockback, Stun, Silence, Weaken/Fracture, persistent burn damage-over-time, generic shield/immunity, huge AoE, free elemental casts, artificial terrain, RTS-style orders or new narrative companions. These are originality safeguards **for the new Strategist design only**, not requests to revise existing gameplay.

Natural source **eligibility is authoritative world data**, never painted graphics, arbitrary grass/dirt, decorative splashes, ash/sunset or a source behind impassable terrain. The Ogre uses real world opportunities and accepts that some fights lack them. Empty-area baseline remains daggers, Soldier, Archer, finite Acid Bottles, Dodge, retaliation and legitimate Reversal.

### Skill 1 — Water: Current Weave (current cure versus anticipated cure)

**Owner-directed asymmetry audit:** The previous tap cured one current hostile condition while charge cured the same condition on many allies. That was an amplified copy of the same action, so the old charged group wash is **superseded**. The owner has approved the **creative distinction rule**, not separately approved this new move detail.

**Normal — Rinsing Cut: cure an EXISTING condition.** With real nearby water in line of sight, immediately remove **one genuinely active, eligible hostile Slow** from the Ogre or one nearby living active companion. Default choose Ogre if affected, otherwise nearest eligible affected ally with stable ID tie. May deliver one modest short contact wash against a legal hostile in <=105 range. A cleanse-only normal cast is valid if a recipient is slowed even with no hostile nearby. If neither legal status nor enemy contact exists, reject rather than consuming a cooldown. Successful **offensive** contact may create the one owned, 3–4-second Water Trace for later Fire or eligible Acid reaction; a cleanse-only cast does not fabricate a trace. The legitimate Slow state is removed but any already incurred cooldown debt stays incurred.

**Charged — Cleansing Circuit: anticipate a FUTURE condition.** With the same genuine visible Water source and a legal chosen living active ally (or Ogre by default), the Ogre places **one small single-recipient flow ring** with a **candidate 4-second lifetime** after the 0.65s hold. It does **NOT cleanse a condition already present** and does not immediately attack or create a Trace. Instead, when **one new genuine removable hostile Slow application** subsequently occurs on that exact chosen recipient before expiration, the Slow undergoes normal native application, and the ring **consumes once to wash away that one actual newly applied Slow**. It is not immunity or protection from the hit/damage; it does not prevent a Slow-causing attack, remove historical cooldown debt, heal, ward, shield, wash multiple allies, suppress a Silence field or preempt repeated hostile field reapplication after the ring expires. If nothing triggers it, the charged action is wasted. Only one Ogre-owned ring may exist at a time; replacement is atomic, expiry/zone/death/reset clear it.

**The choice:** Normal is the quick rescue when an ally is already slowed and offers a close offensive Water Trace opportunity. Charged is an uncertain **predictive contingency on one deliberately chosen ally**, useful *before* a known Slow-inflicting threat, and worse when the party is already compromised. Its charged cooldown and interruptible preparation pay for a different temporal job, not a stronger cleanse.

**Status evidence:** At this Stage 1 design checkpoint, **Slow is the only supported provisional party-hostile timed-condition allowlist**. Additional removable conditions must be verified against actual current native party state before either form may claim them. Forced movement, environmental danger, warning geometry, enemies' Dust Cover, beneficial effects, persistent Silence fields and expired durations are NOT removable Slow receipts. A valid ring is triggered only by a native new-Slow application event, **not by polling the ally each simulation frame**. An active hostile Slow field may reapply after the ring's one-time removal. EN/ES candidate forms: “Cure Slow now / Curar ralentización ahora”; “Wash next Slow / Limpiar la próxima ralentización.”

### Skill 2 — Mineral: Facet Work (owner-confirmed authentic two-sided shot interception; stationary cover versus moving parry)

**Owner-directed asymmetry audit:** The previous normal placed a single shot-stopping plate; charged placed **two larger-capacity shot-stopping plates**. The difference was mainly numerical, not tactical. The owner-confirmed primary identity is **authentic physical interception of directed traveling projectiles from EITHER side, including an obstructed friendly Archer shot, with mandatory low-CPU proof**. The owner's earlier decision **explicitly rules out projectile reflection/ricochets**, so do **not** redirect, relaunch, reverse allegiance or return missiles to the shooter in either form.

**Normal — Stone Facet: plant one STATIONARY lane interceptor.** Work true nearby visible stone/rock/ore, not soil, into a short fixed angled plate (candidate ~55 width, ~4s life) at an independently legal near position. When **one native eligible traveling physical projectile** sweeps across it in either incoming direction, the plate **actually intercepts and terminates that original projectile** once, and breaks. This may stop the Archer's friendly shot as well as an enemy missile. It stays at its placed position even if the Ogre walks away: a tactical decision to defend a specific future shot lane or companion approach. It causes no damage upon placement; no new world collider, navmesh obstacle, spell ward, attack reflection or magical immunity.

**Charged — Running Facet: carry a brief moving shot-parry face, NOT a bigger placed shield.** With genuine nearby visible Mineral and the native ~0.65s charged hold, work a **single small oriented stone paddle close to the Ogre's body/daggers**, tied to his **actual near-field position** during a brief advance (candidate **0.7–1.0s** after release), instead of placing it on the map for later. Its orientation is **fixed by the Ogre's chosen facing at committed release** and does **not** automatically track projectiles or rotate to face threat. The first real eligible **physical traveling projectile** whose native swept motion ACTUALLY intersects this moving narrow face, from either side or either allegiance, is **terminated once**, consuming the face. No valid crossing within the short window means the parry simply expires unused. The charged form gives **zero extra projectile charges**, does **not** reflect or re-emit a missile, and cannot protect a distant Archer or a prechosen static lane once the Ogre moves away. A badly angled run may intercept the Archer's own passing arrow, so the owner-confirmed **team-blind fairness** remains. It does not deflect melee attacks, boss warnings, ground explosions, instant beams, magic fields, a hazard, or another party member's damage. It is **physical shot collision**, never blanket Paladin-style reduction/immunity, generalized Dodge or the separate manual Reversal.

**Meaningful choice:** Use normal to **leave cover in a lane for the company** and give the Ogre freedom to flank elsewhere; use charged to **carry a short one-shot physical shot interception through a melee advance**, sacrificing lasting coverage for mobility and precise timing. Both intercept at most one real shot, and charged preparation/short window can easily be worse. The charged face is attached to the Ogre's **position**, not an auto-aimed projectile seeker. It is a deliberately different positional role, NOT merely two plates, greater radius or longer lifetime.

**Ownership and CPU gate:** **Only one active Ogre Mineral construct of either kind**; committing one replaces the other, and any true successful physical hit or valid Fire/advanced Mineral consumption deregisters it atomically. Normal caches a fixed segment at placement. Charged computes the tiny paddle's actual pose from the Ogre's live position **only within native eligible projectile movement/collision updates**, with cheap no-face exit, short bounding rejection and one bounded **relative swept-path intersection** accounting for both Ogre and projectile motion. No separate per-render-frame full projectile scan, continuous update for each enemy, invisible world wall, homing/block-all fallback or synthetic projectile. High-speed projectile correctness and a moving actor's path must be measured on the actual native owners; if bounded moving interception proves incompatible, **return charged Mineral to design**, not to reflection or a generic ward. Friendly and hostile shots remain identical at the intercept operation. Expiry, death, save/reload, zone transition, and any Fire/Stage 2 plate consumption remove the active face without ghost collisions. EN/ES button copy candidates: “Place shot cover / Colocar cobertura”; “Carry shot parry / Parada móvil de proyectil.”

### Skill 3 — Fire: Thermal Recast (delayed melee setup versus immediate heat shear)

**Owner-directed asymmetry audit:** The former Fire normal primed **one dagger follow-up** and charged primed **two**, with a second attack beat and potentially stronger artifact reaction. The charged increase in counts was the same pattern as slots 4–8. That two-beat/two-primed-contact charged design is **superseded**.

**Normal — Flash Temper: INVEST heat in the NEXT SAME-TARGET DAGGER.** With authenticated nearby real active Fire/heat in clear sight, one legal nearby enemy <=105 receives a compact modest initial contact flash. For candidate ~3s, only **the Ogre's next actually landed ordinary basic dagger contact on that SAME living enemy** claims one prepared target-bound heat seam for one bounded instant rupture. A contact on another enemy does not trigger it. Companions, retaliation, Reversal, Skill Blocked/immune hits, synthetic proc damage and extra Acid splash targets never claim or replicate the seam. No Burn/DoT, persistent aura, boosted critical roll, global attack power or ranged flame projectile.

**Charged — Furnace Shear: RELEASE immediate GEOMETRIC heat rather than preparing daggers.** With the same real active Fire/heat source and interruptible 0.65s hold, make **one directed short pair of crossing narrow heat cuts** anchored at the Ogre's real close-contact geometry (each arm <=105 and blocked by genuine solid terrain). Up to **two UNIQUE genuinely intersected close enemies** receive one instant bounded thermal attack receipt each, with one native skill/critical decision overall. There are **no two separate timed beats, no future primed dagger contacts, and no additional Ogre basic/Acid-chain hit**. No long-range Fire bolt, Burning, stun, Slow, push, enemy debuff, fake terrain or broad Mage-style area storm. The charged technique can be used with only a true Fire source and a legal close enemy, **even if no Water/Mineral artifact is present**; it is not a material-combo-only disabled button.

**Artifact conversion as a SHAPE choice, not a damage multiplier:** If a genuine preexisting Ogre-made **Water Trace or Mineral face** is also eligible within lawful close offensive geometry, the charged Fire preview may **explicitly select exactly one** and **REPLACE** its ordinary cross-cut with a finite **steam shear following that Trace's real path**, or **a physically localized mineral-fragment fan from that face**. Never make the basic shear plus a second reaction, silently spend an unrelated artifact, or grant permission to attack through a wall just because the *natural Fire source* can be seen across a river. The selected artifact is atomically consumed at successful release; after interrupted/invalid charge no phantom spend. When no selected artifact exists, use the ordinary short Furnace Shear.

**The choice:** Normal is focused preparation for the Ogre's next dagger on the **same** foe. Charged is an immediate **aim-and-release geometry decision**, optionally consuming a previous Water/Mineral preparation instead of using a Fire primer. One isolated target often favors normal; a naturally lined-up close group or useful existing artifact can favor charged. This is qualitatively different in **timing, geometry, ownership and follow-up**, not just extra primer duration or damage. It does not copy Stage 2 Fire Ember Ledger, which uniquely depends on **actual Ogre HP loss** before its stored heat can pay out.

**Nonduplication:** The initial normal thermal touch, later dagger-owned primer rupture and charged heat cut have distinct source action receipts. Only the ordinary basic itself advances the 1→2→3 dagger rhythm; charged Fire never generates the third-hit Acid spending event. One original action critical roll, no per-arm reroll and no proc recursion. EN/ES: “Prime dagger / Preparar daga”; “Heat shear / Corte térmico.”

### Phase 2 review — authentic sources and once-only event ownership

**Unchanged owner material rules.** At each committed cast/release, authenticate nearby real current-zone material within line of sight from the Ogre: **Water** genuinely wet/water; **Mineral** stone/rock/ore not dirt/mud; **Fire** active true heat/flame. Natural sources remain present and free. Sight from across an impassable river/ravine may qualify **without pathfinding**; an opaque building/wall blocks sight. Painted texture, protected homes, fake overlays and the Ogre's temporary Water Trace/Mineral face/heat seam never count as newly natural sources. **Stage 1 alpha does not yet permit substitute reagents.** Later paid Neri Advanced Alchemy can use one matching Water Reagent, Mineral Catalyst or Fire Elixir when no real source exists, producing the **same form effect**. Future Lab workers still need genuine physical routes and separate staffing authorization, unlike the Ogre's combat casting.

**Commit/cancel validity.** Recheck actor/learned rank/cooldown, source, real recipient/target/placement, legal close geometry, current owned artifact and any valid native event after charged preparation. Invalidated or interrupted charged casts spend no substitute, artifact, acid, critical receipt or full cooldown. A successfully released charged future-Slow ring or mobile parry that never gets a matching event **does not refund** its cooldown: timing is the gameplay wager. Do not silently swap an expiring Fire reaction to another artifact without player preview.

**Water.** The currently evidenced removable party-hostile timed status is **Slow**. Normal removes one **currently stored** Slow instance/set, never retroactively reversing cooldown debt. Charged stores one small conditional flow ring on exactly one selected actual living active recipient, which consumes itself on that recipient's **newly applied native Slow** event and washes that condition **after** its valid application. No damage prevention, group cleanse, multi-Slow immunity, full Silence suppression, hazard removal or source-free auto-wash. ### Slot 4 — Wood: Tension Loom

**Normal — Set Fulcrum: positional *preparation*.** With nearby genuine Wood in sight or a lawful Wood Essence, choose a legal nonblocking brace position **<=90** from the Ogre. Candidate lifetime **5s**; one active Wood brace per Ogre. The next successful *ordinary Ogre basic dagger contact* qualifies only when Ogre, target and the brace meet a clearly previewed angled facing wedge (~50-unit local neighborhood). It applies a **small capped positioning bonus on that actual target's one basic hit**, and may send one physically legal short splinter continuation toward **at most one other enemy** (~55 beyond contact). No damage occurs on placement or merely because a creature approaches the brace. If the Ogre misses the angle, the brace remains until expiry. A third-beat dagger still automatically spends its one Acid Bottle if stocked, *not* one per splinter; critical receipt is still one.

**Charged — Spring Traverse: intentional *self movement*, not a bigger brace.** From authentic Wood or a matching substitute, pick one **legal nearby landing point** (candidate <=100 from Ogre) and one visible collision-respecting vault arc. The Ogre uses a temporary wooden spring spar to **physically vault to that flank**, with **no attack, companion teleport, ally movement, stun, knockback or invulnerability**. A landing must be reachable along a valid bounded collision-aware movement arc: the Ogre may see a Wood source across a river, but he **cannot vault through impassable water, a wall, a closed doorway or a blocked actor** just because that Wood is eligible. Preserve native boss/actor motion priorities and respect an interrupted charged preparation. The spar has no lasting obstacle, no stored “spring charges” and is not a new Wood source.

**Why choose:** Normal if an enemy will remain within an arranged blade angle; charged when escape/reposition/retaining 90-unit melee contact is more valuable than bonus damage. The two actions are **not** one/two braces. Tap's one brace may survive a later charged vault, but the vault neither triggers nor duplicates it. Expiry, death/zone transition and restore clear the brace.

### Slot 5 — Water: Counterflow

**Normal — Catch the Cure: event-specific *enemy recovery interception*.** With legitimate nearby Water or one lawful Water Reagent, place **one short-lived capillary wick** on one actually targetable living enemy <=100. Candidate life **4s**. When that enemy next receives a **source-verified positive HP-healing receipt**, intercept **at most one bounded portion** of HP it would actually restore and expire. Suggested initial diversion ceiling **25% of one receipt**, with much stricter authored boss limit (e.g. <=2% boss max HP); diverted HP becomes **nothing**, not player healing or true damage. It does not cancel the underlying enemy healing action, boss phases or protected scripted recovery, and it never applies a general anti-heal DoT. Rogue's existing broader duration-based Wither/anti-heal remains wholly unchanged; this distinct Strategist timing mechanic is **one targeted real healing event**, with no damage/withering by itself. If the opponent never heals during the brief window, the wick is wasted.

**Charged — Canal Draw: planned *reaction-route drafting*, not larger heal interception.** With legitimate nearby Water or a lawful Water Reagent, draw **one visible short finite water-guidance path** along legal nearby ground from a real chosen start to end, within candidate **<=100** reach and **~75** total path length. This is one **Ogre-authored Water Trace** (candidate life 4s), **not** a genuine natural Water source, wet terrain, Slow hazard, wall, healing effect or enemy damage. Unlike Stage 1's offensive-contact-generated Water Trace, Canal Draw **requires no enemy target or hit** and grants deliberate placement **before** a Fire/Acid reaction. There is **at most one active Water Trace per Ogre across all Water skills**: drawing a new one replaces an earlier one atomically. A trace only becomes useful if the Ogre later obtains **a separate authentic Fire source/substitute and performs the corresponding Stage 1 Fire reaction**, or **spends real Acid Bottles** on the eligible advanced Acid routing reaction; it is not free damage in itself.

**Why choose:** Tap anticipates a known healer; charged lays a readable future route when enemy healing is irrelevant. Charged can be a poor choice against an isolated non-healing opponent and should not be portrayed as “higher-tier healing denial.” Neither form washes allied Slow—that remains Stage 1 Water's specific identity. A used/expired/replaced Trace has one terminal receipt and can be consumed by **exactly one** valid Fire/Acid reaction. Avoid forced path shape through opaque obstacles.

### Slot 6 — Mineral: Vector Scribing

**Normal — Raking Angle: deliberate *instant scoring attack*.** With legitimate nearby Mineral (real stone/rock/ore, never soil) or lawful Mineral Catalyst, strike one selected legal close enemy <=100 along **one short finite narrow angle**. The physically aligned continuation can contact **at most one additional** enemy if genuinely intersected within ~60 units. No traveling mineral projectile, no auto-target chain, no defense grant, no Slash/Slow/Fracture debuff, no lasting terrain/collision, and one native critical receipt for the whole release. If alone, the primary contact remains useful as modest damage. Optionally preview one existing *own Mineral plate* as an **explicit** one-time pivot for the instant stroke, consuming that plate and deleting its interceptor registration **before** resolving the offensive geometry; without preview/confirmation, the plate is preserved. A broken/expired/consumed plate cannot be spent.

**Charged — Ballast Lock: one-event *anti-displacement footing*.** With legitimate nearby Mineral or lawful Mineral Catalyst, form a fleeting stone counterweight under the Ogre's stance (candidate life **3s**, one active lock). The **next real, explicitly eligible forced movement outcome** targeting the Ogre may be **prevented for position only**: native short shove or forced scatter/displacement, with **damage and any other real linked effects still resolved normally**. Not a stun break, attack negation, reduction, immunity, Paladin ward, general CC resistance, ally protection, Reversal, protection from lethal hits or physical map obstacle. It does not stop ordinary movement commands, incoming projectile hits, enemy actions or non-displacement hazard damage. A real forced move receipt consumes the lock **once**; if no such move happens, the resource expires. Only source-verified displacement families may be added. Scripted boss displacement that cannot be safely separated from its phase remains ineligible unless independently audited.

**Why choose:** Tap to score a carefully aligned foe and decide whether to sacrifice projectile cover; charged when being thrown out of the Ogre's melee position matters more than inflicting damage. The original **Facet Work** plates still intercept both **friendly and enemy traveling physical shots** on their real trajectory, and **Ballast does not reclassify or bypass them**. One Mineral plate owned construct and one personal Ballast state have separate typed IDs; neither becomes a castable natural Mineral source.

### Slot 7 — Fire: Ember Ledger

**Normal — Bank the Heat: accept measured risk for a *future automatic dagger*.** Authentic active Fire/heat source or lawful Fire Elixir, one legal nearby contact <=100, modest instant opening thermal touch. For candidate **4s**, hold **one heat ledger** on the Ogre: the **first eligible actual HP loss** suffered **by the Ogre** after normal defenses may bank a **low bounded amount**. A subsequent **valid ordinary Ogre basic dagger contact** within its remaining window uses that ledger for **one extra bounded heat rupture on that same victim**, then clears it. If he dodges, reverses, is immune/Skill Blocked, no HP is lost, or an unsupported scripted/periodic hazard would be the only source, the ledger banks nothing. A no-damage window still leaves the opening touch but no later bonus. Retaliation, Fire's Stage 1 internal beats and companion attacks do not trigger a ledger.

**Charged — Vent the Ledger: *manual targeted payout* instead of holding heat for a basic.** With the **same genuine Fire/source-or-substitute requirement**, a **live ledger already containing legitimately banked heat** and a legal short close directional target <=100, **consume that one ledger** and release one **instant narrow heat vent** against at most **two genuinely intersected nearby enemies**. The release spends **only** the already banked heat up to the same **combined payout cap** as normal; it does **not** collect a second/larger heat bank, grant immunity or automatically outperform the dagger route. If no actual heat has been banked, **charged is not available** (clear “No stored heat” EN/ES feedback; zero item/cooldown consumed). The player chooses either **wait for the next ordinary dagger contact** *or* commit an interruptible 0.65s charged vent. First valid consumer wins; no double spend if a dagger lands during a queued charge. A charge that fails native interruption retains the preexisting ledger until its original expiry; it cannot preserve/refresh it for free.

**Why choose:** Normal is a quick opening maneuver that rewards surviving a legitimate ordinary hit and staying in melee. Charged is **a different release method**: actively aim the stored heat down a narrow line when an ordinary dagger is undesirable or two targets line up, with longer commitment and meaningful fizzle risk. Neither simply “banks twice.” Do **not** turn self-harm, friendly fire, a manually Reversed warning or deliberate boss hazard abuse into the ledger source; exclude boss warning releases/periodic fields from this first candidate. A single resulting basic may also carry Stage 1 Fire seam/Wood brace/third-beat Acid, but per-basic secondary damage budgets and at-most-one critical decision stay bounded.

### Slot 8 — Acid: Etched Circuit

**Normal — Scoring Stroke: *immediate* aimed expenditure.** This learned Acid skill uses **one Acid Bottle** from the authoritative shared pool on one valid short <=100 directed scoring strike, damaging an actually contacted primary enemy and **at most one** genuinely lined-up secondary enemy. The strike is instant, nonpersistent, not a ranged spell, does not apply Slow/Burn/Weaken/Fracture/poison stacks, and does not advance the dagger three-beat rhythm. If a real Stage 1 or Canal Draw Water Trace is available in the same close fight, the player may **explicitly opt** to consume that **one** trace to follow its finite legal line (positioning change only, no free bonus damage); Fire can no longer use an already spent trace.

**Charged — Bitter Boundary: *delayed spatial wager*, not a wider scoring strike.** Spend a **proposed two Acid Bottles** to author **one short visible, nonblocking corrosive seam** across valid close ground (candidate length <=75, life **4s**). This attack does **zero immediate damage** on cast; the line only pays modest instant damage when a **living hostile genuinely crosses it after placement** along a validated movement segment. Candidate cap **two crossings total** and **once per individual enemy**; no ticking damage for standing on it, no automatic attack upon spawning under an already occupying enemy, no Slow/stun/knockback, no change to enemy navigation weights or world collision, no hidden persistent poison field. The Ogre and his companions are not injured by their own seam under existing friendly-safe hero splash principles, but enemies cannot be damaged by more than the bounded unique crossing receipts. If nobody crosses, the spent bottles are lost. At most **one owned Bitter Boundary** active; a new valid one replaces the old. A legitimate Fire/Water/Mineral material source nearby does **not** waive Acid cost; the hazard is not a natural Acid source. Optional Water Trace may alter its finite legal orientation for **no extra damage**, consuming the trace exactly once.

**Why choose:** Tap if the Ogre needs **damage now**; charged only when enemy movement makes a **future crossing likely** and the two-bottle gamble is acceptable. It can be objectively worse than tap in stationary boss combat. This is the Strategist's authored short-lane control, not another generic two-line explosion, with collision integrity and CPU cost as genuine engineering vetoes.

### Combined tactical relationships and one-receipt ownership

1. **Wood brace + dagger** rewards a real positional hit; a later **Wood charged vault** moves only the Ogre through a legal short arc to set up a position rather than triggering the brace or generating its damage. One ordinary dagger contact advances only one automatic combo beat and, if it is a stocked third hit, consumes **exactly one** Acid Bottle. Splinters/heat premiums never add a fourth autoattack.
2. **Enemy healing forecast vs future reaction geometry**: Water tap can catch one *real enemy HP restoration receipt*; Water charge instead authors a finite Trace without combat contact. A Stage 1 Water offensive Trace and charged Canal Draw use the **same one-Trace ownership cap**. Fire or Acid may claim it **once**, not both.
3. **Mineral cover vs offense vs footing**: Stage 1 Mineral faces preserve **actual bidirectional projectile interception**, including friendly Archer arrows: normal Stone Facet STOPS one crossing shot; charged Running Facet briefly carries one Ogre-relative single-shot physical interceptor instead of stationary cover; its STOP applies equally to both sides. Slot 6 tap may optionally **spend** such cover for local geometry only if specifically previewed. Slot 6 charged protects only the Ogre's *position* from one supported forced movement; it does **not** protect him from a projectile, a status-only warning, damage, or the need to execute Reversal.
4. **Fire ledger fork**: normal opening touch banks at most one actual eligible Ogre HP loss. The **next successful basic** may cash it as close heat; alternatively charged **Vent** spends the very same ledger on one deliberately aimed short line. Neither route changes the authentic incoming hit, manual Reversal, chance Dodge/retaliation or one native critical decision. A failed/expired ledger cannot be conjured again.
5. **Acid now vs later**: normal Acid scores immediately using **one** bottle; charged Acid pays **two** to mark a seam that pays only when an actual enemy movement segment crosses it. The third-dagger Acid finisher always independently spends **one** available bottle on a valid third beat; no player reserve toggle or artificial stock protection, and no duplicate bottle spends from secondary targets.

**No hidden source manufacture:** created braces, heat ledgers, Water Traces, Mineral plates, Ballast Locks and Acid seams are bounded *artifacts*, not legitimate naturally occurring Water/Wood/Mineral/Fire. A legal source must still be newly authenticated for another cast (or matching product spent after appropriate Neri education). Reversal uses true live attack geometry independently, not any of these artifacts as a fake warning.

### Corrective design architecture — meaningful material causality, not another rushed eight-skill list

The next design iteration must start from this **one playable mechanic loop**, not sixteen independent cool names:

**Observe a real obstacle, attack trajectory, ally state or material → choose a real source, facing and a close action → work that material into a finite physical tool or well-defined reaction → deliberately move/strike/enter a genuine opening → claim one real effect with meaningful alternative cost.** When a genuine boss warning occurs, **Reversal remains the highest-skill class-native route**, not a bonus resource gained for failing. Sources change tactical opportunities; without them the Ogre is still effective via 90-unit dual daggers, automatic stocked third-hit Acid, Soldier+Archer, passive defenses and legitimate Reversal.

**Material role discipline:**
- **Water = wash / route / exchange real local flow.** Instant removal of an actual hostile condition is a legitimate role; a second Water form/lesson should **reposition, carry, split or spend a real finite stream** in the encounter, not merely place another Slow timer or copy Rogue enemy anti-heal. Distinguish Stage 1 contact-created trace and advanced deliberate Water control with different, independently useful physical purposes.
- **Mineral = bearing, angle, genuine force interception and leverage.** Slot 2 must continue real two-sided physical projectile contact (including friendly arrows), low CPU and no ricochet. Advanced Mineral should use **the cost of retaining or spending a real protective angle** to create an active close-combat choice, not shoot a short mineral beam or give Paladin-style passive status resistance. Avoid a second generic shield.
- **Fire = conversion, contact, thermal reaction on a previously true event.** Preserve the possibility of preparing a real dagger or consuming a chosen existing Water/Mineral artifact. Advanced Fire should be powered by **successful player offense or bona fide material preparation**, NEVER by asking the player to lose HP or deliberately fail Reversal. Distinguish *priming*, *converting*, *releasing* using player-controlled timing, not three separate lines of heat damage.
- **Wood = lever, tension, counterweight and changed physical line of action.** A brace/spring is a good unifying craft vocabulary **only if it alters actual melee position/contact geometry**, not a temporary damage buff or generic Ranger dash. It should be both legible and useful against a single ordinary close enemy; no artificial terrain generation.

Learned Acid must make scarce bottles **meaningfully different** from that splash (e.g. deliberate preparation of a precise existing contact/construct reaction, not another line/circle hit or generic poison DoT). Costs must be justified against automatic stock depletion; no reserve switch or free Acid.

1. **Solo ordinary nonhealer, 1–3 enemies, no special forced movement:** Every skill the player has learned and has a legal authentic source/consumable for must have **at least one credible tactical value** here. 7. **Native ownership and CPU:** Source check class-only on commitment, finite owned constructs, no new per-render world/enemy/projectile scans, unique critical/Acid/combo receipt, safe old-class/save-v4 paths. **Creative doctrine corrected:** Normal and charged are **not automatically weak/strong variants**, but neither must be unrelated in every slot. Source-rich skills reward the player for selecting fight locations and making short combinations, not for a fake map node. **Paid skills whose charged form is situational (escort with no living companion; melee parry without hostile melee basic; Acid with no workpiece)** still have a standalone normal form when the relevant source/item is present; describe in UI rather than hide the limitation.

**Gameplay input:** No five extra long submenus or source selection. Proposals use the existing directional aim/facing and clear one-recipient/artifact previews, with default deterministic close eligible target; if the actual existing input architecture cannot select a companion or physically author the water segment in one phone-readable hold, **revise the interaction design rather than silently add a new cursor/party command**. Keep the separate Reversal button obvious. For optional one-item substitution after Neri, use a clear natural-material-available indicator **only at cast attempt**, not an always-on world material radar.

### 8. Second-pass design critique and acceptance status

**Compared to the prior audited v2 problems:**
- **Removed entirely:** advanced Water enemy anti-healing (Rogue Sour Note overlap), charged early Water future-Slow insurance token, Fire 3 double-heat lines (mini Mage), advanced Mineral generic one-hit anti-displacement Ballast, **Fire from the Ogre taking HP damage**, and two-Bottle enemy-crossing Acid trap.
- **Preserved and made concrete:** owner-loved honest Water Slow cleansing, Stage 1 physical two-sided **no-reflection** Mineral shot interception (including friendly Archer arrows), close-dagger heat follow-up, physical Wood leverage, one-use material conversion, and separately paid Neri progression.
- **New actual tactical coverage:** one companion positional correction **without** a persistent Rogue/Ranger buff; short *self* recoil versus short *other/foe* physical lateral lever; close ordinary-melee contact parry **distinct from boss-warning Reversal**; **post-landed-basic** earned Fire that chooses *one genuine companion weapon contact* OR one linked close foe without same-slot tap/hold cooldown recursion; and one-Bottle exact-timing/actual-workpiece Acid vs guaranteed third-beat crowd splash.
- **Honest remaining doubts, NOT concealed:** native **moving** projectile-face swept collision; ally positional selection on two-thumb mobile; lateral ordinary enemy movement without encounter/leash script damage; identifying an actual geometry/contact owner for ordinary melee parry; whether Wood's angled basic continuation is materially different and visually readable; and Acid's optional special physical-guard math given native attack-protection owners. These are engineering/source-review **VETO checkpoints**, not authority to broaden the feature or add status systems. In particular, Acid must never set a global all-defenses piercing flag.

**Cross-class anti-copy pass:** The Ogre retains **neither Paladin's persistent Ward/Stance or wide taunt**, **nor Mage's ranged beams, Freeze/Slow and Silence**, **nor Ranger's general Haste/Rally or self/party HP Heal**, **nor Rogue Bard's duration-based Wither, healing, cooldown debt, music stations, party boons or broad displacement**. His class-exclusive payoffs are **authored short-lived real workpieces plus actual personal/companion geometry, directed physical contact, the source-true enemy/friendly projectile drawback, shared finite chemical expenditure, and player-performed full-party warning Reversal**. Some basic melee/contact bonus is unavoidable in an action RPG; it is acceptable only when the **physical trigger and opportunity cost** differ demonstrably from another hero's generic damage attack. If a form becomes a renamed Mage attack, Rogue field, Paladin ward or Ranger dash in runtime, **REVISE rather than declare conceptual distinctiveness by adjective**.

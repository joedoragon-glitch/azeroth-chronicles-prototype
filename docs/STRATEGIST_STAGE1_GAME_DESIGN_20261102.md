**What is already settled:** The playable hero is an existing-species **Ogre**, nimble but unquestionably close-melee, using **dual daggers** with **90-world-unit** ordinary basic reach. He begins with **Soldier + Archer**, Expedition Rank 1, **three already-learned** material skills **Water, Mineral, Fire** (each tap and charged), and a separate manual **Reversal** action outside the eventual eight skills. **Mineral means stone/rock/ore, never dirt**. No new Wind material, no free no-source alchemy, no fabricated environmental nodes or map redesign. Natural sources are free, and later purchased matching substitute items give exactly the same effect but consume stock. There are **five later individually paid skills after rescuing Neri**, plus ordinary martial instructor rank progression; no separate bonus companion slot. The dagger chain's eligible third hit automatically spends **one Acid Bottle if stocked**, otherwise ends with a plain dagger; initial stock is finite. The first playable version has a conservative **Strategist-only Dodge**, distinct melee-basic retaliation and manual whole-affected-party, **attack-instance-specific Reversal**. Later Field Labs, staffed windmill conversion, and across-Succession rules remain as defined in #674; they are **not Stage 1 alpha functionality**.

**What the owner clarified on 2 November:** The three starting alchemies must **not repeat other heroes' or monsters' effects**. In particular, no recycled **Slow**, **knockback**, or generic elemental projectile as a principal effect. The owner explicitly welcomed **water-based in-combat cleansing of real hostile status effects** as a distinctive party support action. Build unique tap-versus-charge decisions and material-dependent tactics. This does not itself mean every new action below is individually approved.

## Phase 1 — identity and distinctiveness

The Ogre is a **battlefield engineer** and environmental opportunist, not an elemental Mage or a generic potion thrower. He reads actual accessible sources of water, solid mineral and active fire; modifies a small piece of the immediate fight; and operates in close reach where positional mistakes matter. His verbs are **Water = wash / route**, **Mineral = work / brace**, **Fire = convert / finish**, **Reversal = enter danger and redirect the source attack**, **daggers = sustained close pressure**. Water can support companions without becoming a Ranger heal; Mineral offers **physical directional projectile interception**, not a Paladin ward; Fire reacts with an earlier Ogre-made construct or primes a finite dagger contact rather than creating Burning, a huge AoE or enemy-style jet.

Principal exclusions: generic Slow, knockback, Stun, Silence, Weaken/Fracture, persistent burn damage-over-time, generic shield/immunity, huge AoE, free elemental casts, artificial terrain, RTS-style orders or new narrative companions. These are originality safeguards **for the new Strategist design only**, not requests to revise existing gameplay.

Natural source **eligibility is authoritative world data**, never painted graphics, arbitrary grass/dirt, decorative splashes, ash/sunset or a source behind impassable terrain. The Ogre uses real world opportunities and accepts that some fights lack them. Empty-area baseline remains daggers, Soldier, Archer, finite Acid Bottles, Dodge, retaliation and legitimate Reversal.

## Phase 2 — three starting material skills / six proposed action forms

**Owner-confirmed:** Water, Mineral and Fire are each learned at rank 1 at New Game. Their **exact effects and working titles below are newly proposed**, with no retroactive claim of final signoff. Existing tap input and approximately **0.65-second hold-to-charge** convention apply. Tap and charged variants share each skill's cooldown. Stage 1 uses genuine natural sources; later Neri products are **not granted early**. Provisional check radius: water ~150, mineral ~140, fire ~140 world units, subject to truthful path/line/reach verification; offensive reach generally <=105 units and the hero's ordinary basic melee reach stays 90. Values are tuning candidates only.

### Skill 1 — Water: Current Weave

**Tap — Rinsing Cut:** From genuinely reachable water, deliver a narrow, modest-damage contact wash to a nearby hostile, while removing **one actual eligible hostile timed condition** from the Ogre or one nearby **living active companion**. Target priority proposal: self if affected; otherwise nearest affected living companion, stable tie-breaker. A **cleanse-only** tap is legal if a recipient is affected but no hostile is in range; no enemy and no removable status means no cast and no spent cooldown.

**Charged — Cleansing Circuit:** Wash the nearby living active company, clearing **all currently eligible removable hostile timed conditions per recipient**, accompanied by a compact low-damage contact wash of enemies actually touched by the local path (suggest cap three). This is **not healing, reviving, broad area damage, blanket immunity, universal status cancellation, nor slowing/pushing the enemy**. Charge's tactical trade is broader allied benefit against a longer cooldown and interruptible preparation.

**Cleansing truth:** A source-defined allowlist must cover only statuses really stored on party members and actually removable. **Slow** is a valid candidate, but cleanse clears its active duration/stacks **without undoing cooldown debt already incurred**. Current combat documentation also describes ongoing **Silence fields**, enemy Dust Cover, forced scatter, hazards, warning geometry and other effects that are **not a removable timed condition on the target**; a water cast cannot delete them. A persistent field may reapply its restriction. The final status allowlist requires a precise owner-side audit; do not promise cleansing every status-like effect in the UI.

**Water Trace (proposed):** A successful offensive Water cast may leave **at most one** short-lived **3–4s** guided trace between real water source and contact position, which Fire may later consume. It is **not a new natural water source**, not a world edit, not a movement hazard, and has no native Slow. Cleansing-only casts do not generate a free offensive trace by default.

### Skill 2 — Mineral: Facet Work

**Tap — Stone Facet:** Work genuine accessible stone/rock/ore into **one short angled projectile-intercepting plate** (suggest 55-unit width) at a nearby legal position. It intercepts **one genuinely eligible directed physical projectile from either side** and breaks; a close enemy at the initial worksite may take a modest one-off contact cut. **It does not stop melee, character movement, ground-targeted blasts, phase attacks, every magical effect, or navigation**. The original resource remains present and undepleted.

**Charged — Cut Gate:** Two oriented short plates with a readable gap, intercepting **two eligible directed projectiles total** before expiry or break. It rewards anticipation and safe-angle positioning, not bigger mineral damage. The construct may hinder **friendly Archer shots as well as enemy shots** if placed badly; its two-sided fairness is essential. Use **one active construct per Ogre** in Stage 1. Fire may optionally consume it in exchange for a distinct offensive fragment reaction.

**Hard feasibility gate:** This is honest projectile-hit interception, **not a newly colliding world wall**. No A* navigation obstacle, pixel-based collisions, arbitrary shield buff or interception of attack families the real engine cannot represent truthfully. If real projectile provenance and two-direction detection cannot be integrated safely, **return this skill to game design** rather than implementing a fake protection effect.

### Skill 3 — Fire: Thermal Recast

**Tap — Flash Temper:** From a real reachable active heat/flame source, strike one nearby enemy with a compact controlled contact flash and **prime one next successful dagger hit** with a small instant extra heat cut (suggest 3-second expiry). No long-range fireball, Burn/DoT, Haste, or general attack-power buff. If the player has an intersecting Ogre-made **Water Trace**, Fire may **spend that trace** to issue a brief directional steam rupture along the original finite path. If a **Mineral Facet** is chosen, it can **consume that cover** for a localized mineral-fragment reaction at its real position. These effects are **finite** and do not induce Slow, push, Stun, Silence or Weaken.

**Charged — Furnace Sequence:** One deliberate compact two-beat thermal maneuver on a close target, with **up to two subsequent dagger contacts primed** (suggest 4-second expiry) when no prepared artifact exists. With valid Water Trace or Mineral Facet, preview and consume **exactly one** chosen artifact for a stronger/differently shaped finite reaction. No double consumption, extra automatic Acid Bottle spending, or extra critical roll per internal beat. The two beats belong to one charged action/cooldown.

**Reaction selection:** The player must be able to see **which one artifact** will be spent. Provisional rule: selected threatened target's intersecting Water Trace, else nearest eligible artifact, stable tie-breaker. Never automatically stack both reactions. The player must **also possess a real fire source**; a trace/facet is not a substitute for Fire. Water+Fire or Mineral+Fire crossover is an **opportunistic discovery**, not a mandatory core rotation.

### Provisional six-form tuning card (not approved class balance)

| Action | Cooldown | Genuine natural source proximity | Range, purpose and temporary cap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Water tap | **2.0 s** | ~150 | Close enemy <=105; one eligible ally <=135; low ~0.8 dagger-hit equivalent; one Trace 4s |
| Water charged | **8.0 s** | ~150 | Company cleanse <=150; compact enemy wash <=105; max three foes; one Trace 4s |
| Mineral tap | **3.0 s** | ~140 | ~55-unit plate; one projectile or 4s; modest local worksite contact |
| Mineral charged | **9.0 s** | ~140 | Two angled plates/opening; two projectile intercepts total or 5s |
| Fire tap | **2.5 s** | ~140 | One close enemy <=105; modest immediate hit; one primed dagger / 3s |
| Fire charged | **7.0 s** | ~140 | One close target <=105; two-beat action, at most two primed contacts / 4s, or one reaction |

All widths, radii, percentages, cooldowns, conditions and effects are **working hypotheses** for the design review and engineering feasibility assessment. Combat source, renderer and device acceptance have not happened.

**Illustrative scenarios:** (1) An Archer slowed near a real shore receives Water tap cleanse but not HP restoration or retroactive cooldown debt removal; (2) a quarry-side bowman shoots into a properly oriented one-use mineral plate, whereas badly oriented cover can stop friendly arrows; (3) a true campfire lets Fire spend a nearby earlier Water Trace as steam; (4) an empty arena denies all three material skills but preserves normal Ogre dagger-and-party combat.

## Phase 3 — dagger rhythm, Acid, Reversal, Dodge and retaliation

**Daggers:** Quick, relatively low per-hit dual-dagger basics at **90-world-unit** melee reach. Every **valid actual third automatic contact** spends **exactly one Acid Bottle when shared stock contains one** and performs a strong **compact-area acid splash**. Without stock, third beat is an ordinary dagger hit, not a skipped hit or free infinite acid. One spend per real finisher, **never** on canceled/missed/duplicated callbacks. No reserve toggle, threshold or elite-only exception. Acid skills later share the pool, but **none is secretly granted at Stage 1**. A **finite 48-bottle trial quantity** is a *local proposal only*, not approved or proven enough for a full campaign; there is **no temporary free regeneration or fake Lab**.

**Manual Reversal:** Separate contextual input, not Skill 1 nor a ninth learned skill. Require the Ogre to **physically enter the true future impact footprint** of a **source-verified reversible live warning** and activate before that specific native release. A success protects **all hero/companions actually affected by that one release**, removing its HP damage and causally attached debuffs, and redirects source-native attack force at its attacker **only where legitimate**. A **status-only reversed release** only cancels its affected statuses; never conjure reflected damage/stagger. Do not cancel an entire boss phase, an unrelated warning, a later volley, an already persistent hazard or unverified moving projectile. No artificial consecutive-success quota. Clear invalid/no-target/outside/late/replaced/duplicate feedback, no fake reflected damage or full ability cooldown on invalid use. First alpha should include **at least one real early Greenwood reversible warning** (Thornfang Tripwire is *only a candidate pending native proof*) and a **second independently validated attack geometry**; #696's Mine and Archive narrow proof does not itself establish early playability or general eligibility.

**Passive Dodge:** Modest **Strategist-only** chance on real eligible hostile hit receipts; protects the Ogre alone and avoids attached on-hit conditions. Provisional **5%**. Not a group ward, teleport, or universal hazard dodge; no spillover to other classes or enemies; honest Ogre-only Miss feedback and one deterministic roll per hit.

**Passive retaliation:** Distinct provisional **10% chance**, evaluated once on an **enemy close melee basic directed at the Ogre**, potentially even if Dodge succeeds. One bounded dagger response if the attacker remains a valid close target. Does **not** trigger for ranged basics, telegraphs, spells, persistent hazards or recursively from another retaliation.

**Starting statistic candidate, not approved:** HP **135**, Power **17**, Armor **9**, speed **290**, natural +25 HP/level; possible +6 movement speed/level (not automatically accepted); proposed purchased discipline ranks Power +6, Mana +2, HP +35, speed +37. No new active mana resource, free global cooldown reduction, generic damage buff, or change to another class. Compare at matched equipment/level/training under Normal/Nightmare and solo/party before claiming balance.

## Phase 4 — first controlled playable journey

- New Game: existing Ogre visual species identity, default Soldier+Archer, rank 1, genuine learned Water/Mineral/Fire at rank 1; **slots 4–8 clearly show future individually paid Neri lessons**, not placeholders masquerading as real attacks. Standard company, training and existing v4 save constraints.
- First combat: ordinary dual daggers work with **no eligible material nearby**. Show finite stocked third-hit acid only when bottles exist. Clear, truthful source eligibility, compact cards and no invented natural materials.
- Exploring: find **existing** accessible Water, real Mineral and active Fire in normal regions; do not move world objects merely to produce a conveniently equal elemental tutorial. If early fire does not exist, document actual first legitimate opportunity and adjust the trial route rather than faking a campfire.
- First support/positioning: Water cleanses an actual legally removable hostile effect on a party member. Mineral intercepts genuine shots **from either side**, explicitly not AoE. Fire works alone and, opportunistically, consumes one suitable prepared construction. Separate early attack witness demonstrates actual Reversal timing and party protection. Test dry arenas as an intentional negative.
- Interface: native tap/charged input, a unique contextual Reversal control within established tactical command region, phone mirrored/two-thumb and desktop bindings/focus, no joystick hitbox conflict. Short accurate source/no-target/cooldown information in **English and Spanish**, limited truthful visuals for Water Trace/Facet/Fire and appropriate reduced-motion behavior. No redesign of other classes' controls or effects.
- Saves: atomic fifth-class admission through merged guarded foundation; do not trigger ordinary Skill-2 companion-training reward just because starting skill ranks 1–3 are prelearned. Persist learned ranks and Acid pool, not ghost expired traces/facets/warning receipts. Old v4 saves and existing heroes remain viable; handle death, recovery, zone change, reload, challenge and Succession edges.
- **Explicit Stage 1 alpha exclusions:** Field Labs, noncombat worker qualification, windmills/production, advanced material substitutes, Neri's later five paid skills, advanced mastery, remote output, other-class Succession bottle throws, optional new companion/story route. Keep canonical future designs intact. First test drive **is not a full-campaign fifth-class launch**.

## Phase 5 — acceptance, failure and handoff to engineering

**Design signoff still required:** Confirm all three mechanics really feel different; agree provisional Water status allowlist and target selection, Mineral projectile interception/facing fairness, Fire setup/spend choice and source scarcity, early warning family, alpha progression limits, acid-stock duration and controls. Resolve any *consequential* differences with owner via the four-option interactive questionnaire. Lower-level tuning can remain parameterized, marked provisional.

**Engineering feasibility duties after design signoff:** Enumerate true reachable existing Water/Mineral/Fire sources; verify source geometry, LOS, blocked shores, migrating/generated world and dungeons. Trace Water recipient status state and removable conditions; trace directed projectile ownership/interception; trace one early authentic reversible warning plus second family; verify current class registry, Skill 2 progression side effects, save-v4 validation, companion status recipient semantics, cooldown/charge input, cross-system critical/Skill Block and both Canvas/Pixi presentations. **Engineering may propose design revision when a claimed physical effect cannot be implemented truthfully, not quietly replace it with a generic spell.**

**Playtest acceptance:** Can start a real Ogre, fight source-free, find one of each legitimate environmental material when naturally available, use six true forms, see one early authentic Reversal, perform legitimate Dodge/retaliation/acid and reload safely. Verify negative/late/outside/border conditions; no unintended older hero changes. Device-verified requires **actual device** observation; browser/CI pass is not hardware acceptance.

**GO:** Full bounded first-play loop and honest exclusions above behave correctly in focused tests and controlled playtest. **REVISE:** Source scarcity/cover semantics/warning timing is unworkable; return only the affected Strategist move to design. **NO-GO:** Corrupt legacy saves or existing classes, duplicate bottles, phantom materials, misleading interception or boss-phase-deleting reversal.

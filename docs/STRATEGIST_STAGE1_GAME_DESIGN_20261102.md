## 3 November design review — v1.1

The entries below refine the **same five Stage 1 design phases**. They are design resolutions under the delegated creative mandate, **not owner-approved changes to the settled canon**. Each phase has a reviewed design contract; no live-engine implementation or full design signoff is implied. The single consequential editorial choice held for the owner is whether to retain the proposed **two-sided, directional Mineral interception** as the class's opening spatial tool or choose a different primary Mineral fantasy. Until selected, two-sided interception is the **recommended working candidate**, not canon.

### Phase 1 review — what makes this a Strategist

**One-page identity contract.** The player approaches a close melee fight with forethought, not remote spellcasting. The Ogre can win a source-free encounter using dual daggers and an ordinary Soldier + Archer; a material source changes *how* the next few seconds unfold, never whether the character can function at all. Water repairs a compromised fighting line by cleansing a real enemy-imposed condition; Mineral changes the direction in which an authentic traveling shot can safely pass; Fire exploits contact and previously prepared material. Reversal is a fourth, separate player skill expression: choose to run **into** an actual warned strike rather than stand outside it, then counter that **one** release for the whole affected company.

**Difference from the four existing classes.** Against a slowed Archer by a river, the Ogre cleanses Slow but does not provide Ranger healing or the Paladin's damage reduction. At an exposed quarry approach, a correctly angled Mineral plate intercepts one shot and can also spoil the friendly Archer's shot; it is **not** a Paladin ward, immune state or terrain wall. On a boss warning, the Ogre's deliberate inside-footprint Reversal is **not** Mage's remote warning interruption. Unlike Rogue Bard, the Strategist neither Charms enemies nor performs musical support. His close-range vulnerability and dependence on authentic sources are real costs.

**Originality checks.** None of the six opening forms principally applies Slow, knockback, Stun, Silence, Weaken, an ordinary Heal-over-time, global damage buff, immunity or a ranged elemental bolt. A cast must produce a materially different next decision: *which ally needs cleansing*, *which actual shot lane is worth covering*, or *which nearby enemy/artifact can receive a finite thermal follow-up*. Water Trace and Mineral plate are short-lived **Ogre-made tactical artifacts**, not new natural terrain, harvestable materials or permanent world obstacles.

**Scarcity is permitted.** Three simultaneous natural-source requirements are never assumed. Fire and Water synergy is an occasional opportunity, not the baseline rotation. When a particular material does not exist nearby, its skill is simply unavailable and the Ogre remains a viable dagger fighter. Any alpha showcase must describe where the existing world actually provides the material, without changing the world to suit the showcase.

## Phase 1 — identity and distinctiveness

The Ogre is a **battlefield engineer** and environmental opportunist, not an elemental Mage or a generic potion thrower. He reads actual accessible sources of water, solid mineral and active fire; modifies a small piece of the immediate fight; and operates in close reach where positional mistakes matter. His verbs are **Water = wash / route**, **Mineral = work / brace**, **Fire = convert / finish**, **Reversal = enter danger and redirect the source attack**, **daggers = sustained close pressure**. Water can support companions without becoming a Ranger heal; Mineral offers **physical directional projectile interception**, not a Paladin ward; Fire reacts with an earlier Ogre-made construct or primes a finite dagger contact rather than creating Burning, a huge AoE or enemy-style jet.

Principal exclusions: generic Slow, knockback, Stun, Silence, Weaken/Fracture, persistent burn damage-over-time, generic shield/immunity, huge AoE, free elemental casts, artificial terrain, RTS-style orders or new narrative companions. These are originality safeguards **for the new Strategist design only**, not requests to revise existing gameplay.

Natural source **eligibility is authoritative world data**, never painted graphics, arbitrary grass/dirt, decorative splashes, ash/sunset or a source behind impassable terrain. The Ogre uses real world opportunities and accepts that some fights lack them. Empty-area baseline remains daggers, Soldier, Archer, finite Acid Bottles, Dodge, retaliation and legitimate Reversal.

## Phase 2 — three starting material skills / six proposed action forms

**Owner-confirmed:** Water, Mineral and Fire are each learned at rank 1 at New Game. Their **exact effects and working titles below are newly proposed**, with no retroactive claim of final signoff. Existing tap input and approximately **0.65-second hold-to-charge** convention apply. Tap and charged variants share each skill's cooldown. Stage 1 uses genuine natural sources; later Neri products are **not granted early**. Provisional check radius: water ~150, mineral ~140, fire ~140 world units, subject to truthful path/line/reach verification; offensive reach generally <=105 units and the hero's ordinary basic melee reach stays 90. Values are tuning candidates only.

### Skill 1 — Water: Current Weave

**Tap — Rinsing Cut:** From genuinely reachable water, deliver a narrow, modest-damage contact wash to a nearby hostile, while removing **one actual eligible hostile timed condition** from the Ogre or one nearby **living active companion**. Target priority proposal: self if affected; otherwise nearest affected living companion, stable tie-breaker. A **cleanse-only** tap is legal if a recipient is affected but no hostile is in range; no enemy and no removable status means no cast and no spent cooldown.

**Charged — Cleansing Circuit:** Wash the nearby living active company, clearing **all currently eligible removable hostile timed conditions per recipient**, accompanied by a compact low-damage contact wash of enemies actually touched by the local path (suggest cap three). This is **not healing, reviving, broad area damage, blanket immunity, universal status cancellation, nor slowing/pushing the enemy**. Charge's tactical trade is broader allied benefit against a longer cooldown and interruptible preparation.

**Cleansing truth:** A source-defined allowlist must cover only statuses really stored on party members and actually removable. **Slow** is a valid candidate, but cleanse clears its active duration/stacks **without undoing cooldown debt already incurred**. Current combat documentation also describes ongoing **Silence fields**, enemy Dust Cover, forced scatter, hazards, warning geometry and other effects that are **not a removable timed condition on the target**; a water cast cannot delete them. A persistent field may reapply its restriction. The final status allowlist requires a precise owner-side audit; do not promise cleansing every status-like effect in the UI.

**Water Trace (proposed):** A successful offensive Water cast may leave **at most one** short-lived **3–4s** guided trace between real water source and contact position, which Fire may later consume. It is **not a new natural water source**, not a world edit, not a movement hazard, and has no native Slow. Cleansing-only casts do not generate a free offensive trace by default.

### Skill 2 — Mineral: Facet Work

**Tap — Stone Facet:** Work genuine accessible stone/rock/ore into **one short angled projectile-intercepting plate** (suggest 55-unit width) at a nearby legal position. It intercepts **one genuinely eligible directed physical projectile from either side** and breaks; it inflicts no incidental construction damage. **It does not stop melee, character movement, ground-targeted blasts, phase attacks, every magical effect, or navigation**. The original resource remains present and undepleted. The proposed incidental worksite damage is withdrawn in v1.1: the plate is a deliberate defensive geometry tool, not a free damaging cast.

**Charged — Cut Gate:** Two oriented short plates with a readable gap, intercepting **two eligible directed projectiles total** before expiry or break. It rewards anticipation and safe-angle positioning, not bigger mineral damage. The construct may hinder **friendly Archer shots as well as enemy shots** if placed badly; its two-sided fairness is essential. Use **one active construct per Ogre** in Stage 1. Fire may optionally consume it in exchange for a distinct offensive fragment reaction.

**Hard feasibility gate:** This is honest projectile-hit interception, **not a newly colliding world wall**. No A* navigation obstacle, pixel-based collisions, arbitrary shield buff or interception of attack families the real engine cannot represent truthfully. If real projectile provenance and two-direction detection cannot be integrated safely, **return this skill to game design** rather than implementing a fake protection effect.

### Skill 3 — Fire: Thermal Recast

**Tap — Flash Temper:** From a real reachable active heat/flame source, strike one nearby enemy with a compact controlled contact flash and **prepare one next successful dagger contact against that same struck enemy** for a small instant heat rupture (suggest 3-second expiry). No long-range fireball, Burn/DoT, Haste, or general attack-power buff. If the player has an intersecting Ogre-made **Water Trace**, Fire may **spend that trace** to issue a brief directional steam rupture along the original finite path. If a **Mineral Facet** is chosen, it can **consume that cover** for a localized mineral-fragment reaction at its real position. These effects are **finite** and do not induce Slow, push, Stun, Silence or Weaken.

**Charged — Furnace Sequence:** One deliberate compact two-beat thermal maneuver on a close target, with **up to two subsequent contacts against that same target prepared** (suggest 4-second expiry) when no prepared artifact exists. With valid Water Trace or Mineral Facet, preview and consume **exactly one** chosen artifact for a stronger/differently shaped finite reaction. No double consumption, extra automatic Acid Bottle spending, or extra critical roll per internal beat. The two beats belong to one charged action/cooldown.

**Reaction selection:** The player must be able to see **which one artifact** will be spent. Provisional rule: selected threatened target's intersecting Water Trace, else nearest eligible artifact, stable tie-breaker. Never automatically stack both reactions. The player must **also possess a real fire source**; a trace/facet is not a substitute for Fire. Water+Fire or Mineral+Fire crossover is an **opportunistic discovery**, not a mandatory core rotation.

### Phase 2 review — binding candidate interaction rules (v1.1)

**Source-oracle contract.** Each natural cast refers to a genuine source object/terrain fact in the **current zone**, with a stable identity and currently usable state. The source must be within the specified candidate proximity and actually reachable/usable from a legal standing position; close proximity across a wall, impassable water, blocked shore or disconnected floor is not sufficient. Water must correspond to authored water, Mineral to authentic stone/rock/ore, and Fire to an active, accessible heat/flame source. Art particles, ambient lighting, decorative recolors, dirt and obsolete saved coordinates never qualify. A source can be sampled repeatedly; casting does not deplete it. The exact whitelist, locations, path/line checks and query budget are **engineering-preparation evidence to be supplied**, not source facts invented by this document.

**Validation and failure order.** On a press or charged-release commitment, confirm: (1) Ogre is alive and action input is legal; (2) learned rank/cooldown/charge state permits it; (3) the **same** still-authentic source or lawful later substitute is eligible; (4) intended target, recipient, placement and actual attack path are legal; (5) any reaction artifact is still alive and uniquely identified. Only then commit the cooldown, apply effects and consume **at most one** selected artifact. Recheck volatile source/target facts after the 0.65s charged preparation; an interrupted or invalidated charge gives normal failure/retry feedback rather than full cooldown, artifact spend or phantom damage. Stage 1 has **no** consumable source substitutes. Tap and charge share a single skill's cooldown ledger: a tap imposes the tap value, a charge the longer charge value; they are not two simultaneous cooldown pools.

**Water.** For Stage 1, the only clearly documented *removable party-hostile timed condition* is **Slow** (the duration and active stacks, not the historical cooldown debt). That is the **safe provisional allowlist**; additional statuses require an actual stored-party-state audit and a subsequent targeted design revision. Never remove beneficial Haste/guard, field-based Silence, physical forced movement, hazards, boss warnings or the enemy's Dust Cover. Water tap chooses one eligible recipient: Ogre first if affected, otherwise nearest living affected active companion, deterministic ID tie-break. If no hostile is in legal reach but a recipient has Slow, tap performs a legitimate cleanse-only action. Charged Water affects every living active recipient inside its actual local wash path/radius; it can clear Slow for several companions but is not worth pretending it clears multiple distinct statuses if only Slow is authored. If no valid enemy *and* no eligible recipient exists, fail without full cooldown. A Trace is created only after real offensive contact, at most **one active** trace per Ogre; it neither washes allies by itself nor grants Fire source access.

**Mineral.** Tap places one short plate in a legal reachable nearby worksite, oriented to an explicitly previewed lane; no free construction damage. Charge replaces it with two short plates and a visible opening. Treat a charged two-plate arrangement as **one owned construct** and cap total interceptions at two for the arrangement, not two per plate. Only actual directed, traveling projectiles whose source/trajectory and collision receipt can be established are eligible. Both hostile and friendly shots collide from either side, consuming one interception; no selective team immunity. Do **not** block ground circles/lanes, instant beams, melee, stationary traps, ground navigation or ally movement. Expiry, destruction, zone travel and Ogre death clear the construct, with no permanent collision map mutation. Placement must not auto-track threats after being committed. A preview should expose the likely crossing direction, remaining charges and the possibility of obstructing one's own Archer. If native projectile interception is not viable, this specific candidate returns to design, rather than silently becoming a ward.

**Fire.** Tap requires a real Fire source and a reachable close enemy, deals one modest instant contact event, and creates a **target-bound** heat seam: only the next successful Ogre dagger contact on **that same living target** can cause the extra instant rupture before expiry. There is no transferable global attack-power buff. Charged Fire is a single interruptible, two-beat compact action; with no artifact, it prepares at most two subsequent **same-target** dagger contacts, never two additional autonomous attacks. With an eligible owned Water Trace or Mineral construct, preview exactly which **one** artifact will be consumed at release and replace the contact preparation with a short, physically located steam-path or fragment reaction. Reaction must follow the finite actual trace or construct position and be bounded to reachable targets; no new natural water, wind, fire or stone is created. Stable priority: selected target intersecting a Trace; otherwise closest legal artifact by distance then creation ID. Both absent: use the ordinary Fire maneuver. Expiring/invalidating an artifact during charge must not spend an old receipt or choose a hidden second one; show the fallback before commit or reject cleanly.

**Control/readability.** Water shows affected eligible ally count, Mineral shows plate-facing/remaining interception charges, and Fire shows the same-target seam or specifically selected artifact. On phone, these are lightweight transient highlights, not extra permanent targeting controls. Source-unavailable, invalid-placement, no-target, interrupted-charge, cooldown and skill-locked messages must be distinct, short and translatable. No graphic may promise protection/cleansing that the actual rule did not grant.

**Candidate tuning remains explicitly unapproved.** The original 2/8s Water, 3/9s Mineral and 2.5/7s Fire cooldowns, ~140–150 source radius, 90 basic reach, <=105 offensive reach, 3–5s artifact life and two-shot plate budget are *initial test knobs*, not passed balance. Do not change original heroes or enemies to fit them.

## Phase 3 — dagger rhythm, Acid, Reversal, Dodge and retaliation

**Daggers:** Quick, relatively low per-hit dual-dagger basics at **90-world-unit** melee reach. Every **valid actual third automatic contact** spends **exactly one Acid Bottle when shared stock contains one** and performs a strong **compact-area acid splash**. Without stock, third beat is an ordinary dagger hit, not a skipped hit or free infinite acid. One spend per real finisher, **never** on canceled/missed/duplicated callbacks. No reserve toggle, threshold or elite-only exception. Acid skills later share the pool, but **none is secretly granted at Stage 1**. A **finite 48-bottle trial quantity** is a *local proposal only*, not approved or proven enough for a full campaign; there is **no temporary free regeneration or fake Lab**.

**Manual Reversal:** Separate contextual input, not Skill 1 nor a ninth learned skill. Require the Ogre to **physically enter the true future impact footprint** of a **source-verified reversible live warning** and activate before that specific native release. A success protects **all hero/companions actually affected by that one release**, removing its HP damage and causally attached debuffs, and redirects source-native attack force at its attacker **only where legitimate**. A **status-only reversed release** only cancels its affected statuses; never conjure reflected damage/stagger. Do not cancel an entire boss phase, an unrelated warning, a later volley, an already persistent hazard or unverified moving projectile. No artificial consecutive-success quota. Clear invalid/no-target/outside/late/replaced/duplicate feedback, no fake reflected damage or full ability cooldown on invalid use. First alpha should include **at least one real early Greenwood reversible warning** (Thornfang Tripwire is *only a candidate pending native proof*) and a **second independently validated attack geometry**; #696's Mine and Archive narrow proof does not itself establish early playability or general eligibility.

**Passive Dodge:** Modest **Strategist-only** chance on real eligible hostile hit receipts; protects the Ogre alone and avoids attached on-hit conditions. Provisional **5%**. Not a group ward, teleport, or universal hazard dodge; no spillover to other classes or enemies; honest Ogre-only Miss feedback and one deterministic roll per hit.

**Passive retaliation:** Distinct provisional **10% chance**, evaluated once on an **enemy close melee basic directed at the Ogre**, potentially even if Dodge succeeds. One bounded dagger response if the attacker remains a valid close target. Does **not** trigger for ranged basics, telegraphs, spells, persistent hazards or recursively from another retaliation.

**Starting statistic candidate, not approved:** HP **135**, Power **17**, Armor **9**, speed **290**, natural +25 HP/level; possible +6 movement speed/level (not automatically accepted); proposed purchased discipline ranks Power +6, Mana +2, HP +35, speed +37. No new active mana resource, free global cooldown reduction, generic damage buff, or change to another class. Compare at matched equipment/level/training under Normal/Nightmare and solo/party before claiming balance.

### Phase 3 review — single-event combat ownership (v1.1)

**Dagger chain definition.** Count only committed, valid, actually landed ordinary Ogre basic contacts toward the 1→2→3 rhythm. A canceled wind-up, target disappearing, physical miss, legitimate Dodge/Skill Block or immune contact does not invent a successful third hit or spend a bottle. A third contact with stock spends **exactly one** bottle once and delivers one compact friendly-safe splash with its own target set; extra enemies reached by the splash do not consume more bottles or increment the chain. The no-stock third beat is an ordinary dagger and still resets the successful-contact rhythm to beat one. The timing window/reset cadence and base hit strength are balance parameters for engineering measurement, not a fourth free acid attack. Retaliation attacks, Fire's two internal beats, primed contact ruptures and per-projectile/area hit callbacks do not independently advance the autoattack rhythm or roll extra native criticals.

**Shared Acid ledger.** The Stage 1 starter bottles are finite; no replenishing Lab, hidden regen, hidden safety reserve, reserved override or per-target cost multiplication. Each valid third beat always consumes one bottle if any remain, even against a weak foe. The proposed **48** bottles are a trial allocation requiring a source-grounded *time-to-first-legitimate-spare-worker* and attack-throughput comparison; they are not an owner-approved campaign economic guarantee. Show real stock and a clear transition to the ordinary beat-three animation when empty.

**Hit-event priority.** For one enemy attack instance: a legitimate, timely manual **Reversal** can suppress that instance's damage and causally attached debuffs for all originally affected party victims **before** individual defense; other concurrent/next attacks still resolve. Otherwise each victim independently resolves existing defenses/Skill Block as authored; only an actually eligible **Ogre** hit gets one proposed Dodge roll, before its owned HP/status receipt is committed. On a real Dodge, cancel both the Ogre's HP and inseparable on-hit conditions, with one Ogre-only “Miss” cue. Do not treat walking outside a warning, current immunity or preexisting Blocked! as Dodge. The enemy melee-basic **retaliation opportunity is separate** from Ogre damage outcome, can coexist with a valid Dodge, but occurs only once for an actually committed hostile melee basic directed at the Ogre and only if the source remains a legitimate adjacent target. It cannot recursively retaliate, grant Acid, or trigger on warnings/spells/projectiles. Exact relative priority with any pending Skill Block integration requires later engineering trace; this document does not rewrite Skill Block.

**Reversal receipt.** Bind command to an actual active warning's **unique attack release ID**, attacker, native footprint, future deadline and one-use consumption state. Check that the Ogre deliberately occupies a truly threatened portion of the released attack's footprint at the designated command moment, rather than relying on painted warning art; do not grant global target immunity or redirect a missed/irrelevant warning. The affected party is the actual victim set of *that same release*, wherever in its native attack geometry they stand. Status-only attacks produce cancellation **without reflected damage or invented stagger**. After a valid counter, duplicate presses, late taps, leaving the zone, dying, reloading or warning replacement must not reuse the receipt. An invalid attempt never commits a full ability cooldown. No encounter-wide success cap, forced chip damage or artificial immunity loss.

**Battle sequence check.** A source-poor goblin skirmish uses normal dagger hits and optional one-bottle third-contact finishers. A later shore fight gives Water a legal Slow cleanse and a temporary trace; a truly adjacent active fire is still required for a steam reaction. A telegraphed boss strike can be reversed only by the player actually entering *that* warned footprint; a simultaneous unreversed hit or persistent hazard remains dangerous. All four loops are viable without granting a new general-purpose protective status.

### Phase 4 review — player-visible Stage 1 test script (v1.1)

1. **Character creation:** Select the established Ogre hero variant; show Soldier + Archer, Expedition Rank 1, learned Water/Mineral/Fire, ordinary martial training access and the dedicated Reversal button. Show ranks 4–8 as future **locked paid Neri lessons**, not clickable ghost abilities. Never fire Skill 2's unrelated companion-training reward simply because the Ogre starts with all three ranks.
2. **Dry first encounter:** Begin with actual available terrain, even if no valid source is within reach. Teach the 90-unit dual-dagger approach, three successful-contact rhythm, finite Acid stock, companion behavior, Dodge/retaliation feedback, and visibly honest unavailable source chips. Do **not** put fake water or campfires on the map to improve the tutorial.
3. **Water opportunity:** At the earliest source-authenticated reachable shore/wet site, show source eligibility. When a genuine enemy-applied Slow reaches a living party member, demonstrate Water tap single-recipient cleansing and charged group cleansing if several recipients qualify. If Slow cannot honestly be encountered there, offer a **later natural encounter** and mark this witness pending, never script an inauthentic debuff.
4. **Mineral opportunity:** At a legitimate rock/ore site, preview the plate angle and demonstrate one authentic enemy shot intercepted; separately show a bad-angle case where the existing Archer's own valid shot can be caught. 5. **Fire opportunity:** At an actual active fire/heat site, demonstrate same-target thermal seam and subsequent dagger contact. If Water Trace or plate also happens to be legal nearby, demonstrate one finite artifact-consuming reaction. Do not require the coincidence to complete the first ordinary combat lesson; the environmental combination is an advanced optional witness.
6. **Reversal opportunity:** Provide **one genuinely early available native warning** (Greenwood preferred, but Thornfang Tripwire unproven), plus a second independently verified shape. Teach stepping into the actual telegraph and pressing Reversal before the native release. Show a late/outside failure and a multi-victim protected success without erasing independent warnings. Do not promise the encounter until engineering identifies its actual timeline and owner.
7. **Exit and reload:** Save, quit/reopen, return to the same class, party, Acid stock, learned ranks and honest source availability. Expired traces, plates, old warning receipts and queued reactions do not persist; v4 legacy four-class saves remain intact. Test controlled death/recovery and one source-poor indoor encounter.
8. **Alpha boundary visible to testers:** Field Labs, windmills, material substitutes, Wood/Acid advanced techniques, individual post-Neri lessons, Succession Acid throws and new narrative companions are **not in this alpha**. Neri may remain in the world for existing classes; Strategist-only later lessons must not be falsely purchasable.

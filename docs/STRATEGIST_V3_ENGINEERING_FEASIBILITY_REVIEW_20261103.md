## A. Verified native architecture and decisive integration gaps

1. **The fifth class is not admitted.** [`engine.js` lines 14–45, 85–230](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/engine.js#L14-L45) has foundations only for Paladin/Mage/Ranger/Rogue, and `Campaign` admits only `R.balance.classes`. [`hero-combat.js` lines 5–23 and 1785–1810](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L5-L23) guards new classes from falling into Ranger/Mage/Paladin combat. [`save.js` lines 211–220](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/save.js#L211-L220) rejects unknown hero classes. **None of the sixteen abilities is playable.** Skill 1's charged cooldown lookup is class-indexed ([`hero-combat.js` 674–690](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L674-L690)); [`rules.js` 6344–6370](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/rules.js#L6344-L6370) lacks the Ogre entry. An admitted Ogre requires its own finite cooldowns and explicit actions, not a fallback.
2. **Starting with Skills 1–3 is not a cosmetic change.** [`progression.js` 284–324](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/progression.js#L284-L324) unconditionally treats learning slot 2 as companion-combat training; [`save.js` 430–451 and 510–533](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/save.js#L510-L533) also promotes companion training on restored saves with learned slot 2. This **must be class-specific**, or a fresh Ogre would obtain unearned Soldier/Archer ability training. The v4 save can retain eight skill/cooldown positions, but Neri purchases, shared Acid inventory, authenticated recipes and lifecycle need independent validated ownership.
3. **The existing 1→2→3 basic combo is attempt-based, NOT a proof of three successful contacts.** [`hero-combat.js` 594–610](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L594-L610) advances when called; the Rogue and existing classes call it **before** damage/contact succeeds ([1702–1750](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L1702-L1750), [2745–2785](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L2745-L2785)). Ogre must count **three returned true native ordinary dagger contacts**, on an explicit Ogre-only receipt, with miss, immunity, blocked contact, non-basic skill, off-target invalidity and third-hit bottle transaction handled honestly. No stage may increment on a cast animation or critical VFX.
4. **True flying objects exist, but not a Mineral interceptor.** [`combat.js` 1082–1485](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/combat.js#L1082-L1485) updates hostile and friendly `s.projectiles` by real `before → after` swept paths. Hostile hits evaluate terrain and swept target intersection; friendly arrows and other actual missiles are moved, terrain-checked and applied via `damage`. Friendly Arrow source ownership is established in [`party.js` 1206–1254](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/party.js#L1206-L1254) and [4035–4070](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/party.js#L4035-L4070). A **new** one-face collision resolver must run at the same native trajectory owners *before actual impact*, use first-contact ordering (terrain, face, target), classify physical shots (not every magic projectile), and terminate **one** physical missile from either direction, including a real ally arrow. Current target lookup/order must not be mistaken for guaranteed first physical contact. No Ogre active face = constant-time exit on the projectile path, no scenery/render scan.
5. **Moving-face relative sweep requires information currently absent at projectile-resolution time.** The tick advances hero motion, party AI and enemy AI **before** `updateProjectiles` ([`engine.js` 4427–4658](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/engine.js#L4427-L4658)). Reading Ogre's *final* position when an arrow updates cannot establish whether the arrow crossed a face attached to his *earlier* position during that tick. Correct synchronized relative swept collision needs an explicit prior pose/tick window, locked face orientation and first-time-of-contact ordering. Neither #770 nor #696 supplies it. CPU/accuracy cannot be certified from code inspection.
6. **An ordinary enemy melee-basic release exists, but a weapon swing contact path does not.** [`boss-combat.js` 1300–1356](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/boss-combat.js#L1300-L1356) commits a true ordinary melee basic, emits a `swing` presentation event and synchronously invokes `hitParty`; `melee` is emitted **after** the landed hit. There is no native timed weapon trajectory for the advanced stone counterface. [`hitParty` in `combat.js` 960–1030](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/combat.js#L960-L1030) serves ordinary hits, spells and warnings and is **not** permission to install a global damage-negating flag. [#696's branch-only Reversal proof](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/feat/strategist-warning-reversal-prototype-20261030/docs/STRATEGIST_WARNING_REVERSAL_PROTOTYPE_20261030.md) demonstrates limited special-warning ownership for mine/0 and archive/1, **not** ordinary-melee parry or full-game warnings.
7. **Physical navigation is usable but insufficient to promise exact body positioning.** [`navigation.js` 964–1175](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/navigation.js#L964-L1175) supplies blocked terrain/LOS/swept segment tools; [1338–1360](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/navigation.js#L1338-L1360) advances movement with slide fallbacks. [`hero-combat.js` 61–83](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/hero-combat.js#L61-L83) has a limited existing push with terrain/leash precautions, not a universal shove. [`party.js` 4112–4205](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/party.js#L4112-L4205) runs autonomous judgment, Recall, work and combat orders. Single-unit guided movement therefore needs narrowly owned interruption/resumption, no body phasing, no boss movement and no encounter reset.
8. **Native Slow is removable, but inflicted cooldown debt already exists.** [`combat.js` 650–718](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/combat.js#L650-L718) stores live `slow`, `slowStacks`, possible `rogueSlow` **and separately adds existing cooldown debt**. A Rinsing Cut cure can end an authentic current Slow, not reverse elapsed penalties or cancel unrelated Silence.
9. **The existing input/UI chooses enemy targets, not allies or workpieces.** [`app.js` 6287–6300](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/app.js#L6287-L6300), [6418–6485](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/app.js#L6418-L6485) and [6710–6920](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/a158f6e773f3150076147037056fa68168056090/src/prototype/app.js#L6710-L6920) have fixed enemy-target charge, quick-tap/held/queue/interrupt semantics; there is **no proven two-thumb directional segment drawing, physical-artifact choice or recipient chooser**. A single deterministic eligible recipient and lock-at-release facing are safer candidate interactions than tiny dragged paths or an RTS cursor. A warning must never be masked by skill-aim UI; separate Reversal input remains reserved.
10. **Draft source scanning is authentic but only a dormant component.** [#770's `strategist-materials.js`](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/99e610e852996b67e2253335c4b5efb222c90ffa/src/prototype/strategist-materials.js) uses true outdoor terrain/harbors/drainage, proven prop identities, live fire flags and optics-based `hasSight` independent of movement collision. `forCombat` rejects non-Ogres; `forGather` requires a separately authorized physically traversable worker route. Authored fire may be absent in an ordinary fight, Wood may be absent indoors, painted puddles/plants/industrial heat are not automatically real sources, and a visible river may qualify as Water without permitting movement through it. Field Lab and recipe stock are not built here. [Regional-source reconciliation](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/blob/feat/strategist-material-sources-20261103/docs/STRATEGIST_REGIONAL_SOURCE_RECONCILIATION_20261103.md) documents actual asset/source distinctions; it does not prove joint spawned source density at every encounter. **Source-candidate indexing by prop center with a bounded nearby-cell margin deserves review for larger true source shapes; do not claim a missing-material bug without a generated-zone witness.**

Bank the Spark requires explicit native ordinary-basic tagging before the eventual actual contact, not attachment to a generic `projectileImpact` effect after damage. A shot blocked by the Ogre's own Mineral face must produce no heat contact.

## C. Cross-form interaction, atomicity and scarcity

**Future event/data ownership sketch (design only):**

- Each **manual skill tap/hold** validates current active zone, learned rank/Neri unlock, source or exactly one qualifying substitute, CD, target, material geometry, alive actor, stock and artifact **immediately at actual release**, then claims a distinct native action ID and its one critical decision. A held button must not reserve or create a phantom bottle: an automatic third-beat basic during the hold wins stock in normal tick order; the manual Acid release must recheck remaining stock and fail cleanly if none. A currently active single Mineral face participates in native projectile movement with registration removed synchronously on first true stop or any authorized consumption. Rendering illustrates the live state but does not own contact. No growing collision-object list.

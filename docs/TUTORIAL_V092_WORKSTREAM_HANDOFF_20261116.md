## Owner decisions and what has been implemented

The owner approved three genuine tutorial chapters, **not** a new global quest framework:

1. **Your First Company** (`quest-barracks`): Captain → class-legal first living companion (Rogue earns her own authenticated Charm, never a free conventional recruit) → genuine Recall → optional Captain field/build guidance → the **existing first free Basic Barracks** finished by legitimate labor. Independent early construction stays legal. No new quest IDs replace the original one.
2. **Quest Board / navigation** (`quest-tutorial-board`): activate after Chapter I; discover the real Greenwood Board, inspect Main Story versus Side Quests, track/navigate using the native Map, and use the **existing Old Orchard Cellars** book *The Art of Misdirection / El arte del engaño*. Its one-time native Codex grants +1 **percentage point** critical chance, not a second reward. A prior Codex and earlier Board visit are recognized. The Cellars already have six Goblins and traps: **earned beginner accessibility remains a design-acceptance question**, not something a fixture alone can certify. Never fabricate an easier dungeon or overwrite canon.
3. **First-use local gathering** (`quest-tutorial-gather`): contextual when the real completed Barracks Operations gathering service becomes usable at **Expedition Rank 2** (Mira/training prerequisite). Native idle workers including authenticated Rogue Charmed allies recover finite local Dark Lord Tribute, and real Town Supplies/Full Barracks/refuge deposits yield crowns. Opening a locked menu or a stale `gathered` counter does *not* prove completed instruction or recovered tribute. Full Barracks remote Resource Expeditions are a different feature and receive true EN/ES descriptions, **not** a fourth tutorial.

Truthful Captain / Board exclamation cues follow tutorial state, not roster size or permanent first-visit discovery. Passive basics/splash/worker attacks must not awaken unengaged captains/bosses, while explicit Skill 1 initiation, already engaged elites, ordinary enemies, and authentic class skills remain legal. The candidate preserves v4 save identities, four classes, established lore, original 30 regional quest identities, actual worker/reward rules, and English/Spanish text. Game-wide character presentation, name/pronoun consistency, rescued specialist names and Rogue-specific Keeper Archive Skill descriptions were also reconciled on this draft.

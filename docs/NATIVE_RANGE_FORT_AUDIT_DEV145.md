# Native enemy reach, cover and fort audit · dev.145, rechecked on dev.146

Joel approved this bounded follow-up after the specialist Nightmare test upkeep in #383. **Two gameplay defects are confirmed:** outdoor Frontier recovery invokes enclosed-dungeon cleanup, and fortified Veyra canister fragments damage beyond their rendered warning. This is an audit result, with executable reproductions and proposed repair boundaries. Gameplay repairs have not been implemented or published by this audit. During the review, Joel’s separate Thornfang patrol update was published as dev.146. Its exact source bytes were integrated without reverting that work; all six selected original suites and four probe scripts were rerun, with the same findings and outcomes.

## Native controls that passed

- Seven actual ranged residents, one per selected location: Frontier's Orc road fort, Abyss, Citadel and all four Treasuries. Their real AI starts aiming, launches and damages through its own fort walls; six of those lanes are beyond the actor's ordinary unfortified range. The March Treasury case is an actual Mireling captain at 240 units, inside ordinary range, and is counted only as a wall-permission case.
- Those seven lanes still block movement and friendly sight. An actual wall belonging to the other Frontier fort remains solid to the selected shooter's cover permissions. Outer map boundaries remain solid.
- All five authored low screens in the unfortified Crypt and Archive admit hostile firing geometry while blocking movement and friendly sight. Mine has no authored low screen and receives no invented test count. None of those three main dungeons receives a fort bonus.
- At native Thornfang's home, a target at the expanded 210-unit cone fringe is warned and damaged; a target 10 units beyond its 227.7-unit radius is safe. Lifebinder's three real delayed volley members retain a 780-unit nominal span and no fort grant after launch preparation. The latter is a launch-contract control, not an unobstructed 780-unit native flight claim.

The lane probe does not move the chosen enemies, clear scenery or suppress recovery. Low-screen checks are geometry crossings of actual partitions rather than fabricated obstacle props or full combat pilots. Selected sample counts do not certify every encounter/skill/withdrawal combination.

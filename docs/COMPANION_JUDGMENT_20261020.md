# Companion judgment and regrouping — dev.106

Joel requested companions use the same practical caution as enemies: consider the positions of their own allies, avoid isolated charges into larger forces, and regroup without learning new Rogue skills.

The party owner assesses local support and danger every 0.4 seconds, with rules in `R.companionJudgment`. Only living nearby companions with no labor assignment and a clear physical connection count as immediate support; distant, inactive, dead, gathering and wall-separated units do not inflate confidence. Wounded allies contribute less. Bosses, captains and ringleaders weigh more than regular monsters. Assessments include enemies around the target and the companion.

Unsupported long pursuits, unfavorable local force ratios and wounded exposure trigger a regrouping movement toward the hero's formation or a nearby healthier ally. The group prefers lower-pressure anchors and attempts a hostile-aware physical return route. If an enclosing crowd prevents a fully clear route, a bounded fallback uses ordinary collision-aware movement toward support. There is no teleport, invulnerability, heal, outgoing damage bonus or new learned/active skill. Active retreat receives the same 50% incoming-damage reduction as Recall, ending when the unit reaches its regroup anchor. Local existing basics can cover a withdrawal; companions do not chase from that defensive state.

Healthy companions still assist when the hero has committed nearby. A cautious unit requires roughly 1.4 seconds of favorable observations before rejoining, avoiding repeated charge/retreat switching. Normal BOSS/ADDS selection remains; holding for a distant unsupported add does not authorize shooting the boss instead. Gathering and Recall retain their earlier priority and behavior. Runtime-only judgments clear on Recall, rest/redeployment, recovery and zone travel; save/restore cannot retain permanent panic or new skill state.

Focused verification: `tools/visual-review/companion-judgment-dev106/check.cjs` exercises real party updates for isolated Soldier/Archer and melee/ranged pets, supported commitment, local versus distant/labor/wall-separated support, wounded retreat, unsupported boss engagement, regroup/rejoin, cover basics and Recall/save/travel. Existing squad-doctrine checks retain BOSS/ADDS behavior while replacing the obsolete expectation that troops must chase a distant unsupported summon. Doctrine assertions isolate their manually edited encounter states; the separate simulation covers regroup hysteresis. The dev.105 gathering loop and travel-collision checks pass. Build/generated source and changed-source formatting pass before package-only publication.

These are focused headless simulations, not a whole-game/browser matrix or measured live survival rates. Owner play remains the check for tuning in actual crowded fights. Existing Frontier/Abyss geometry, field-boss recovery, resource-job persistence, accepted art and attack tuning are preserved.


## One-press Recall review

Joel clarified that Recall must cancel current activity and reliably bring every companion to the player from one press, with retreat defense. The review found that an explicit offensive hero skill could cancel Recall while units were still far away; repeated Recall calls also cleared their navigation again. Normal formations could leave ranged pets too far from the hero to count as arrival.

Recall now cancels labor, casts and tactical target intent once, returns all living active companions to a compact formation within 100 world units, and remains active through hero offense/doctrine switches until everyone arrives. Repeated presses are idempotent, preserving the current route. The hero’s existing movement destination is preserved while queued attack approaches are cancelled. Forced enemy holds still obey their normal release mechanics. After arrival, the ordinary defensive hold remains until a new combat order; Recall from a resource job resumes normal behavior after the group reaches the player, as requested earlier. Explicit new labor orders and zone travel remain new assignments.

A returning party member takes 50% less incoming damage. This uses the same defensive layer as wards, applying the stronger protection rather than multiplying reductions. It ends on arrival; the hero and non-party escorts do not receive it. Automatic tactical regrouping gets that protection only while returning toward its anchor. The boolean Recall order now survives save/reload, with validation/default compatibility for old v4 saves; runtime tactical observations remain transient.

`recall-check.cjs` covers one press with all three hero classes, Soldier/Archer/pet, job/cast cancellation, repeat-press path preservation, manual offense and doctrine during return, complete arrival, hold/release, reload persistence, defensive reduction/ward interaction/end and old-save validation. Existing automatic-fire targeting checks also run against the revised contract.

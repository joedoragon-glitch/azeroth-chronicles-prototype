## 1. Status legend and revision precedence

- **Settled owner direction:** female, guitar-carrying, boldly playful Rogue Bard; **lowest established starting class HP, Power and movement speed**; does not receive bonus ordinary base attributes from innate passive; starts with **zero** Soldier/Archer companions and cannot hire new ordinary troops; **one more active companion slot** than the existing heroes at matching Expedition progress; ordinary basic attacks offer a **single primary-target chance to Charm legal regular monsters** that become persistent, identity-preserving, recoverable company with friendly companion AI; captains, Ringleaders and bosses cannot be Charmed; existing charmed monsters remain owned across **Succession**, even by non-Rogue successors (which cannot charm more); boss pets remain recruitable; 2-second **1-HP Endure** concept; static visual assets only, no animation-system rewrite; musical repertoire names below **accepted**.
- **Further owner-directed design:** all **16 manual normal/charged forms** are reasonable **combat-only AoE performances** whose **same bounded spatial effect** hurts/debilitates enemies and gives helpful **conceptually opposite** effects to active nearby companions; different music boons can overlap; same-family repeats must not grow without bounds. Add a **fourth independently adjustable Level 1–3 *Rogue* critical intensity** category (default Level 1) separate from the old Hero / Companion / World settings. The Rogue should derive identity from frequent effective personal crits; **+2 percentage points per hero level** is the *current favored owner idea to evaluate*, superseding the earlier draft's +1pp as the working numeric candidate, **not yet final proven balance**.
- **Newest settled owner direction — Charm company and Barracks:** Rogue's Expedition active cap at ranks 1–6 is **3/4/4/5/6/7**. That same cap is her **maximum total owned of each individual eligible monster type**, counting active, resting, fallen and away members together. Different eligible monster types have independent quotas; **there is no overall 12-charmed-monster ownership ceiling**. Barracks construction or upgrade is never required to Charm or retain creatures. Basic Barracks can manage at most **3 active deployments**, Full Barracks up to the actual class/Expedition cap; neither limits ownership of the global resting roster. Full Barracks needs fast type-first, paginated and status-filtered management. [Binding detailed contract](ROGUE_BARD_CHARMED_ROSTER_BARRACKS_CONTRACT_20261027.md). Charm probability remains subject to playtest evidence.
- **Tentative implementation numbers/policies:** all AoE radius/target caps/durations, musical station rather than physical trap placements, family support percentages, which one existing native critical lever each form enhances, 12% primary hit Charm chance, source-stat normalization, dormant MP and Discipline rank increments, 90-second Endure internal cooldown and expiration behavior, static-art palette and specifics. **None** is approved gameplay or proven balanced.
- **Historical superseded ideas:** invented HP100 / Power19 / Armor4 bases; standalone single-target tap variants; arbitrary non-mirrored supporter bonuses (e.g. attack-speed boon for the anti-interrupt song instead of casting protection); physical-only placed traps as mandatory Skill 5; +1pp critical per level as the **current** numeric recommendation; **12 charmed creatures in total** (now superseded by per-individual-monster-type Expedition quotas). These may remain in chronological background for provenance but **must not be used as the implementation spec**.
- **Not done:** no new playable class, crit slider, skills, monsters converted, procedural art, sprite, executed gameplay tests or release in this PR. Focused CI has run on earlier heads but reported failures; current-head verification is still a release gate (see source audit). This draft is **not merge-ready as a functional class**.

## Owner clarification — independent critical intensity and Succession inheritance (27 October)

**Critical-intensity controls: exactly four distinct groups after Rogue implementation**, each independently selectable at Levels 1–3: **Hero** = Paladin, Mage and Ranger; **Rogue** = the Rogue Bard hero alone; **Companion** = all companions including inherited Soldier/Ranger, boss pets and captured creatures; **World** = enemy and boss actions. The current native source has only Hero/Companion/World; the fourth Rogue group and its user-visible control remain **not implemented**. The Rogue's intensity is separate from critical-hit **probability**; all friendly critical rolls still belong to the one established Party domain, World rolls to World. No third stream and no change to other characters' intensity.

**Newly settled Succession exception:** Beginning a fresh campaign as Rogue grants **zero Soldiers/Archers** and Rogue **cannot hire** either ordinary type at a Captain or Barracks. But a Rogue who becomes the hero through Succession **inherits every existing Soldier and Archer/Ranger**, as well as pets and lawful charmed creatures, without losing names, levels, health/fallen status, roster membership or saved identity. She may command those inherited units in her party, rest/recover them and redeploy resting ones from a completed Barracks under its Basic/Full and Expedition caps. This is *ownership inherited from an earlier class*, not a new recruitment permission. A Rogue never loses her ability to Charm because she has zero starters; Charm does not require a Barracks. The owner specifically welcomes mixed inherited conventional troops, pets and captured monsters. Ordinary recruitment remains blocked even if inherited units die; it is never a means to replace them.

The existing Succession runtime keeps the global party rather than spawning fresh starter troops, and the barracks Add/Rest services are type-agnostic for these inherited units. Tests now probe an inherited deployed Soldier/Archer, an inherited reserve Archer, redeployment, identity preservation, no-hire and save validation using temporary future-class registration **in the test only**. The current draft still does **not** add a playable Rogue or authenticated Charm.

## 2. Verified baseline — avoid the invented-stat problem

Actual live `src/prototype/rules.js::balance.classes`: **Paladin 120 HP / 18 Power / 8 Armor / 300 Speed**; **Mage 90 / 22 / 3 / 300**; **Ranger 105 / 20 / 5 / 320**. Mage **+9 Power is purchased *per Discipline rank***; it is **not** Mage's base Power. Working Rogue starting line **90 HP / 18 Power / 3 Armor / 300 Speed**, before gear/training, uses actual class minima. Dormant MP **60** and Discipline rank increments **+7 Power / +2 Mana / +27 HP / +38 Speed** also borrow existing minima as *proposals*. Preserve natural +25 HP per later level, existing equipment, regular talent ceilings and XP.

**Balance danger:** unlike all three current classes, Rogue has **no Soldier + Archer to start**. Minimum base stats plus 12% hypothetical Charm on a legal *surviving primary auto hit* may cause miserable early survival or many kills before acquiring the first ally. This is a **mandatory measured onboarding check**, not a reason to secretly increase her Power, movement or HP.

**Basic auto** is distinct from the eight manual combat-only songs: proposed short **260-range musical fan**, original primary 0.80×(Power+12), **at most 2 secondary targets** each receiving 25% of that original primary, 0.85s base cadence. At Power18 that is **24 primary + up to 6 per splash** before defense/combo. Only the **one designated legal surviving primary** may roll Charm, never splashes, crit VFX, DoT, fields, or the wider third-hit finisher. One auto critical action release, one receipt. Third basic has a bounded wider fan, maximum **5** contacts, no extra Charm.

## 3. Critical design: four dials but still two RNG domains

Merged [#440 critical kernel](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/440) supports three independently configured intensity groups today, Hero/Companion/World, each Level 1/2/3 with **Level 1 default**. **The fourth dedicated Rogue dial does NOT exist yet**. Future implementation adds `rogue` to critical levels groups/defaults, registers **17 Rogue actions** (`hero/rogue/auto` + eight tap/charged pairs) with this group, changes no preexisting class's group, and offers the **actual independent adjustment UI with validated persistence/default-on-old-saves**. `critical-party-native.js` already looks up the registered group and can still roll using the **existing Party RNG stream**. Enemy World stream remains independent; no new third RNG stream or extra critical roll.

**Chance is separate from intensity.** Current recommendation for Rogue hero-source chance only: `10% base + (2 percentage points × current hero level) + up to 5 percentage points from earned Codices`, counted beginning **at level 1** and derived from class/level, not saved as a permanent incremental stat. Illustrations: L1 **12%** (17% with five Codices), L15 **40%** (45%), L18 **46%** (51%). The Rogue's increase is **personal only**, not a free permanent boost to charmed monsters or pets. One later bounded Skill 8 ally boon *may temporarily* boost companion crit frequency without touching their separate **Companion** intensity Level. Other heroes' chance rules remain unchanged.

**One critical event enhances exactly one original outcome per legal action** at selected intensity; do not multiply every enemy debuff *and* every ally boon. PR #440 native Level 1/2/3 levers, among others, are damage ×1.08/1.20/1.40; heal budget ×1.10/1.25/1.45; Slow/Weaken duration +0.20/0.50/1.00 seconds; cooldown debt +0.20/0.50/0.90 seconds; Silence +0.15/0.35/0.65 seconds; Haste duration +0.30/0.75/1.40 seconds; push ×1.08/1.18/1.30; ward strength +1.5/+3.5/+6.0 percentage points. A cast shares its single frozen critical receipt across its enemies, allies and delayed original pulses. **No reroll at every target or tick; crit never improves the Charm probability.**

## 5. Overlap, combat admission, existing mechanics and safety

- **Positioning matters.** A manual skill requires a live legal hostile engagement and reachable location. Both sides of its **same** area are resolved with real geometry/LOS/encounter boundaries. Allies may receive a normal boon even if a hostile is immune to its corresponding debuff; a failed/queued/canceled/out-of-combat cast gives neither benefit nor critical roll. Non-station songs snapshot in-range legal allies at release; stations have **ongoing presence** only inside their bounded fields and expire on leaving or combat end, so leaving does not preserve a free long-duration buff.
- **Different families may overlap** (up to eight named families if the player times them). **Same family** has at most one active instance per companion, strongest legal magnitude and finite expiry, **no stacking repeated healing budgets**. Movement duplicates native Ranger Rally by maximum relevant component rather than multiplication; Skill 2 and 6 additive cooldown recovery have a **proposed total +25% Rogue-source cap**, integrated once into the native cooldown clock, not twice each frame. Allied damage boons are type-specific and proposed Rogue aggregate capped, not permanent `u.damage` mutations.
- **Source proof:** `combat.js::recordPartyChargeDamage` has **real damage-threshold charge interruption**, `party.js::resolveCompanionCharges` has cast admission, while `hero-combat.js::mageSilenceActive` currently checks **the hero Mage's own silence field**, *not* a proven enemy-to-companion Silence source. Skill 3's **baseline protected charged casting is implementable in principle** if narrowed to actual damage interruption. Adding an immunity against *hostile Silence* remains a **conditional desired effect** requiring its actual enemy ownership path and tests; do not fake universal Silence immunity, ignore death/Recall, or let silenced actors retroactively cast. The protected companion **still takes actual HP damage**.
- **Source proof:** `party.js::addSupportEffect/updateRangerSupport` already handles fixed healing budgets, but **no generic received-healing multiplier** exists; Skill 4 needs a bounded real healing-recipient hook avoiding self-multiplication or extra ticks. `combat.js::cooldownRecoveryElapsed` already handles Slow; adding ally cooldown acceleration must preserve its original Slow penalty and apply once. `combat.js::hitParty` has a max-based ward layer; new station defensive reduction must not stack multiplicatively with an existing ward. Spell mitigation/status against named captains/bosses must preserve original target immunities and cooldown limits.
- **Bounded performance:** no new dance sprites, animated poses, extra summoning AI, ambient audio loops, per-frame actor clouds, reserve AI, or renderer overhaul. Fixed-size per-ally song status records and ≤2 fields. The current static hero and crowned/Dark Lord candidate art remain separate, still unapproved appearance proposals.
- **Saves:** historical version-4 records must load; active party cap +1, seventh non-overlapping slot, four Succession class validation, converted monster identity and recovery need targeted changes. Combat boons/music fields should be strictly temporary and cleared on appropriate save/restart/zone/death boundaries, as existing Ranger Rally is; the **Rogue slider** is a *setting*, distinct from temporary combat statuses, and needs a validated default/persistent storage design. Inherited charmed monsters stay owned; successor non-Rogue never gains new Charm.
- **User-facing UX:** show meaningful short song boon/icon durations without flooding the HUD; touch controls and mobile readability. Keep bilingual names/skill descriptions and offline GitHub Pages/PWA files and cache graph. Multi-file game architecture is explicitly supported; do not reintroduce the obsolete single-HTML constraint.

## 6. Actual balance and acceptance gates before approval to implement or merge

**First-capture feasibility** (≥20 repeatable early seeds, enemy HP and real primary survival hits, death rate, time/quests to first ally); **late Rogue critical frequency** L1/L5/L10/L15/L18 with 0/5 Codices; independent four sliders at Level 1, 2, 3 across Normal/Nightmare/Developer; **all 16 legal manual actions × three intensity tiers**; one frozen critical receipt across split ally/enemy AoE; Skill 4 HoT + incoming heal received; charged interruption protection without global immunity; Skill 5 stationary entry/exit, caps and combat shutdown; Slow and accelerated cooldown one-step arithmetic; cross-family buff overlap with Ranger Rally; crowd and boss protection (including enemy healing, summons, TRUE forms and silence lockouts); 0–7 active friendly AI; original pet skills; permanent charmed ownership across four-class Succession, falling/recovering and v4 saves; Canvas 2D and experimental Pixi both stable; phone and offline PWA smoke; tests/benchmarks and source-exact CI **only after a future approval to implement**.

**Do not confuse a readable design with a balance pass**. Simultaneous crit frequency approaching 51%, many overlapping party buffs and 7 active companions could be **too powerful late-game**, while low-level zero-companion RNG could be **too weak**. Compare matched levels, gear, Codices and both equal-party-size and permitted +1-party-size conditions. Tune finite buff/Charm numbers *before* increasing ordinary hero stats.

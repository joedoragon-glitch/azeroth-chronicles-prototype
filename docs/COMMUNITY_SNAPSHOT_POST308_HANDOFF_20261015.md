## Suggested engineering decisions (review against exact merged source)

**Pet collection versus hire:** Unlock is permanent, account-independent *within the campaign save*, at first TRUE win. Treat availability as an entitlement calculated from existing `s.true`/victories. A pet can then be hired/activated through the Barracks; preserve a clear distinction between *unlocked*, *recruited*, *active*, *resting* and *fallen*. Decide whether repeated pet recruitment is appropriate **before** implementing: a unique named boss pet per boss is the less exploitable default. Do not charge a retroactive unlock cost or erase an existing companion to make room; use current reserve/group management.

**Kit complexity:** Begin with two distinctive pet abilities modeled on non-summon source moves, not entire boss arsenals. Share existing companion cooldown/charge, damage, status, projectile, event and rendering contracts when truly appropriate. Give each pet a coherent tactical niche within the Soldier/Archer power band; avoid boss-strength effects or hostiles' arena-wide hazard/summon controllers. Allow nuanced choices without requiring 11 wholly separate AI subsystems.

**Visual quality rubric:** inventory *all* still-procedural enemy families, variants, structure/entrance families and repeated region-specific props; record current code owner/actual draw entry, location, canon cues, visual issue, expected improvement, gameplay-size readability and preserve/upgrade/no-change verdict. Prioritize (a) player-visible ugly or incoherent enemies, (b) prominent architecture/entrances, (c) frequently repeated weak props, (d) lesser background scenery. Existing deliberately simple combat cues can be left alone. Pet visuals get explicit front/side silhouette and grounding checks.

**Comparison contexts:** same five regions, settlements and key dungeons; both desktop/phone; normal/night lighting; visual scale and occlusion; existing illustrated entities mixed with procedural entities; reduced motion; normal/TRUE enemies. Keep collision, entity order, targeting circles and legibility equivalent.

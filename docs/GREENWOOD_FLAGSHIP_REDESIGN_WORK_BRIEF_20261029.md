# Greenwood Vale flagship — environmental redesign: Work restart commission

**29 October 2026 · New owner-authorized design and implementation commission · Status: planning handoff; no redesigned code yet**

**Game:** *So I Became the Dark Lord's Problem* (the repository's Azeroth Chronicles name is historical). **Repository:** [joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype). **Epic:** [#545](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/issues/545) and existing stages [#546–551](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/issues/546). **Original Work package:** closed [PR #544](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/544). **Shipped attempt:** [#570](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/570), dev.233, merge [323bb2a7](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/commit/323bb2a75ceec9bafa8dfe9d567be057309a0115), documentation closeout [#637](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/637). Its 56 focused suites and Pages build passed; **the owner's actual iPhone playtest then rejected its artistic/environmental quality**. The release is technically published and remains live unless a separately authorized rollback occurs. Passing code tests does not equal authorial acceptance.

## 1. The central mistake, now expressly corrected

> **The original Greenwood map was a prototype that served early game development. It is NOT the map design to preserve. Preserve the game Greenwood contains. Completely redesign the WORLD in which the game takes place.**

The owner's newly explicit direction supersedes the overly conservative interpretation of the 2026-10-28 Work mandate. A regional rebuild equivalent to Flooded Marches, Ironroot Highlands and Ashen Frontier was commissioned—not a bigger version of the old scattered procedural world with extra props. The old 2,700-unit geometry and the rejected shipped 3,200-unit road/district layout have **no aesthetic authority**. Do **not** make an unchanged map, river, road, bridge, fort, fence, forest or building position a prerequisite. An actual world redesign must be free to reorganize entire landforms, settlement clusters, defensive compounds, industry and outer districts; migrating saved positions is engineering work, not a justification for doing almost nothing.

**Preserve what has meaning in gameplay and story:** canon/identity, all original quests, rewards, progression, existing economic totals, factions and inhabitants, boss and patrol functions, interaction nodes, owned barracks, player construction, treasure and explored/paid state, exact protected dungeon/interior identifiers, the two crossing functions, and compatibility with version-4 saves including progress migrated into dev.233. Keep Scornfang's Treasury distinct from Thornfang/Mira; preserve Goblin-occupied Old Orchard Cellars separately from the decorative working fruit cellar. Do not dilute encounter density or invent new enemies, quest payouts, trade fees or lore.

**Explicitly free to overhaul:** topography, river path and bank artwork, crossings and their locations, ground material and new regional art assets, all shrubs/thickets/forest species and distribution, meaningful map-edge use, roads and frontage hierarchies, town/hamlet/fort parcel layouts, mill/waterworks, farm economy scenes and the arrangement of existing named sites and actors. Their identities survive relocation. Prefer the 3,200 bounds if a compelling full-map plan fits, but measure and justify a better extent if necessary. All such changes need source-grounded one-time save migration and collision/path evidence.

**Environmental identity remains:** mature-summer green temperate woodland and cultivated river valley inspired by the eastern American Piedmont, translated into original medieval fantasy. Use gentle low relief, stratified hardwood forests, sparing dry-rise conifers, meaningful thickets, varied ground, red-brown exposed clay only where worn, agricultural smallholds and populated working spaces, shaded uneasy forest interiors and the unfinished winter estate. Do not copy contemporary American towns, Ironroot's autumn alpine look, Marches coastal wetlands or Frontier's ash.

## 4. Mandatory whole-region redesign, not patches

**A. First DESIGN THE MAP.** Create and compare at least **two substantially different whole-region masterplans** before committing coordinates: geography/basin and watersheds, gentle relief and boundaries, forest/clearing/field zones, useful river/bridge crossings, Millhaven and Orchard Hamlet, industrial waterworks, Goblin/Skeleton territory, Scornfang/Crypt/estate corridor, safe transports and the important perimeter/edge districts. Classify each square/outer band as a purposeful landscape, traveled approach, settlement, encounter territory or deliberately quiet scenic contrast. **No purposeless dead edges**, and do not fill the map with random props to fake density. Preserve safe travel pacing and localized monster danger; avoid crossing the region with endless empty roadway.

**B. REPLACE THE GENERIC NATURE AND GROUND.** Author meaningful mature Piedmont-inspired hardwood types/silhouettes and stable variation, low saplings/bushes, thickets/understory, clumps of shrubs/wildflowers, fallen wood, cultivated margins and riparian growth. Design distinct meadow/pasture, cultivated soil, leaf-litter forest, damp ground, banks and restrained red-clay cart wear. Arrange forests as believable stands, open edges and interior density, not repetitive circular stamps or evenly spaced decorations. Use the [Regional Environment Sprite Scope](REGIONAL_ENVIRONMENT_SPRITE_SCOPE.md), [Terrain Texture Pipeline](TERRAIN_TEXTURE_PIPELINE.md) and current asset masters/manifests. The work is allowed to build **new or revised source-authored procedural art and ground materials**, subject to established sprite lineage, export, fallback, scale and PWA/cache budgets. Retaining sprite IDs never means retaining poor appearance.

**C. REBUILD RIVER, WATER AND CROSSINGS.** Design a naturally varied river with bend, bank, shallows/shoreline vegetation and actual collision/paint equivalence. Keep the functionality, discovery and identity of two crossings but reshape/reposition bridge spans and approaches if necessary: maintained freight/main bridge vs narrower southern footbridge. Make their actual scale and traffic explain their construction. For the western mill, specify the actual upstream water or engineered pond retention, dam/weir crossing its bank, a *visibly wetted* short headrace/flume meeting the wheel, and an intelligible downstream tailrace/outlet, and an accessible dry loading apron. Keep the distinct eastern farm-water source. No fake second pond; no new water physics/economy needed. Measure actual flow/relative elevation depiction and occlusion in 150% views, not a test that only says “sluice is close to pond center.”

**D. RECOMPOSE SETTLEMENTS AND ECONOMY.** Millhaven must have an intentional central working street/crossroads and safe newcomer sightline; align the supplies, Captain, refuge, quest board, homes, public work, well, market, storage, workshops and freight movements with roads, real doors and defensible plots. Reserve whole building fronts/streets BEFORE sprinkling background props. Farm lanes are **narrower and purposeful**, forest tracks faint. Orchard Hamlet should read as a quieter productive village with stage-differentiated fruit trees, irregular real orchard rows, saplings, fruit-drying/storage and a modern decorative working cellar, connected to freight and town. Keep Goblin Old Orchard Cellars as a separate occupied interior. Do not create duplicated generic town rings with a separate shuffle algorithm.

**E. RECOMPOSE OCCUPIED COMPOUNDS AND FOREST SOCIETIES.** The Goblin roadside camp must be a rough but rational inhabited palisade compound: recognizable perimeter/gate, approach/guard observation, practical yard, work/sleep/cooking/store areas, secure enemy homes, fair fight space and relevant access; borrow Frontier's fort **spatial principles**, not its art/faction. Organize Treasury defense, Scornfang's supply staging, bridge traffic, Crypt reclamation and estate work into coherent guarded yards/checkpoints/convoys; do not scatter random palisade pieces that defend nothing. Maintain all Goblin, Skeleton, Thornfang and Scornfang story distinctions, troop roles, side dungeons and rescued identities. More forceful environmental overhaul **does not** authorize new combat systems, lore or rewards.

**F. DESIGN FOR PLAYER SCALE.** Real 150% camera screenshot composition (and phone portrait/landscape plus desktop), discernible hero/sprites, safe interactive frontages, visibility of threats, readable day/night travel, no overfull HUD/scene, reasonable rendering cost. Artwork must survive both actual Pixi/WebGL and Canvas fallback where supported; graphics materials/road caches and PWA publication are first-class engineering responsibilities.

## 5. Save safety and source ownership — never confuse with preserving a map

All current/old v4 campaigns must survive. The previous 2,700-to-3,200 migration has already happened in some live players' saves. Include **both historical 2,700-coordinate fixtures and current dev.233 saves**, plus newer constructed barracks, companion orders, NPC locations, defeated enemies, existing player camps, quest/tribute/discovered/paid flags and separate interregional arrivals. Build versioned, **one-time** layout migration for moved actors, site markers, scenery, enemy homes/patrols and entrances. Guard second/third reload idempotence. Do not overwrite owned construction or invent “safe” relocation that loses a saved object; retain backups and define conservative no-fit treatment. Preserve the exact quest, enemy and reward invariants: 6 Greenwood quests, 32 ordinary outside enemies, finite outdoor tribute 640 crowns, paid/discovered state, all exits, bridges and travel. A preserved source backup is not a reverse save migration.

Treat painted roads as a consequence of deliberate **authored arterial, farm-lane and footpath routing**, not of the same-width global pathfinder. Use the existing pathfinder for verified walkable connections, nav-safe cornering, performance and fallback. All banks, crossing widths, roof footprints, prop clearance, click/tap navigation and AI paths must agree with rendered geometry. Preserve all other regions unless a verified shared fix is unavoidable. Account for current Pixi/WebGL renderer/caching and procedural fallback; do not write tests only for an obsolete rendering mode.

**Stage 3 — settlements, economy and defensive composition:** Rebuild Millhaven/Orchard Hamlet and all functional frontage/industry scenes; compose the mill hydraulic site, Goblin enclosure and Scornfang's Treasury/bridge defenses and larger estate approach. Streets, buildings, doors, fences, props, guards and water belong to **one authored layout** per district, not separate random-placement passes.

**Stage 4 — adversarial self-audit:** Real whole-region and scene screenshots at 150% on supported viewports, four distinct seeded maps, old and shipped migrated saves with repeat reload, hero+companion routes and all interactables, 30–60-minute free-roam and first-ten-minute onboarding, day/night, Canvas/Pixi output and route/performance measurements against baseline. Fix obvious bad scenes **internally**. A green unit suite that checks distances but misses visibly absurd roads is **not** acceptance. Triage unrelated historical CI failures separately with exact baseline/head evidence; retain necessary strict tests.

**Stage 5 — owner-reviewable candidate:** Deliver before/after **visual evidence pack/contact sheet**, actual screenshots from whole region and districts, named test/build receipts on exact PR head, remaining tradeoffs/risks, decisions matrix and documentation updates. Keep the implementation in scoped feature branches/draft PRs until owner approves. **Do not merge or publish a replacement just because engineering checks passed.** Device verification means the user actually checked it on the named hardware; native/browser automation is not device verification.

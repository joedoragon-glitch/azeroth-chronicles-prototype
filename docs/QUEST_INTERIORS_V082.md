# Quest interiors and controls — v0.8.2

The old "Recover 3 crates from the orchard" text was misleading. The three pickups existed, but two were scattered to other Vale landmarks. This update uses the orchard building as an actual entrance. The same one-outdoor, two-indoor structure applies to all four three-pickup quests: Vale orchard watchtower, Marches stranded wagon hold, Highlands quarry storehouse, and Crown foundry storeroom. Frontier has an escort quest and no three-crate objective.

Each room is a separate 900×900 interior with solid cover and two safe passages through its dividing wall. Four permanent guardians defend the two crates; the last is a tougher named captain. They use regional species, guard-tier gold and XP, and the usual combat and ringleader rules. The room has an exit at the entrance and no duplicate quest payment. The first outdoor crate retains its existing guarded site. This keeps the outdoor field and resource mini dungeons relevant while giving the pickup quest its own distinct interior.

Existing saves retain `region:bundle-0/1/2` collection flags and one-time quest payment. On load, previously placed outdoor crates 1 and 2 are removed, the nearby entrance is added once, and already collected indoor crates stay hidden. Living indoor guards and pending guard ringleaders block pickup. Exiting returns beside the entrance. After peace the room is safe and uncollected supplies remain available. `tests/local-sites.test.cjs` checks all four quest paths, reachability, guardian gating, old-save migration and reload.

Quest boards move away from surrounding town buildings on existing and new saves. A gold `!` appears when the board has an unaccepted quest; a green `?` appears when a reward is ready. The HUD and menu panel move to the right, skills remain above the left joystick, and Confirm/Order (F) occupies the lower right. Touching entries in the menu panel does not activate them; joystick movement selects an entry, and F confirms. Every menu, including NPC services and game options, ends with a selectable Back entry. The opening class chooser returns to the challenge choice; closing a successor or game-over menu leaves the frozen state available from the Menu button. Desktop keyboard and mouse interactions remain.

Health and mana potions retain their 35% automatic threshold, health priority, manual shortcuts and shared ten-second cooldown. Their authored restoration values arrive over five active seconds, so use is not an instant rescue from lethal damage. The remaining amount and time persist across reload and stop at full resources; death, rest and level-up clear a running effect. `tests/auto-potions.test.cjs` checks the gradual and saved behavior.

The prior v0.8.1 secret-encounter audit and tests are included. Movement-triggered skill 1 remains a single-target attack for all three classes, with separate per-class switches if balancing later calls for a change.

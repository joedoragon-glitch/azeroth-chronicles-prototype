# Implementation decisions

Design documents 01–08 define the world, economy, combat, ending and Nightmare rules. Documents 09–10 add execution and audio. Document 11 adds Succession; document 12 records verification. Later specific decisions supersede earlier assumptions.

The expanded shell runs one shared Campaign state. `data.js` carries approved content tables, `engine.js` owns progression/combat/saves, `app.js` owns controls/rendering and `audio.js` owns original local music and sound. The legacy implementation remains available independently.

- Succession is optional for a fresh Normal or Nightmare run. Gold, boss facts, prisoners, services, quests, resources, equipment, supplies and party survive. Class, level, XP, learned skills, ranks, discipline-training ranks and temporary buffs reset. Inherited equipment and supplies are an interpretation of retained progress and a playtest tuning point.
- Freed specialists never impose hero-level requirements, including for successors. Rescue access, gold, the instructor curriculum/rank ceiling, equipment tier offered and reforge ownership are the progression gates; advanced services are still not granted automatically.
- Expedition is a six-rank specialist-trained skill. Rank 1 is learned by default. Mira trains through Rank 2, Sela through 3, Orin through 4, Lyss through 5 and Tovan through 6; later rescued instructors may train skipped lower Expedition ranks one step at a time. Expedition training costs 0g but must still be deliberately trained either from the instructor directly or through rescued-specialist access at a barracks. Rank 1 provides the starting Soldier + Archer, Recall, Basic Barracks and an active-group cap of 2. Rank 2 unlocks recruitment, resource labor and cap 3. Rank 3 unlocks manual TARGET/THREATS and BOSS/ADDS switching. Rank 4 unlocks Full Barracks and cap 4. Ranks 5 and 6 raise the active group to 5 and 6.
- A completed Full Barracks is a compact five-choice hub: Expedition Skill, Company, Rescued specialists, Operations, and Inventory & supplies. Recruitment, recovery and active-group composition are grouped under Company; map/objectives/resource labor are grouped under Operations. Specialist-taught inheritance skills stay inside the relevant specialist service instead of becoming additional top-level barracks entries.
- Player-built barracks are the field home of the Expedition system. Before Expedition 4, every newly built Basic Barracks costs 20g; this deliberately makes early placement low-risk. Basic Barracks provide wounded treatment, fallen recovery, rescued-specialist access, group management and, from Expedition 2, recruitment and resource labor. At Expedition 4, an existing Basic Barracks upgrades for exactly 100g with one companion providing labor; new barracks instead cost 120g and are Full immediately. Full Barracks keep the same appearance, support active groups above 3, act as resource deposit points and expose the complete operations/inventory package. No further building tiers exist.
- Hiring and fielding are separate. Town recruitment unlocks at Expedition 2 but stops once the player employs three total companions, counting active, resting and fallen followers; the menu then explicitly directs the player to a barracks. Barracks may continue hiring companions into reserve, with a practical save-safety ceiling far above normal play. Expedition rank limits only the active group traveling with the hero. Barracks group management freely moves employed companions between the active group and resting reserve; Basic Barracks can support at most three active companions, while Full Barracks support the full Expedition cap.
- Squad control is an extension of hero combat, not a separate RTS mode. WASD/joystick always controls the hero; there is no free squad cursor, field unit selection, individual attack/move command, or Hold Fire state. Combat troops use one encounter-scoped binary doctrine: TARGET/THREATS in field fights and BOSS/ADDS in boss fights. Paladin defaults to focus (TARGET/BOSS), while Mage and Ranger default to guard (THREATS/ADDS). Manual doctrine switching appears only from Expedition 3; disengagement restores the class default. Recall is available from Rank 1 as the emergency regroup command. Nearby aggro defines the active encounter even when the hero has not yet landed a hit, so the party defends the hero automatically.
- Ordinary outdoor ranged monsters use a separate regional scaling curve rather than the melee curve. Their HP/damage multipliers are 1.05/1.00, 1.10/1.05, 1.20/1.10, 1.35/1.20 and 1.55/1.35 from Vale through Crown. This deliberately keeps ranged enemies weaker than equivalent melee enemies because range, projectile pressure and retreat are part of their power budget, while preventing the late-game ranged roster from falling to roughly half the melee stat line.
- Charged Skills 1–3 use the same ordinary skill cooldowns rather than hidden secondary cooldowns. Charge input may be held during the remaining ordinary cooldown; the UI shows WAIT until readiness, then starts the 0.65-second charge. For Skill 1, the held/queued charge suppresses movement-triggered automatic basic attacks so they cannot immediately consume the shared cooldown. Starting a charge gesture no longer requires the charged MP cost up front, preserving the normal low-cost/free tap behavior; charged casts still require 20%/30%/35% max MP at release and failure reasons are surfaced to the player. Cooldown text rounds upward to tenths so a positive cooldown never displays as 0.0s.
- The former talent UI is treated as **Discipline Training**, not raw attribute assignment. Four shared disciplines remain—Power Training, Mana Training, Health Training and Movement Training—with caps 5/5/5/3. Their hidden growth budget is redistributed from each class's natural level-1 profile while preserving the original 80-point total: Paladin 7 Power / 2 Mana / 33 HP / 38 movement, Mage 9 / 3 / 27 / 41, Ranger 8 / 2 / 29 / 41. The player sees available training points, each discipline's Rank current/max, and the exact per-rank gameplay effect for the current class. A fresh or migrated hero receives two free complete training resets that cannot be wasted with zero spent points; Neri's 250g reset remains the repeatable paid option afterward. Existing v4 saves preserve allocated ranks and missing HP while adopting class-weighted effects.
- Companion durability uses the matching hero archetype as its level-1 baseline: Soldier = Paladin baseline (120 HP, 8 defense), Archer = Ranger baseline (105 HP, 5 defense). Each companion then gains +12 max HP per hero level after level 1; defense does not grow automatically with level. Shared Training/Shared Strength inherited HP is added after that base formula. Companion defense has no free level growth: Soldier stays at base 8 and Archer at base 5 unless Shared Strength is learned, which adds 25/50/75/100% of the hero's armor-tier and armor-reforge bonus. Existing companions resynchronize live and on restore, preserving absolute missing HP and keeping fallen units at 0 HP.
- Neri the Alchemist owns the separate companion-survivability gold sink. After she is rescued, **Companion Vitality** may be trained repeatedly for 200g per rank; each rank adds +10% companion max HP additively after the normal role/level/inheritance HP calculation. It has no gameplay rank cap and may exceed +100%. It affects Soldiers and Archers only, never the hero, applies immediately to active and reserve companions, and raises fallen companions' max HP without reviving them. Neri exposes the same training service when spoken to directly in town and through a barracks' Rescued specialists menu. She also sells a flat 250g discipline-training reset through those same two access paths. Respec refunds every spent training point, removes the corresponding hero training bonuses, preserves level/skills/equipment, and immediately recalculates companion inheritance.
- Companion inheritance is handled by two paid support skills with separate rank counts. Shared Training has five cumulative instructor ceilings: Mira trains through Rank 1, Sela through Rank 2, Orin through Rank 3, Lyss through Rank 4 and Tovan through Rank 5; later instructors can cover skipped lower ranks one step at a time. It grants 20/40/60/80/100% of every applicable hero discipline-training stat to companions: training-derived HP, attack damage and movement speed. Mana regeneration remains hero-only because companions do not use mana. Shared Strength uses cumulative equipment-specialist ceilings: Borin trains through Rank 1, Dara through Rank 2, Eren through Rank 3 and Vera through Rank 4; each later smith can cover skipped lower ranks one step at a time, and any future smith after Vera should retain the Rank-4 ceiling. It grants 25/50/75/100% inheritance of non-talent bonus HP and damage above the hero's natural class/level baseline plus armor-tier/reforge defense bonuses. Both are live formulas rather than snapshots: learning catches the roster up immediately and later eligible hero-stat changes propagate automatically. Shared Training costs 280g per rank across Ranks 1–5 (1,400g total) because the training-derived bonus pool is smaller. Shared Strength costs 250g, 400g, 600g and 850g (2,100g total) because the non-talent equipment/reforge bonus pool is larger. Shared Training ends at Rank 5; Shared Strength ends at Rank 4.
- Party formation is automatic, role-aware and class-aware, and rotates from the hero’s recent movement direction. Mage/Ranger formations keep Archers close to the ranged hero and Soldiers on a wider protective ring. Paladin formations keep Soldiers close to the melee hero and Archers farther behind. Under THREATS/ADDS Soldiers prefer enemies pressing the hero or unassigned Archers; under TARGET/BOSS they attack the doctrine target directly. Archers maintain class-aware formation positioning, step forward only enough to acquire firing range and fall back toward the formation side when rushed.
- Worker is no longer a party class. New runs start with one Soldier and one Archer. Any idle active Soldier or Archer can perform expedition labor; resource assignment sends all currently idle active troops, while barracks construction/upgrading uses one. Laboring troops are temporarily unavailable for combat, and Recall cancels labor without erasing building progress. Existing saved Workers migrate to Soldiers with health proportion and carried resources preserved.
- Living companion healing and fallen-unit recovery are distinct systems. Refuge rest fully heals every living companion for free together with the hero; fallen companions remain fallen. A completed barracks can treat all living wounded companions for 30g only when no nearby enemies are actively engaged. Recovering one fallen companion costs 40g at a town Captain or barracks. Hero potions and hero recovery skills do not heal companions.
- Dungeon TRUE entitlements appear on reentry, leaving time to rescue captives and prepare. Bosses start away from the entrance and are announced.
- Authored water/cliff partitions, bridge gaps, forest clusters and connected roads form five regions. Terrain checks cover every service, captive, entrance, transport stand and boss home.
- Four boss slots combine cones, charges, circles, separated patches, projectiles, expanding rings and capped summons. Shared prototype primitives approximate some advanced animation descriptions while retaining readable warnings and safe routes.
- Music uses original synthesized instruments, harmony and filtered ambience. Every place has a melody and peaceful arrangement. Recorded orchestration is outside this prototype.
- Sprint remains dormant and unavailable. Q is reserved; no menu or saved setting enables it. Source and activation notes remain explicit.
- Offline exports are portable backups. Reloading the current Succession run cannot restore fallen classes, while deliberate imports of older exports can rewind it. There is no online anti-cheat service.

The expanded game is published at the existing GitHub Pages URL; legacy.html retains v0.5. GitHub Pages/PWA is the supported game target, with assets cached by the service worker after the first connected load.

## v0.7.0 audit fixes

Attack and quest prose no longer drives mechanics. Explicit rule tables define stages, motion, damage, recovery and authored sites. Existing version-4 saves remain compatible, with repaired road/site geometry and safe positions when changed terrain overlaps a saved actor. Legacy v2 exports remain backed up unchanged; their supply values, regional buildings and selectable weapons carry forward. See `AUDIT_FIXES_V070.md` for all 23 findings and the limits of automated acceptance.

## Decisions and separation of approval

**New owner directions and proposed embodiment:** Rogue is a **critical-centered class**; evaluate **+2 percentage points of personal critical chance per hero level** (instead of prior +1pp), level 1 included; every regular/charged skill should be a **reasonably sized AoE performance** doing something negative to hostiles **and something favorable to allied companions inside the same performance footprint**; distinct short buffs should be able to **overlap** under skilled timing; **all eight manual skills are combat-only**. The Rogue must have a **dedicated independent Level 1/2/3 critical-intensity category/slider**, not inherit the existing Hero intensity setting. The sixteen initial musical skill titles were prototype selections and have **since been reopened for owner review** following the bell-staff change; none is a fixed current naming requirement. All specific numbers, buff choices, per-skill critical levers and exact UI/save persistence mechanics below are **recommendations pending gameplay review**, not statements of owner signoff.

Never accidentally rewrite the original three hero classes, reassign the global Hero intensity, give the Rogue HP/Power/Armor/Speed passives, add buff spam actors, or treat this design document as a runtime implementation.

## Dual-sided AoE policy — MIRRORED performances (owner refinement)

**Latest owner intent:** These should feel like **two interpretations of exactly the same piece of music**. Each of eight normal and charged forms inflicts an offensive/debilitating effect on legal hostile actors while simultaneously creating a favorable **conceptual opposite** on living friendly companions within the **same bounded area**. Examples specifically given by the owner: **Slow ↔ speed and cooldown recovery**; **interrupt/Silence ↔ uninterruptible/protected casting**; **DoT/anti-heal ↔ healing over time and increased healing received**; **cooldown debt ↔ faster allied cooldown recovery**; **pushback/Weaken ↔ allied power/stability**; **wide blast/Silence ↔ allied power/critical opportunity**. The user's suggested mirror examples are design direction; the particular strengths below are unapproved candidates.

- **AoE for all 16 manual forms, including tap.** An individual tap may legally contact only one foe but still projects a small area to both sides. A charged form increases radius/target budget or potency moderately; it doesn't create a new class of global auras. **Proposed** music-focus location: legal reachable hostile position or instrument placement inside **260** from Rogue for tap (**300** charged); circular radii ~**135–165** tap, ~**175–205** charged. Skill-specific enemy target caps **2–4 tap / 3–6 charged**; friendly recipients are all eligible **living, active, in-zone** Soldier/Archer/charmed/pet members in exactly that circle (actual Rogue party cap up to seven), with LOS/wall safety. Enemy and ally outcomes are **independent** after one lawful cast: hostile resistance must not delete legitimate allied buffs, and allied absence must not cancel the hostile effect. Rogue herself gets **no companion buff** in this conservative candidate (owner can revisit).
- **Combat-only**: no opener from rest/camp, menu, or a long-lived passive benefit; must be near/engaged with at least one actually legal hostile at cast placement. No enemy? no legal manual cast, no friendly buff, no cooldown, no critical roll. This gate must fit actual targeting/hero approach and boss phases, not merely check any enemy anywhere in a zone. If combat ends, any persistent station ends immediately and short timed buffs dissipate naturally (or are cleared on transition/death). Never extend them by repeatedly placing stations after the encounter.
- **Intentional layerable symphony:** buffs from DIFFERENT skill families may coexist — movement, cast stability, healing, ward, special cooldown acceleration, damage and companion critical chance — so multi-button positioning/timing rewards a skilled Rogue. **Do not impose the earlier arbitrary 'maximum four distinct song boons' suggestion.** A companion may have at most **one active instance of each of the eight named families**; its normal/charged variants merge by best potency + later expiry **within bounded per-family duration**. No duplicate HoT budgets, double critical chance, accumulating casts, or per-tick permanent stat changes.
- **Stats shared between songs are combined predictably and safely**: speed bonus from Skill 2 and native Ranger Rally uses higher of the two for the same movement channel (not product); outgoing damage bonuses from Skill 1 (basic only), 7 (legal skill/basic damage), 8 (special only) add within capped relevant damage channels (candidate cap **+18%** from Rogue songs, not counting unrelated native class training or native critical damage). **Skill 2's small generic cooldown-recovery bonus and Skill 6's stronger special-only cooldown-recovery bonus CAN both contribute to special cooldowns**, but sum **additively** and cap combined Rogue-sourced acceleration at **+25%**, not two multiplicative time reductions. Skills on cooldown stay unavailable until a legitimate single native recovery step reaches zero; never shorten or bypass their immutable cooldown floor by casting repeatedly. This nuance implements the owner's desired layering without a cooldown loop.
- **No mass ally interruption bypass**: Skill 3's `Steady Cadence` boon protects an ally **that is actually preparing a lawful cast** from *damage-threshold cancellation*, for its short duration. An additional protection against genuine hostile Silence/skill cancellation is a **conditional extension requiring proof of a relevant native source**. It **does not** prevent death, Recall, target loss, zone change, invalid LOS/charge, exhaustion, keeper lock or script-owned cancel; it doesn't grant readiness, remove existing cooldowns or conjure a non-existent skill. Existing `combat.js::recordPartyChargeDamage` and `party.js::resolveCompanionCharges` prove both damage interruption and a Silence cast gate exist; any proposed immunity needs a narrow actor-specific exemption in those two existing paths. An ally silenced **before** gaining protection does **not** retroactively get a cast for free, unless a separate cleansing rule is approved. Do not redefine full game Silence generally. Enemies retain their existing boss restrictions for offensive Silence.
- **Healing inverse is two actual related benefits**: Skill 4's Mending Refrain has its own small *bounded HoT budget* and a capped **incoming-healing multiplier** on legitimately delivered outside heals (e.g., Archer Heal or pet heal), but never heals a fallen/off-field ally or exceeds maximum HP. Existing `party.js::updateRangerSupport` does not have a generic healing-received modifier: implement **one shared applied-HP-heal hook** in the future, exclude the song's own HoT from multiplying itself, and guard against recursive HoT accounting. If two source heals overlap, each stays within its source budget/health cap, no re-crit per tick.
- **Recommended Skill 5 reinterpretation, NOT owner-finalized deletion of the original two-trap concept:** replace conventional player traps with a *placed enchanted musical device/station* — a spell-guitar amp/standing music sigil, the fantasy 'portable speakers' analogy. Stationary, no summoned AI or moving sprites, **at most 2 placed stations** total, ~**8s tap / 10s charged**, center at valid point, same-radius friendly/enemy polarity. While an **ally is present in its area**, its positive beat lends resistance to Slows and incoming damage (charged), and while an **enemy enters/occupies** it, hostile pulses slow, and charged pulses deal modest damage. **Proposed bounded sampling every 0.5s**, timed pulses at most once per hostile per 1s; never roll a new critical for pulses, and no critical damage amplification to every pulse if registered lever targets a duration. An ally's station bonus ends on exit (grace ≤0.5s to avoid boundary chatter). Do not apply a second free, full-duration friendly buff at placement as the prior draft suggested: that would let allies depart without listening. A station cannot capture/charm enemies, pay kill XP on mere status entry, last after combat or become an invisible free heal station. If this area's field would be disproportionately expensive, limit active stations to **1 tap/2 charged** or slower bounded 1s ticks before adding actor animation.
- **The 16 performance names are reopened and may be renamed without modifying mechanics**. *Sticky Shuffle* and *Briar Boogie* can describe a snaring magical rhythm station just as well as a physical trap; never rename user-approved titles without renewed owner input. Candidate *Barbed Riff* auto / *Rattling Crescendo* third-hit finisher remain separate, have ordinary area damage and primary-only Charm rules; this 'all manual skills AoE' decision does not add allied buffs to **every 0.85-second automatic basic**, which would produce nearly perpetual buffs with no tactical commitment.
- **Once per legitimate committed action:** use one native PR #440 Party critical roll and **one** registered critical outcome lever, with a frozen receipt across all affected enemies/allies and all delayed descendants. Only Level 1/2/3 changes that **one pre-existing outcome** on a critical; normal copies of both hostile AND allied effects happen even without a crit. Do not implement 'one crit for enemies, another crit for allies', reroll per ally, or make crits generate more recipients/stations/status categories. Independent `rogue` intensity group stays Level 2 by default, even as +2pp/level makes high-level Rogue crits frequent.

**Tactical example with legal space/timing:** Rogue engages an enemy pack beside two companions. A *Lead Feet* slows enemies while helping allies move/recover; *Beat Breaker* disrupts a warned skill and keeps the Archer's next **legal** charge from being damage-canceled; *Sour Note* poisons hostile healing while mending those companions; *Time Out* further improves their special cooldown recovery within the shared **+25% Rogue-sourced cap**; the Rogue plants *Briar Boogie* as a small defensive rally location. Then *Last Laugh* offers a limited final crit-chance/special-damage window. Not every companion must be covered by all circles — party positioning, AoE selection, finite buff duration, cooldowns and target eligibility determine the actual layered result. This is stronger **party synergy**, not Mage-class raw hero damage.

**Interaction with existing systems:**
- `party.js::companionAttackDamage`, the Soldier/Archer/Pet damage owners and `companionRallyAttackSpeed` are real. Skill 1/7/8 outgoing-damage boons need central *per-action* multipliers, not permanent `u.damage` stat mutation. A critical attack's native Party level-based potency and timed buff potency must have defined order; proposed: build legal ordinary baseline, apply additive capped song bonuses, then apply single native critical lever. Do not crit the buff itself unless that skill's registered lever explicitly says to.
- `combat.js::cooldownRecoveryElapsed` today **returns real elapsed time, modified downward by Slow**. `engine.js` then decrements `healCd/manaCd/survivalCd` and `party.js` other special timers. Add bounded song acceleration at a **single common party clock owner** and ensure Slow interaction/diminishing suppression stays valid; Skill 2 could affect all timers while Skill 6 only affects *special* timers, **without** boosting basic cooldown `u.cd` twice. If native minimum/reaction floor matters, keep it. `rangerRallyAttackSpeed` only changes ordinary attack cadence; it is not Skill 6's same cooldown channel.
- `combat.js::recordPartyChargeDamage` accumulates actual HP lost until a charge is damage-interrupted; `party.js::resolveCompanionCharges` requires `!mageSilenceActive(u)`, death/Recall/keeper/zone/target validity. **Important verified nuance:** `hero-combat.js::mageSilenceActive(unit)` tests the *current hero's Mage-created silence field*, not a generic documented hostile Silence effect. A Rogue cannot normally use Mage Skill 3 during the same life. Do not invent one or bypass the old Mage's field globally. Implement one applied healing receive modifier that respects each source's own budget and no `Mending Refrain` self-amplification. `hitParty` existing `wardReduction` and `wardUntil` favor max-based shared defense, not multiplicative independent ward stacking.
- `save.js` strips transient `rangerRallyUntil` and wards from snapshots, and `engine.js` clears temporary support on death/recovery/zone changes. Design the Rogue's 8 song states, station entities and charge protection as **temporary validated combat state** stripped on save/travel/restart or intentionally resumed via an explicitly safe minimal snapshot, **not persistent attribute growth**.

## Performance and acceptance gates

1. **Baseline fairness and first capture:** 90 HP/18 Power/3 Armor/300 Speed at L1; zero starting companions. Compare actual early enemy HP/hits and first Charm access (12% original rate still tentative) against the three existing classes that start with two allies. A dense buff kit must not hide a disastrous solo opening.
2. **Chance, odds and isolates:** at L1/15/18 prove 12%/40%/46% Rogue without Codices and 17%/45%/51% with all five; Paladin/Mage/Ranger keep their own odds and Hero intensity; companions get only the proposed limited, timed Skill 8 **Finale Inspiration** critical-chance boon when actually within the footprint. Party/World RNG streams remain exactly two.
3. **Four-slider independence:** for each of Normal/Nightmare/Developer, exercise `hero=3,rogue=1,companion=2,world=3` and inverse combinations; verify only correct performers change. Rogue slider change after a released projectile/trap cannot affect its frozen receipt. Ensure slider is genuinely accessible and preference survives reopen/load if promised; old saves default Level 2.

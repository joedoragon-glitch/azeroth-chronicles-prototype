### Additional owner directions — 27 October 2026
Every regular and charged Rogue skill now affects both **hostile units negatively** and **nearby active friendly companions positively** in the **same bounded legal area**, with the companion benefit designed as a **thematic inverse of the enemy consequence** rather than an unrelated bonus. Examples: hostile Slow ↔ companion movement and cooldown recovery; interrupt/Silence ↔ protection for real legal companion casts against damage cancellation/new Silence; DoT + anti-heal ↔ allied HoT + enhanced healing received; cooldown debt ↔ allied special recharge; push/Weaken ↔ companion offense/conditional poise; final damaging Silence ↔ companion crit chance + special damage. **Skill Five is now at most two placed enchanted musical stations, not ordinary player-owned physical traps**; enemies inside are hindered while allies currently inside receive protection, on a bounded contact schedule without extra crit rolls. Multiple **different** song families may coexist (up to eight), while a cast never duplicates the same boon family. Proposed Skill 2 + 6 companion cooldown acceleration stacks **additively with a 25% Rogue-sourced cap**, not multiplicatively. Skills cannot be cast out of combat. The dedicated **Rogue-only Level 1–3 critical intensity** is a **fourth group** and must be **independently adjustable** from Paladin/Mage/Ranger, companions, and enemies. Rogue chance (proposed 10% base +2pp × hero level +Codices) and Rogue intensity remain independent. The existing Party RNG stream still handles Rogue critical rolls, and captured companions keep Companion intensity. Per-action tier catalogue uses only the merged #440 existing levers, one selected consequence per action. This approval is **directional**; not authorization to deploy any untested implementation.

21 October 2026. Requested by Joel after authorizing the complete regional overhaul. Prepared implementation in PR #374; live status belongs to its publication receipt.

## Review scale and evidence

The default camera is 150% in `platform.js`. All 25 location/day/night/phone gameplay captures use 1.5×. The separate 0.31× overview checks composition only. ## Result

The focused review supports publication of this complete Highlands update. The concrete audit repairs—saved encounter metadata, explicit coastal travel copy and cliff coloring on Map & Navigation—are included in the same candidate. Per-design handoffs retain exact states, source hashes, appearance scope and native references for later sprite work.

The pre-existing unrelated Marches material/scope audit mismatches remain outside this update. Broad CI and the full browser/device matrix were not run under Joel’s focused owner workflow. The original report of more than 900 crowns in about a minute was not independently reproduced as the 640-crown outdoor pool alone; quest/enemy income remains separate. Owner play/appearance feedback follows publication.

Spawn audit: four original dev.137 seed controls retain 49/49 native outdoor IDs (48 ordinary enemies and Ridge Tyrant), with clear relocated homes and unchanged night/leave/return/reload counts. Both scheduled ringleaders and TRUE Ridge spawn on valid ground. Pending bases now reuse their original monster’s migrated home, preserving counts and timers. Evidence: `tools/visual-review/highlands-dev138/spawn-checks.json`.

The later requested Highlands Treasury interior is also complete: see [its scoped audit and pilot standard](HIGHLANDS_TREASURY_DEV138.md). Its five additional native scenes use 150%, all 18 prop bases respect usable space, and all seven original defenders, three exact quest caches, fort benefits, paired returns and saved balances remain.

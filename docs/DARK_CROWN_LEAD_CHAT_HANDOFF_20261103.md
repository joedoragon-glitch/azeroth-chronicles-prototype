- **NL15** **clear cool dry nights by default**, supplemented by occasional **localized** blowing ash and **subtle** geothermal warmth/heat shimmer; never hide player, enemy cues or routes (the owner's A + measured B/C).
- **NL16** deliberate visual sequence of **civilization → open lava country → sheltered greenery**, rather than scenery scatter or full-region vegetation carpet.

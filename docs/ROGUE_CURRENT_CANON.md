# Rogue / Pícara — current playable canon and development authority

**Maintained reconciliation · 28 October 2026.** This page is the **start-here authority** for Rogue-specific design, visual identity, implementation guidance and public copy in **So I Became the Dark Lord's Problem**. It resolves contradictory older Rogue Bard proposals. For the latest publication SHA and device acceptance, use [Development state](DEVELOPMENT_STATE.md), not a dated handoff.

## What is actually current

- **Release:** Rogue/Pícara is the fourth **normally selectable, playable** class after [PR #511](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/511) merged on **28 October 2026**. The published source described by the development ledger is **0.9.1+snapshot.dev.208**. This is a *soft launch*, not a claim of completed player, artwork, sound, performance, balance or full-campaign acceptance.
- **Public class name:** **Rogue** (Spanish **Pícara**), not “Rogue Bard” on character selection or player-facing class labels. “Rogue Bard” remains a legitimate *historical/internal document and development feature name*, not another class.
- **Weapon — binding owner decision (27 October):** **one staff bearing one oversized spherical jingle bell suspended from its head**, for both regular and crowned Rogue. **No guitar, lute, strings, strumming or guitar-playing poses** are part of Rogue equipment or attack identity. A standalone spherical jingle-bell symbol (without the staff) represents her class in New Game, the HUD and Succession. Her basic-attack audio identity is a **short jingle/rattle**, not plucked-string music. An unrelated background soundtrack may contain guitar; do not confuse that with Rogue equipment.
- **Character:** female Rogue, warm dark-brown skin, visible long wavy black hair, mischievous/confident expression, asymmetrical sari-inspired plum/violet/copper traveling clothing with sash and gold/brass accessories. Crowned Rogue keeps the **same woman and same jingle-bell staff**, adding a dark-violet royal mantle, crimson details and narrow five-point circlet. Reference imagery supplied for the bell staff was **weapon-only** and did not authorize an outfit/face redesign.
- **Rendering:** Current regular and crowned Rogue **procedural static drawings** are implemented in `src/prototype/visuals.js` (`rogueBellStaff()`, `rogueHero()`) and use existing Canvas and optional experimental Pixi art pathways. No approved `hero:rogue` raster sprite or separate walk/dance animation set is required. Native-scale, phone and Pixi visual acceptance remains distinct from having coded the artwork.
- **Gameplay identity:** Rogue starts alone, cannot *newly hire* ordinary Soldiers or Archers, and can **Charm eligible surviving monsters via lawful automatic-primary basic hits**. Initial approved chance is **12% per eligible hit**, independent of criticals. Each captured monster retains species identity, receives an automatically assigned permanent name and friendly full HP, and joins persistent company subject to its type-specific Expedition ownership quota. Bosses, captains and Ringleaders are not charmable. Do not apply Charm on splash targets.
- **First Barracks:** Rogue **does not construct it personally**. A legitimately Charmed monster must be ordered to build it; Charmed monsters are eligible for normal companion construction and resource labor, subject to the existing availability/rank rules. No fake starting worker or construction shortcut. The intended onboarding order is **Charm first → order the captured companion to build Barracks**.
- **Other class behavior:** Existing Soldiers/Archers legitimately inherited during Succession remain usable, and Charmed companions remain owned/recoverable after a later non-Rogue successor. A non-Rogue successor cannot perform *new* Charm. Existing v4 save semantics and protection apply; there are no extra Rogue save slots.
- **Skills and criticals:** Rogue's **eight tap / eight charged combat performances** use actual skill definitions in `src/prototype/skill-reference.js`, `src/prototype/hero-combat.js` and related rules; Skill 5 uses **placed magical music stations** rather than the old proposed physical traps. **Endure** is the authored brief 1-HP survival mechanic. The separate Rogue critical-intensity category has developer-authored **Level 2 default**; it is **not** a player-facing slider, Game Settings option or saved profile control. Earlier song-name approval states, proposed effect coefficients and prototype tables are not release authority; verify names and numbers against current source.

## How to read Rogue documentation without restoring old decisions

1. **Authority order:** latest explicit owner decision → current runtime source and accepted tests for *what is implemented* → this maintained Rogue canon and current development ledger → dated implementation evidence. Never promote a dated proposal into current requirements because it contains detailed numbers, art directions, or the words “approved” or “mandatory” at its historical checkpoint.
2. **Historical design records:** the 26 October [discussion](ROGUE_BARD_DISCUSSION_RECORD_20261026.md), [visual specification](ROGUE_BARD_STATIC_VISUAL_DESIGN_20261026.md), [one-page owner review](ROGUE_BARD_OWNER_REVIEW_20261026.md), [balance candidate](ROGUE_BARD_GAMEPLAY_BALANCE_CANDIDATE_20261026.md), feasibility, phased handoffs and the [inert JSON candidate](../tools/rogue-bard/rogue-bard-candidate.json) **preserve superseded alternatives**. Their guitar, basic attack, wounded-capture, skill-title, crit-slider, first-Barracks, draft-only and release-status instructions are **not executable requirements**. Those records are retained for provenance, not copied into implementation or announcements.
3. **Original source and tests before editing:** inspect `src/prototype/rules.js`, `visuals.js`, `app.js`, `engine.js`, `hero-combat.js`, `audio-hero.js`, and relevant Rogue tests. Existing `tests/rogue-bard-procedural-art.test.cjs` already rejects guitar/lute/stringed Rogue procedural artwork; `tests/rogue-jingle-icon-readability.test.cjs` protects the standalone jingle symbol. Any change to the weapon must also reconcile world art, combat cues, audio, New Game, HUD, Succession and affected tests.
4. **Public announcements:** describe the live Rogue, not her 26 October guitarist prototype or 27 October draft state. Do **not** claim completed phone/boss/visual/audio/performance acceptance. If the owner has not authorized an announcement, this document is **not** permission to publish one.
5. **Documentation maintenance:** update this file, affected current guides and existing tests together when the owner changes class canon or implementation. Mark older choices as historical while preserving their records. Keep links and [documentation routing](DOCUMENTATION_INDEX.md) synchronized; do not resurrect an abandoned feature merely because it remains in the archive.

**This is a documentation reconciliation only.** It does not alter gameplay, art, audio, balance, release version, installed-PWA caches or device certification.

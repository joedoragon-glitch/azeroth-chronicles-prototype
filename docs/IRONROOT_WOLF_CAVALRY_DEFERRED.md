# #239 P2 — Crown soldiers riding Ironroot Wolves (retained idea)

**Explicitly excluded from the beta-essential Ironroot money patch.** Existing wild Wolves, dens, Ogre households, Crown soldiers, ridge archers, encounters and collision remain unchanged. No rider sprites, mounted attack logic, troop additions, wolf recruitment or new travel system are shipped by #239 P0.

The image worth retaining is a small Crown escort detail: an Ironroot Wolf willingly or reluctantly working as a powerful mount under the Master of Coin's contracted caravan guard, with one ordinary Crown soldier carrying a spear or bow. This should not read as all Wolves serving the regime, and it must leave intact the autonomous wild packs.

A later, separately approved prototype needs a bound upper rider count, consent/capture/relationship canon, visually distinct Wolf anatomy + rider gear, ownership of melee and ranged attacks, ranged elevation/aim, mounted movement/turns, projectile hit boxes, hazard response, aggression/target selection, dismount/death/summon semantics, save compatibility and travel-corridor tests. It should be demonstrated as a fixed-size isolated encounter before wider use.

**No dependency:** Ironroot's crown ore→refinery→mint→caravan chain is fully reviewable without cavalry.

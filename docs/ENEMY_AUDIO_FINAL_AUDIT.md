# Final enemy audio/VFX synchronization audit · v0.8.117

Baseline: main `cdc8286ca499af1de4de53ffa3014bb89203cdab`, retaining merged audio #164, rogue #146 and VFX #162. The audit changes presentation metadata, sound levels and authoring tools only. No damage, collision, target selection, cooldown, movement, AI, balance, warning geometry or v4 save code changes.

## Corrected findings

- Automatic TRUE reinforcement births could inherit an unrelated active telegraph or another actor's resolving attack. Only a same-actor explicit `kind: summon` resolution now owns cast births. Automatic boss waves use the actual authored summon slot; captain opening/phase births use `captain/<profile>/phase`. Captain role comes from the real `ranged` field. No cast, warning or damage footprint is manufactured for automatic births.
- The birth observer also dereferenced a null caller after the original summon method had safely returned zero. It now preserves guarded no-op calls and exits before deriving metadata when no unit was born.
- The basic-melee silent path accepted unknown stages, and the acceptance gate did not check personality textures. Routing now validates stages, material, personality, action and accent before silent/shared decisions. The audio owner exports its actual personality texture table; coverage checks it along with materials and motifs. No second skill registry is introduced.
- Warm recorded foreground effects were substantially louder than their immediate procedural fallback: representative desktop/phone comparisons found roughly 6–8 dB impact jumps. Shared recorded release/contact gains are reduced in the existing manifest, with separate retained levels for spectral/holy/arcane textures. Procedural recipes and live actor/signature motifs remain intact.
- The warning recording applied its global decay to the second pulse before that pulse began. Each pitched pulse now has its own local envelope, restoring the descending pair without changing its 0.3-second duration or the stage dispatch time. Its WAV, manifest hash and measurements are regenerated from the retained original synthesis recipe.
- A single-cue authoring render discarded measurements for untouched recordings. `render-sfx.py --id` now retains those measurements and accurately reports the number of rendered cues.

Six commander paths check damage-bearing rally warnings, exact native-defender revival positions/IDs, audible spawn routing and silence when already-living defenders are rallied again. Normal/TRUE attack resolutions, miss/contact discrimination, cold immediate fallback, warm exact bindings, source reservation and disposal retain their existing tests. Rogue integration independently checks all effect outcomes, dodges, cover, restraint and save/death cleanup.

The browser audio gate renders all twelve material families through cold and warm playback in desktop/reference and phone mixes. It requires actual warm recorded dispatch, finite/unclipped output, no missed-contact sound, bounded voices, comparable foreground RMS and two measurable warning pulses. Numeric renders are signal checks, not subjective speaker certification. Per-browser measurements are retained as `test-results/enemy-audio-mix-<engine>-<desktop|phone>.json` in workflow artifacts.

All thirty effects are reproduced in memory from `sfx-book.json` and the renderer, then compared byte-for-byte and by SHA-256 with the registered WAVs. Only the warning master changes. The other 29 masters, music/environment assets, credits, guides, mono format, duration and package inventory remain intact. Single-cue generation retains the complete 30-entry measurement catalog.

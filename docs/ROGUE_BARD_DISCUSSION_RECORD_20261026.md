## Why preserve this historical owner/assistant design chronology

The intended fourth class does **not** compete by being the strongest, toughest, or most agile. She succeeds by being the most shamelessly disruptive character in the game: misdirection, casting interruption, Slow, traps, status manipulation, survival tricks, and recruiting former enemies. The owner called the character a Rogue, presented *as a bard*, with a guitar, music and dancing themes, irreverent humor and a charismatic, mischievous personality. The comparison was to a showy, playful “Ricardo” kind of dancer, not a request to copy another person's likeness. She is **female**, joining the female Mage; the Ranger and Paladin remain male as specified in the conversation. The role is a distinctive gameplay archetype rather than a generic high-damage or high-dexterity Rogue.

This was explicitly late-night brainstorming: preserve the direction, do not silently finalize numerical balance, content, or art. The owner subsequently instructed us to ground the proposal in the current game, which uses **static sprites/procedural drawings moved by the engine**, and to **avoid adding walking/dancing/attack animation systems, poses, sprite sheets, or costly new renderer machinery** simply to ship this class. New visual framing should use a single readable static appearance and existing lightweight presentation conventions. The class must not destabilize game performance or established gameplay.

## A. Owner-originated design intentions

The following are the owner's concepts, recorded as requirements-to-investigate, not evidence that current systems already support them.

1. **Class identity:** female Rogue Bard; guitar, music-themed attacks, dance/music personality, glamorous/showy, deliberately “scumbag” battlefield trickster. She is neither especially powerful, tough, nor agile. Her special strength is inflicting misery through manipulation and control.
2. **Basic-attack charm:** ordinary basic attacks automatically carry a **chance** to charm an eligible opponent; successfully charmed monsters **join her company persistently**. This is her replacement for buying ordinary troops. Initial roster **empty**. Recruitment comes from enemy captures and the existing collectable boss pets.
3. **Ordinary troops forbidden:** Rogue cannot hire conventional Soldier or Archer/Ranger companions at either Captain or Barracks, including recruitment queues. This is a gameplay constraint, not just hiding a menu.
4. **Charisma advantage:** from the **beginning of the campaign**, Rogue has **one additional active companion slot relative to the equivalent Expedition stage**, despite beginning with zero companions. The owner called it access to a third companion from the start as an illustration of that advantage.
5. **Enemy eligibility:** ordinary, eligible monster species may be charmed. **Bosses, captains and Ringleaders are excluded**. Genuine hybrid combatants are *not committed*: possibly reuse pet-style behavior, but explicitly defer if complicated. Summoned or temporary enemies were not separately decided.
6. **Converted AI:** melee converts should follow the established **Soldier companion behavior**; ranged converts should follow the established **Archer companion behavior**, not continue running hostile AI. For an eligible ranged healer, player-side healing must follow **friendly companion healing and Alchemist training**, not its original enemy healing rules. Avoid duplicating companion engines.
7. **Converted statistics:** retain the identity and distinguishing native characteristics of the captured monster **and** adapt its growth to existing companion progression/inheritance. Exact treatment of initial HP, damage, armor, level, ranged/melee identity, and level normalization remains unresolved; the two instincts must be reconciled, not discarded.
8. **Death and recovery:** initial thought of permanent loss was **revised**: dead charmed companions should **be recoverable**, using ordinary company lifecycle. The objective is to avoid repeated travel and recapture chores.
9. **Barracks restraint:** owner specifically does not want unlimited monster storage. For **each enemy species**, combined active + reserve + fallen owned copies should be at most the Rogue's **current maximum active-companion capacity at her current Expedition progression**, including her class advantage. Example: if she can field four companions, she may own no more than four Goblin Slingers, across all roster states. Increasing Expedition raises the species bound. The larger aggregate Barracks limit needs a further decision; a species-only limit is not necessarily enough.
10. **Boss pets:** Rogue can still obtain collectible companions from defeating boss monsters through the existing pet system; does **not** charm bosses into party actors. The class-specific rules for boss-pet ownership under Succession follow the existing persistent-company design.
11. **Trap expertise:** detect/interact with and disable eligible existing traps. She may **place at least two varieties of trap**. Her traps should work against hostile monsters. Class should be the most irritating trap user without altering the world layout.
12. **Disruption:** frequently interrupt enemy spells or cast preparation, more conveniently/frequently than Mage Interrupt (without presuming permanent lockout or replacing the Mage's area Silence); Slow approaching or fleeing enemies; apply damage over time, reduced incoming healing, cooldown delays/debt and other annoying statuses; punish summons and attempts to escape.
13. **Summoning:** owner suggested she can summon as part of her trickster theme, but **did not decide what is summoned**, its lifetime, slot treatment, AI, or whether it is a combatant, decoy, or effect. Must not turn into a hidden second army.
14. **Endure passive:** upon lethal damage she can endure for **two seconds at one HP**, as a distinct class survival trick. Exact invulnerability/damage gating, follow-on death check, cooldown and interaction with recovery, boss hits, Succession, save/reload and current death flow remain **open**.
15. **Visual direction tentative:** attractive/darker-skinned woman with South Asian or Middle Eastern / Persian-inspired fantasy influences; owner floated sari-like draping, exposed hair and no hijab as one direction. These are exploratory character-design ideas, **not** a finalized cultural costume, portrait, sprite, palette, or animation brief. Keep expressive stylization compatible with current original-art canon and game scale.

## B. Assistant suggestions during brainstorming — NOT owner approvals

- Named eight-skill kit: Cutting Chord, Off-Key Waltz, Heckler's Note, Rotten Serenade, Tripwire Tango, Sabotage Verse, Backup Dancers, Grand Finale. All names, slot numbers and **normal/charged effects are provisional examples**; the owner has not approved them.

- Proposed cooldown length (e.g. around 90 seconds) for Endure and a possible auto-death-on-expiration rule. **No numerical cooldown or post-Endure outcome has been approved.**
- A temporary decoy or “backup dancer” unit, larger showpiece music fields, or a specific widening/charged charm attack. Owner suggested summoning but not these implementations.

- The earlier statement that Rogue summons/dances would introduce no cost or be easy is **not** a performance claim or accepted design.
- Male/female description should follow the owner's corrected decision: **female Rogue, female Mage, male Ranger, male Paladin**. Older ancillary descriptions or prototype legacy labels are not approval to change this intent.

## C. Questions explicitly reserved for later design review

These should be individually decided before coding, not answered by developer assumption:

- Basic hit charm probability and cooldown/suppression; what qualifies as a hit; auto targeting; eligibility if the roster is full; how early she recruits her first companion; whether damage threshold, escalating chance, or immunity applies.
- Which species/variants are eligible (night creatures, field guardians, dungeon guardians, ranged healers, hybrid species, special scripted enemies); summoned enemies default to **no eligibility pending review**.
- Is charmed species strength a fixed companion-normalized baseline with visual/species profile, a level-preserving base, or another method? How are progression HP/damage/armor, Companion Vitality, Shared Training, healing and Alchemist upgrades applied? Avoid hostile rank/TRUE/region multipliers leaking to allies.
- Are charm-converted monsters permanent across regions, transitions, death, game reloads and game phases? Owner's persistent-companion intent suggests yes; verify consequences.
- Does the per-species cap count a captured subtype by species alone or species+role variant (e.g. Goblin melee vs Goblin Slinger)? Owner example explicitly uses “Goblin Slinger”; precise roster key needed.
- What is the **total** monster reserve budget beyond the species quota? Keep the ordinary 200-saved-actors defensive maximum as an emergency bound, not a desired gameplay inventory.
- Can charmed monsters perform labor, carry tribute, use equipment, inherit skills and count toward quests? No blanket assumption. Recommended first release: ordinary **combat-only** companions pending explicit policy, to keep scope manageable.
- How is the bonus slot handled under Basic vs Full Barracks, Expedition, existing saves, and party capacity after changing classes? The owner's **Succession inheritance rule is settled** below; deterministic reserve overflow is the remaining proposed detail.
- Is trap disarm permanent, temporary, by trap instance, by cycle or until dungeon reset? What materials/cooldowns cost placing a trap? Can monsters trigger player traps while remaining immune to environmental traps?
- Which existing statuses adequately express damage-over-time, anti-healing and cooldown sabotage, and which need new bounded fields? What immune/exclusion mechanics do bosses and captains need?
- How does the two-second Endure interact with repeated lethal hits, death penalties, potions, healing, regeneration, interrupts, pauses, time skips and Succession? Owner also proposed **+1 percentage point personal critical chance per hero level**; this proposal should follow the level-1 onset convention in draft PR #509.
- Where, if anywhere, should an additional summon exist in the eight-skill kit, and is it a visual/taunt decoy rather than an actual new AI actor?
- Equipment, talents, training, skill journal, combat diagnostics, tutorial/quest wording, bilingual localization, sound effects and class naming.
- Art direction, final silhouette, guitar model and outfit should be reviewed as one **static** hero design; do not commission animation production.

## C2. Later owner decisions — binding amendments on 26 October 2026

**Further stat-baseline correction (owner):** do not invent Rogue base attributes without directly comparing the three live hero classes. The owner wants the Rogue's **starting HP equal to the weakest existing class, Power equal to the weakest, and speed equal to the slowest**, with weakness in ordinary attributes compensated by quirky mechanics—not a stronger base class. Verified `R.balance.classes`: Paladin 120 HP / 18 Power / 8 Armor / 300 Speed; Mage 90 / 22 / 3 / 300; Ranger 105 / 20 / 5 / 320. Therefore source-based Rogue initial candidate = **90 HP / 18 Power / 3 Armor / 300 Speed**, plus **60 dormant MP** proposed to match the minimum existing MP. Mage's **9** comes from `disciplines.profiles.mage.power` (+9 **per purchased Power Discipline rank**), not her starting Power (22). Armor 3 and dormant MP 60 are logical smallest-baseline **recommendations** rather than individually spoken numeric owner approvals. Do not silently adjust the other three classes. Nor does class-minimum HP/Power/Speed prove early combat fairness: Rogue uniquely begins with **no companions** and needs Charm to recruit; test opening viability before accepting final Charm odds or AoE damage.

## C2a. Succession and mechanics clarifications (owner)

1. **Succession ownership resolved:** successors of any class **inherit existing charmed monsters**, still as ordinary active/reserve/fallen persistent companions with species and recovery intact. A non-Rogue successor **cannot charm new monsters**. Do not delete, convert back to hostile or invalidate inherited charmed allies. This is exclusively an extension of the existing Succession company persistence, not a new Succession mechanic. The existing class selector already derives available classes from the registered class list; adding Rogue simply makes the class pool four.
2. **Personal critical passive proposed:** the Rogue should gain **+1 percentage point of hero-only crit chance per hero level**, with the class passive active at level 1 as in draft PR #509, and **no new HP, Armor, Power, Speed, CDR or other stat benefit from the passive**. Her existing two-second 1-HP Endure remains the other effect of that passive identity. Base chance currently 10% and five Codices can provide up to 5%; proposed Rogue at L15 is 25% without Codices / 30% with five. Precise crit effect and 17 action keys are in the candidate gameplay spec.
3. **Basic attack direction:** user wants an ordinary automatic guitar/music attack **in between melee and long range**, ideally the only character with a **limited AoE on every basic**, plus a Rogue-appropriate three-hit finisher. Avoid animated body movement or inventing a stance-switch mechanic. The candidate proposes a single short-range instant fan, small fixed number of victims, one crit and one primary-target Charm check per lawful auto.
4. **Correction from deeper source audit:** `save.js` retains hardcoded three-class bounds for Succession saved-state validation, and `party.js` has six positional offsets for ordinary troopers. They require small four-class / seventh-party-slot adaptations, **not a redesign**.
5. **Art and crown:** the later request explicitly calls for normal **and Dark Lord/crowned** Rogue appearances. Both must be documented and implemented as existing-style **static** art, not poses or animations. See [static art design candidate](ROGUE_BARD_STATIC_VISUAL_DESIGN_20261026.md).

## C3. Latest owner clarification — musical titles that signal their effects (27 October 2026)

**Owner-approved naming principle, followed by explicit approval of the 16 displayed titles:** Rogue's **normal and charged attacks** should have names that could be **actual dance moves, musical movements, songs or composition titles**. A name should also suggest **what its attack does to the enemy**. Prefer expressive, clever performance nomenclature (*Broken Cadence* interrupting casts, *Dragging Waltz* slowing foes, *Stolen Tempo* delaying readiness, *The Silencing Coda* causing brief Silence) over generic “Bard Attack,” “Dance Attack,” “Rogue Strike,” or unrelated flavor names. A charged title should feel like the larger or darker movement of the corresponding normal composition, **not a separate skill family**.

The latest [gameplay candidate](ROGUE_BARD_GAMEPLAY_BALANCE_CANDIDATE_20261026.md) and [owner review](ROGUE_BARD_OWNER_REVIEW_20261026.md) present **eight new normal/charged title pairs** plus auto and third-hit finisher. **Latest decision (27 October 2026):** the owner explicitly responded “Yeah, I like the names. Those are good” to the displayed table of eight normal and eight charged titles. Treat the **16 individual titles in that table as approved naming**, not merely examples. Exact skill effects, values, critical levers and implementation remain unapproved design candidates. The automatic basic and third-hit finisher names were outside that displayed table and stay proposals. The earlier Cutting Chord/Off-Key Waltz/etc. list remains in section B as preserved historical brainstorming only. The user did not request different mechanics, more effects, singing audio, animated dance cycles, or name-dependent combat bonuses; do not change any of those under a naming-only revision.


## C4. Owner design expansion — Rogue as critical-led dual-sided AoE supporter (27 October 2026)

**New owner directions:** every regular and charged Rogue skill should be a **reasonable AoE**, usable **only during actual combat**, whose music simultaneously applies its negative effect to enemies and a **helpful bounded timed effect** to allied companions **inside that same AoE**. A skilled Rogue can time casts to **overlap several different ally boons**, making her companions formidable despite her own modest base statistics. This **supersedes** earlier single-target-only Rogue skills/strictly hostile-only effects. Normal/charged 16 names remain the approved titles. The owner is actively considering replacing **+1pp** Rogue personal crit chance per hero level with **+2pp per hero level**, starting at L1; treat exact increased slope as a **promising owner-proposed balance value**, not proof or implemented change. It would mean 40% at L15 / 46% at L18, before the separately earned max5pp Codices.

**Additional owner requirement:** Rogue must have a **FOURTH independent Level 1–3 critical-effect intensity category and its own adjustable slider**, not share the conventional Paladin/Mage/Ranger Hero intensity setting. Retain distinct ordinary Hero, Companions/Pets, World settings. Rogue action critical RNG remains in the Party stream and companion critical outcomes obey Companions intensity. All categories default Level 1; moving Rogue slider affects only future Rogue hero criticals. The current merged #440 source has API tuning but not yet a persistent user-facing slider. Implementation needs real UI/config and migration, not simply a label.

**Critical design contract:** merged [PR #440](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/440) has three **strength** levels and one critical consequence lever per action, one critical roll at cast/release shared across area/periodic contacts. Not every critical doubles damage; a Level3 Rogue crit may meaningfully improve **one** enemy debuff or **one** allied performance buff. No extra Charm, bonus enemies/companions, extra attack, or three independent critical rolls. Preserve performance constraints, limit ally status record counts and prevent unbounded self-refresh. Comprehensive specific proposals are in [the 27 October dual-sided/critical matrix](ROGUE_BARD_DUAL_SIDED_PERFORMANCES_CRITICAL_TIERS_20261027.md); its exact buffs/values/levers need review.

## C5. Latest owner refinement — mirror effects, companion dependence, music stations (27 October 2026)

**Owner's most recent correction:** allied buffs should generally be **the BENEFICIAL inverse of what that same musical performance does to enemies**, not loosely associated bonuses; for less obvious attacks we may develop a creative but mechanically useful musical opposite. All eight tap/charged skills remain appropriately sized AoEs and combat-only. The owner specifically illustrated hostile **Slow ↔ allied movement speed plus cooldown acceleration**, hostile **interrupt/Silence ↔ protection from damage-induced charge interruption and from hostile silence stopping a legal cast**, hostile **damage-over-time/healing suppression ↔ allied HoT plus improved healing received**, hostile **cooldown debt ↔ ally cooldown acceleration** layered with other sources, **push/Weaken ↔ ally movement/power**, and a destructive/silencing finale ↔ **power/critical opportunity for allies**. These are example directions, not numerical agreements, and an ally benefit must have actual combat utility given Soldier/Archer/Pet AI.

**Skill 5 changed conceptually:** instead of an enemy-only physical trap with a friendly buff thrown on at cast time, the Rogue may place a **limited magical musical station — a fantasy portable speaker / active music platform**. It applies debuffs to *enemies presently within the field* and buffs to *allies presently within it*, simultaneously, with a small radius and finite lifetime. Recommend max two static devices and a bounded contact schedule. The owner remains interested in separate Rogue trap *detection/disarming*, but do not treat the previously proposed physical player trap form of Skill 5 as already settled. The sixteen approved musical move names are still the default; the owner invited reconsideration if a mechanic truly demands a different name, **not a directive to rename**.

**Class identity refinement:** she should rely on her charmed monsters, standard inherited troops where permitted, and boss pets as a **battlefield director**, not compete on Mage raw output, Paladin frontline defense or Ranger mobility. Skillful play creates **multiple overlapping DISTINCT supportive performances** with reasonable time caps; **do not invent a hard four-distinct-buff limit**. CDR overlap needs a clear additive cap and single-clock application; anti-interrupt protection must not make comrades immortal or bypass death/Recall/invalid target. Specific eight-pair effects and 16 native Level-1/2/3 critical strengths are now proposed in [the revised two-sided music-spec](ROGUE_BARD_DUAL_SIDED_PERFORMANCES_CRITICAL_TIERS_20261027.md), with actual source references and documented integration gaps. **+2pp crit chance per Rogue hero level is still being evaluated**, while the separate Rogue 1–3 intensity category itself is owner-requested.

## C5. Latest mirrored-effect interpretation / boundaries (27 October 2026)

**Owner conceptual examples, not signed-off numerical effects:** each song has a hostile consequence and a **thematically inverse friendly consequence in the same limited AoE**, not a random secondary bonus. Slow → ally speed *and* cooldown recovery; interruption/Silence → casting harder to cancel; damage-over-time/heal reduction → heal-over-time and increased incoming heals; cooldown debt → cooldown recovery; push/Weaken → increased friendly power and stability; broad damage/Silence → companion offensive power and perhaps companion crit chance. The Rogue is specifically intended to **depend heavily on companions and boss pets**, not overpower the Paladin/Mage/Ranger on ordinary stats.

**Skill 5 choice still open:** the owner floated changing original two physical traps into stationary two-polarity magical music sources—the fantasy equivalent of portable speakers, harming nearby foes and helping allies while within their fields. This strongly informs the current candidate, but was framed as an exploration, **not explicit cancellation of previously requested trap-placement expertise**. Keep existing-trap disarming as a separate Rogue quirk and ask in the future whether the proposed two charged/tap musical stations satisfy the earlier “two trap types” goal. No extra actor AI or new animation.

**Native implementation correction:** actual `combat.js::recordPartyChargeDamage` can cancel a living companion's legitimate charged cast after enough lost HP; a temporary protection against that is coherent. The existing `hero-combat.js::mageSilenceActive()` checks the *current hero Mage silence field*, not a proven general enemy-applied companion Silence. Thus the owner-desired 'can't be Silenced/interrupted' fantasy should first protect actual damage interruption, then extend to a real hostile Silence cancellation route **only if one is found and verified**; do not create a fake immunity or retroactively activate a barred cast. All owner-approved sixteen names stay intact.

**Latest design authority** is [Rogue mirrored performance / critical tier proposal](ROGUE_BARD_DUAL_SIDED_PERFORMANCES_CRITICAL_TIERS_20261027.md), with forthcoming concise reconciliation index. Older +1pp candidate, strictly single-target tap skills, arbitrary ally buffs and physical trap-only Skill 5 sketches are **historical entries**, not equal current alternatives.

## C6. Owner clarification — low-friction monster collection and per-type Expedition quotas (27 October 2026)

**This is newer than the unresolved aggregate-Barracks question in section A.9.** The Rogue's current Expedition rank, including her class-only +1 field slot, sets the total number of copies she can own of **each individual eligible monster type**: **3/4/4/5/6/7** for ranks 1–6. Count active, reserve, fallen and away companions together. Goblin Slingers are a separate precise authored type from other Goblins. **Do not impose the earlier proposed twelve-creature aggregate cap.** Different monster types have independent quotas; the owner wishes to revisit actual **Charm probability** only after evidence of excessive conversions, not preemptively suppress ownership by a guessed overall threshold.

**Barracks clarify access, not ownership:** the existing Basic Barracks remains a useful limited field-service facility and manages a maximum of **three active members locally**. The upgraded Full Barracks allows the full effective Expedition cap (seven at Rogue rank six). The Rogue can Charm and retain eligible units even when no Barracks has been built and even if her active party is full (overflow to roster reserve, subject to type quota). A Barracks does **not** have a separate physical storage capacity that deletes or disallows charmed members. Existing boss pets remain their own collection.

**Navigation priority:** the high-volume management UI should show **type groups first** and short, paginated individual lists with clear active/resting/fallen status and fast Rest/Add, stable Back/page context, and light-touch naming. Basic Barracks needs only conservative housekeeping; Full Barracks must be comfortable when many types are represented. See the [source-reviewed owner contract](ROGUE_BARD_CHARMED_ROSTER_BARRACKS_CONTRACT_20261027.md) and [same-PR preparatory interface work](ROGUE_BARD_FOURTH_CLASS_HOUSEKEEPING_20261027.md).

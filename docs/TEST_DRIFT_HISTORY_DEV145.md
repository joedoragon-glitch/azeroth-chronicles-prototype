## Additional coverage gap identified during the trace

**A “Nightmare” label does not prove permanent-night coverage.** The specialist fixtures in `snapshot-dreadmaw` and `cindermaw-ministry` instantiate fresh Nightmare campaigns, then advance enemy/attack phases directly. Those calls do not consume the global sunset timer. Their current mode cases can therefore exercise Nightmare during its intended grace period instead of the full-night behavior the older fixtures implicitly represented.

A direct current control advances enemy updates for five seconds: the intro remains 30 seconds and `night()` stays false. Advancing the actual daylight owner then starts night. This is a test-coverage gap, not a confirmed gameplay defect; the main game advances its full simulation. The passing specialist suites establish their real mechanics/lifecycle in the tested contexts, but do not certify all permanent-night scaling/tempo interactions. The new explicit night/healing tests cover the boundary itself, not every specialist combination.

## Reproduction and limits

[check.cjs](../tools/survival-test-history-dev145/check.cjs) runs focused historical controls, not a historical full campaign. [Combined results](../tools/survival-test-history-dev145/combined-controls.json), individual controls and an [archive manifest](../tools/survival-test-history-dev145/archive-manifest.json) retain exact refs/inputs. The manifest lists the pinned Node runtime modules used for native dev.100/101 terrain; unchanged dev.101 modules reuse its immediate parent's identical source. Historical source copies are temporary and recoverable from those refs; they are not duplicated in the current game. `HISTORY_ARCHIVE_ROOT=/tmp node tools/survival-test-history-dev145/check.cjs` reproduces the controls after preparing the listed archives.

The rule control tests its immutable preservation receipt; the Nightmare control isolates actual pinned methods with explicit state, rather than claiming a complete old runtime run. The Cinder controls use actual pinned native runtimes and geometry. The travel control spans old native builds and current runtime, with the intervening identity change established from the source diff. Review-only static candidate lists do not imply the named suites all fail. No completed unchanged suite was rerun solely because another chat/update occurred.

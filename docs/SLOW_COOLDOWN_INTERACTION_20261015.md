# Timed Slow now reduces skill cooldown recovery — review candidate

**Owner request:** An enemy Rogue bind, snare, captain/boss Slow, projectile or trap Slow must protect a monster against player and companion ability spam, not merely impair foot movement. Apply the same rule to slowed monsters for predictable status semantics.

- `R.cooldownBalance.slowRecoveryMultiplier = 0.65` for heroes/companions; `enemySlowRecoveryMultiplier = 0.85` for monsters: every second that overlaps actual Slow counts as **0.65 / 0.85 seconds**, respectively, toward remaining skill cooldowns. Sustained Slow makes a cooldown take about **54% longer** to refill, but finite Slow only adds the missing recovery while active. A 3-second Slow adds up to 1.05 seconds of recovery debt to a longer cooldown.
- A skill activated **after** Slow begins starts its full normal cooldown, then immediately recovers at the impaired rate; both pre-existing and freshly activated cooldowns are treated alike. The smaller 15% enemy penalty limits near-permanent Mage frost from suppressing boss attacks too strongly.
- Keep **base cooldowns and talent reductions** intact. Countdown recovery slows temporarily; **do not extend the current timer on every Slow hit**, stack additive cooldown penalties or retroactively modify completed cooldowns.
- Hero Skills 1–8, charged variants, companion basic/global/first/second skill timers, companion Ranger healing/recovery and survival cooldowns, enemy basic/special/summon/recovery timers, captain overtime recruitment, Cindermaw protection, Dreadmaw feeding, Royal Prerogative interception and pending Rogue reaction deadlines all obey this. Status, phase, hazard, cast/warning and animation timers remain at wall-clock speed.
- Slow is still only applied after a valid hit to living nonimmune units; longer reapplications refresh the remaining duration without multiplying penalties. Mage frost now also slows monster skill recovery; this is a reciprocal balance consideration to evaluate against stronger hero skills in draft PR #305.
- Absolute encounter-clock deadlines (currently Rogue reactions and Royal Prerogative) must be adjusted using the **remaining time at the start of each frame**, not only when the deadline is still in the future at the end of a frame. This prevents a near-ready skill from bypassing Slow on its final frame.
- No new persisted field or migration. Save v4 already holds `unit.slow` and remaining cooldowns. Timers recover normally at Slow expiry, including an exact sub-frame expiry.
- Keep the new feature isolated from PR #305 and avoid merge/deploy until an integrated balance review with the completed hero progression overhaul, full automated checks, old-save and phone/desktop gameplay verification.

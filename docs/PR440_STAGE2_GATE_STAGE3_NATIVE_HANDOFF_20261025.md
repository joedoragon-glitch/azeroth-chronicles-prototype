Total native heal budget, maximum HP, self/other targeting, leader/ordinary eligibility, leader recovery clocks, interrupted-state rules, healer cooldown, and tick ownership are unchanged. This is a candidate tuning choice for owner balance review. No roll on an ineligible cast or later recovery tick.

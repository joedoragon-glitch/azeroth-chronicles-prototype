# Mechanics audit — v0.8.1, 4 October 2026

This audit compares the current prototype with the twelve expansion design documents in **Azeroth Chronicles Expansion Design October 2026** and the later author decisions recorded in the repository. It concentrates on executable rules and persistent state. The original documents explicitly leave numerical tuning and human playtesting open; later author changes to ringleader health, pursuit, enemy rewards, and controls supersede their initial values.

The ringleader sweep includes goblin, skeleton, mireling, reedbeast, wraith, wolf, ogre, orc, archer, stalker, ashbeast, and crownguard. Dungeon guardian variants keep their guard status, reduced reward basis, and ranged profile. The night-only correction fixes a pending event that previously took daytime stats if the two kills happened just before dawn.

The dungeon roll belongs to the **normal boss's first defeat**, not to every kill and not to the ordinary-species streak. A successful early roll is deliberately presented on reentry, as specified by the combat design and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. The awakening guarantee can make a failed early roll available later, so late encounters are not evidence of a repeated one-in-three roll.

`npm test` verifies the engine and UI logic; GitHub Actions runs browser checks and the portable build. Automated checks cannot establish enjoyable balance, encounter readability for every player, or an uninterrupted human completion of every route. The 35% potion threshold and automatic use across all three classes should be assessed in playtesting, particularly while kiting and in boss fights.

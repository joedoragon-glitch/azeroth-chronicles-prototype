## Historical captured-wound proposal (superseded at Charm); future growth/recovery contract remains Phase 4

These are **design requirements**, not a claim that all native mechanics have passed acceptance.

- **A wound persists through Charm.** A surviving lawful primary-hit capture must start with the creature's actual **post-hit HP fraction**, normalized onto its friendly companion maximum HP; it must never silently heal to full. Example: a 25/100 HP monster joining an ally archetype with 120 maximum HP becomes 30/120 HP. The fraction must be measured after the qualifying attack. Capture itself grants neither a free heal nor immunity. Verify fractional rounding/minimum 1 HP, recent combat hazards, save/restore, active-to-reserve transfer and zone transition, and do not accidentally reset to full at those boundaries.
- **Ordinary care and later song healing.** A wounded Charmed ally follows the same Barracks **treatment** rules as Soldiers, Rangers and pets, plus real healing from authored Rogue skills when implemented. A fallen Charmed ally remains owned, with its exact type ID, companion ID, name, pet-independent provenance and rank quota, until standard Barracks **recovery** restores the same member. Recovery is not a fresh capture and does not reroll Charm or erase provenance. Verify both native care methods with actual fallen and wounded captures.
- **Species-aware base stats with native level scaling.** Each legitimate captured species/subtype must have a vetted, normalized **friendly baseline** (HP, damage, armor, combat role and movement as needed), not a copied high-level hostile stat block. The baseline must increase with the **hero's current level** using the same companion progression pathway as Soldiers, Rangers and pets; it must honor available Vitality, shared-training, shared-strength and other legitimate inheritance. Capturing a Goblin at hero level 1 must not cause it to become obsolete when the hero levels up; capturing one later should yield appropriate same-type contemporary stats without pay-to-recruit pressure. Apply HP increases without free complete wound resets. Distinct monster types must not all be flattened permanently into numerically identical Soldiers/Archers. Initial role-based adapter is Phase 3 groundwork; subtype baseline/behavior is Phase 4.
- **Companion allegiance and behavior.** Friendly combat, movement, targeting, safety/retreat, Recall, formation and orders must use the proven *ally* engine, never blindly continue enemy AI/summons/quest actions. Ordinary Charmed species have the already-approved right to perform native construction/upgrade and, when unlocked, gathering. Test roles (Goblin melee vs Slinger ranged, large Ogre, hybrids, special enemy units) without introducing unapproved hostile abilities, skills or new paid rescue costs.

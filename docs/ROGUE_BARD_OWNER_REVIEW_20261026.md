## Historical pre-implementation decision overview (see current banner)

The fourth playable hero is a **female, guitar-playing Rogue Bard** who beats opponents through disruption, short-range musical AoE, proposed deployable musical stations, and automatic basic-hit Charm. She starts with **zero** companions, can never **newly hire** Soldier/Archer troops, and has **+1 active companion capacity from the beginning**. Eligible ordinary monsters permanently become friendly melee/ranged/healer companions with normal friendly role AI, persistent identity, level benefits, recovery and Barracks rules. Boss pets remain available. Bosses, captains, Ringleaders are not charmable. Hybrids and actual summons can defer. Her Endure keeps her at **1 HP for 2s**. The owner is now considering **two percentage points of personal critical chance per level** from Level 1 (+2pp/level, not yet final balance approval); no passive ordinary-stat boosts. The owner wants all eight regular/charged skills **combat-only and dual-sided AoE**: enemy harm and companion benefit in the same effect footprint, with bounded different-benefit stacking. A **fourth Rogue-only critical intensity group and adjustable Level 1–3 slider** is mandatory, independent of ordinary Hero, Companions and World.

**Owner's settled Succession rule:** old Rogue-charmed monsters survive **all** subsequent class changes, remaining owned and recoverable. Any non-Rogue successor cannot charm new ones. The existing Succession automatically presents all four registered classes; only small saved-class-count/roster-size validations and UI copy need updating.

## Baseline-first correction — latest owner direction

**Source-derived actual original level-1 classes** from `src/prototype/rules.js`: Paladin **HP 120 / Power 18 / Armor 8 / Speed 300**, Mage **90 / 22 / 3 / 300**, Ranger **105 / 20 / 5 / 320**. The Mage's **9** refers to **+9 Power per purchased Discipline rank**, not a 9 starting Power. The Rogue now adopts the **minimum existing base HP (90), Power (18), Armor (3) and Speed (300)** instead of invented intermediate values. 60 dormant MP is a proposal that also equals the minimum existing class MP. **No passive combat stats beyond the proposed hero-only crit progression.**

**Important:** three legacy heroes currently start with **Soldier and Archer**, while the Rogue starts with **zero** companions. Matching the minimum base stats does **not itself prove** level-1 balance; the first Charm's probability/time and survival are top-priority acceptance tests. Do not inflate Rogue stats to fix a bad opening—consider explicit, owner-approved Charm onboarding instead.

## Three implementation risks worth watching

1. **Converting an enemy into company** is the core work: clear hostile pack/healing/aggro/respawn/target state without rewarding a kill or duplicating the actor, and convert to a safe trusted role. First candidate is a tagged Soldier/Archer with validated monster species provenance and ordinary friendly AI; alternate full type only if needed. Source stats are companion-normalized without leaking hostile elite/night multipliers. The exact profile calibration remains open.
2. **Succession and roster validation:** \`save.js\` still hardcodes a maximum of 3 fallen classes and Game Over exactly at 3, despite dynamic class choice. It also rejects a Rogue's seventh active companion. \`party.js\` troop formations have 6 slots and recovery recreates by type, losing charmed species. Each has a targeted fix; no redesign of Succession or navigation.
3. **Performance and visual honesty:** static heroine and crowned form; 1 bounded AoE action with max 3/5 targets, no added auto projectile actor, max 2 static magical music stations, no reserve AI, no live enemy-AI code on converted units, no body/pose/dance animation. Bounded audio and effects use existing Canvas/Pixi/PWA architecture.

No implication that the effect values or implementation are approved. Auto and finisher names were not in the displayed paired-name table, so keep them as design candidates.


### Latest two-sided performance / critical specification (27 October)

**Review first:** [16-action native critical-tier matrix, eight simultaneous enemy/ally AoE pairs and fourth independent Rogue intensity slider design](ROGUE_BARD_DUAL_SIDED_PERFORMANCES_CRITICAL_TIERS_20261027.md). This now supersedes the earlier strictly debuff-focused single-target effects and one-per-level (+1pp) chance candidate. **Proposed Rogue personal crit chance**: Level 1 **12%**, Level 15 **40%**, Level 18 **46%** without Codices; **17% / 45% / 51%** with all five. Her intensity control defaults to **Level 1** independently of the other three, and only affects critical *strength*, not chance. The merged #440 source currently exposes developer/native tuning methods, **not a persistent visual in-game slider**; making it independently adjustable in the game is additional UI/settings work, not accomplished by this doc-only PR.

**Latest owner refinement:** the two halves of a performance must be **thematic opposites**, not arbitrary bonuses. The separate existing-trap disarm talent remains an earlier owner idea; replacing two physical placed traps with music stations is a **promising candidate, not yet a confirmed policy replacement**. Companion cast protection can cover existing damage-based charged interruption, but generic enemy-imposed Silence immunity requires a verified native source. *Dragging Waltz* Slows enemies while giving nearby companions **speed + cooldown recovery**; *Broken Cadence* interrupts/Silences foes while providing **temporary protection for legal friendly skill casts against actual damage-interruption/new Silence**; *Withering Lament* damages/suppresses hostile healing while granting **HoT + increased incoming healing** to friends. *Stolen Tempo* inflicts cooldown debt while accelerating companion cooldowns. *Reeling Rondo* pushes/Weakens foes and grants companions stronger attacks/conditional stability. *The Silencing Coda* damages/silences enemies while briefly boosting friends' **critical chance + special damage**. *Snaring Tango/Thornstep Tarantella* now place at most **two static magical sound stations**, not physical traps: enemies inside are hindered, friends **currently inside** are protected. Different song families may all overlap; the old proposed arbitrary four-boon cap is discarded. Same-family refreshes, outgoing-damage caps and combined Skill 2+6 recovery cap remain proposals.

**All 16 normal/charged attacks are small-area combat-only performances**; target/ally admission shares real footprint and range rather than a zone-wide passive. Merged #440 criticals enhance **one native enemy OR ally consequence** per legal cast at Level 1–3; all other parts still occur at baseline. The exact revised 8-pair and 16-form matrix is [here](ROGUE_BARD_DUAL_SIDED_PERFORMANCES_CRITICAL_TIERS_20261027.md). At critical release only **one** authored native lever gets extra strength per level (a hostile debuff/damage **or** an allied boon), preserving the original deterministic shared receipt. Boss/pet and inherited captured-monster crit intensity does not follow the Rogue dial.

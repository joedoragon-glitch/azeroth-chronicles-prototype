## Deferred native acceptance

Review the named changed cases individually at 100/150/175% zoom on 1280×800 desktop and supported portrait/landscape phone sizes. Review front/side facing, sprites mixed with procedural bodies, day/night, TRUE/rank finishes, pet charge/status cues, depth/grounding/occlusion and label clearance. Check wolves mounted by human riders after the shared anatomy change. Check ceilings/entrances and crowded towns without moving gameplay geometry to hide a drawing problem. Verify the Wraith lamp remains a local light rather than a halo replacement. Inspect all eleven pet galleries and full-party scenes; screenshots of a gallery alone do not establish play readability.

The revised browser fixture and existing snapshot visual capture suite are authored for this review, **not executed**. Regional images are saved before overwrite. All retained body fixtures receive default-camera desktop/portrait captures; every changed contract receives all five native viewports at the default camera, with default desktop/portrait day/night comparisons. Eight representative bodies/materials/entrances cover the additional motion/zoom cross-product; the all-eleven pet-facing/skill and five-region matrices still cover those axes comprehensively. Existing production sprite/interior/AV gates supply remaining contexts. A capture or nonempty actor alone cannot approve proportions, readability or beauty.

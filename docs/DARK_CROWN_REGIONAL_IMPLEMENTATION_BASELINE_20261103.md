### Actual visual reference generation

`tests/dark-crown-regional-baseline.test.cjs` is a narrow, repeatable, **real Campaign/Canvas** reference and safety test. It renders 23 distinct scenes twice at **150% camera**: an 880×560 desktop canvas and a 390×680 phone-sized *canvas crop* (not a physical phone or phone-specific UI). Frames include the two bridges, city arrival/markets/homes, Ash Refuge, heat and freight, Foundry exterior and interior, Citadel, Treasury, Cindermaw's domain, orrery, crystal site, fortress and contrasting night treatments. Each shot records camera displacement, actors, and a SHA-256 fingerprint. Render must not mutate simulation. Saved Foundry wall/enemy identities are asserted after restore. When CI runs this test, its PNGs and scene index appear under the existing `environment-708-wraith-and-regions` artifact's `dark-crown-707` subdirectory. This is **not** automatic art acceptance; someone must examine framing, usable walk space, daylight/night layering and factual occupancy.

**Status:** native captures and test results are **pending CI** until an exact-head workflow succeeds. Not yet evidence of design completion.

## Engineering design — before new city assets

1. **Civic city/terrain:** Draft a district overlay using current geography, rather than a new entire map. Crownwatch is a balanced stone/ceramic/glass city with elegant common architecture, protected gardens, open public squares and inhabited courtyard housing; Ash Refuge keeps its quiet residential/artisan role. Keep street frontage open at 150% and phone scale. Use existing original procedural sprites as blueprint, not generic Gothic art.
2. **Heat/industry:** Diagram physical hot-zone intake → indirect exchanger → protected flues → separate kilns, drying, heated halls/washhouses → service and waste routes. Map receiving, sorting, cooling, finishing and outbound yards for multiple glassworks and ceramics; distributed yards plus one restrained freight focus. The current isolated decorative props are a starting scaffold, not proof the chain is operational.
3. **Water/bathhouse feasibility (unresolved):** Study a source/replenishment budget before approving cistern geometry or bathhouse placement. Existing Ash Refuge well, imported provincial water, local subsurface recovery, and rainfall capture are *candidates to investigate*, not established safe supply. Address protected catchment, treatment, potable/bathing separation, thermal transfer, drainage and disposal. A selected bathhouse aesthetic is not permission to show water arriving magically.
4. **Foundry engineering:** Retain `side-crown-foundry`, existing entrance, guards, encounter/traps, reward and Codex ownership. Spatially redesign a real operational floor with receiving/inventory, preparation, hot stations, cooling/inspection, finished storage, shipping, worker circulation, rest and guard separation. Existing hostile guards do not count as productive staff. Worker presentation needs purposeful animation/ambient task and collision-safe placement; do not invent named worker lore, quests or manufactured goods until authored.
5. **Government and ecology:** Maintain gradient from civilian pride to checkpoints, the professional well-crafted Citadel, Cindermaw's public/private contrast, communal Ash-beast geothermal habitation and supply-backed fortress; keep Ironroot's Crown mint and Frontier's Abyss Bastion project in their original regions.

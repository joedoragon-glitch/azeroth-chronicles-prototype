# Abyss Bastion: the Dark Lord's air-superiority project

Joel's 8 October 2026 decision supersedes the earlier unresolved dragon politics and generic containment interpretation.

The Dark Lord already rules the land and wants to rule the air above it. He chose the purple Abyss Dragon because it matches his preferred clothing colors. The dragon belongs to him and is his personal flying mount: his dragon equivalent of Air Force One. He enjoys flying and has already flown on it.

Abyss Bastion is the preparation center for scaling that relationship into an air force. The project breeds more dragons, prepares riders and flight equipment, supplies them, and builds the facilities they will need. Air superiority is the Dark Lord's next priority for consolidating his rule; expansion beyond the current frontiers is a possible later ambition. The air force is a project in progress, not an already deployed fleet.

The dragon is not a generic prisoner. Its royal aerie, hatchery, feeding/service wing, rider preparation and flight-training spaces serve the project. Chains and anchors are equipment, logistics and flight preparation. Guards protect the facility and the ruler's dragon. The separate Frontier transport roost is not automatically the Abyss Dragon or a completed military fleet.

## Visible implementation v0.8.86

- Purple royal dragon standards connect the project to the Dark Lord.
- A flight-planning table depicts projected aerial routes and dragon formations.
- Harness stations carry saddles, stirrups, straps and maintenance tools.
- The royal launch platform has open framing, spare beams and tools: construction remains unfinished.
- Existing egg cradles, hatchery, warming braziers, feed stores and feeding structures support population growth.
- First entry explains the project in a brief gameplay message.

Decorative props keep their existing coordinates and zero collision. Existing saves update presentation without resetting occupants or progress. Guardians, traps, attacks, boss anatomy, levels, rewards, Eren's rescue and dungeon geometry remain unchanged. This decision does not assign Eren a new forced-labor role, replace summon species, add flight gameplay, introduce an air-force quest, or settle how the dragon was acquired.

Cindermaw and Dreadmaw retain their current procedural appearances, as Joel confirmed on 8 October. Their proposed alternate designs were previews and are not approved replacements.

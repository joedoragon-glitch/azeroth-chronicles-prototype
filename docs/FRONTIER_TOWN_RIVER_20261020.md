# Frontier town, river and recovery scenery — dev.108

Joel's nine 20 October screenshots show Emberwatch buildings disconnected from streets, unrelated fence fragments and crowded work props, riverbank/bridge clutter, a brown cap at the channel's map edge, repeated triangular poppies, an isolated cut-channel feature, and an unexplained flat circle. He requested aesthetic correction using the available space, more naturally varied poppies, and a grain-storage silo in place of the circle. The earlier dev.105 edge-plot review did not establish a coherent town composition; this is a separate, completed native scene audit of these reported areas.

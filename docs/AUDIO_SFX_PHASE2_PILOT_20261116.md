## Real physical source and provenance

The complete cue list, deterministic seeds, physical gestural intentions and material identity are in `tools/audio/sfx-phase2-pilot.json`. ## Eight in-game owner routes — draft only

1. **Rogue physical bell:** `audio-hero.js:heroSkillEffect` keeps its existing event gate, 75ms rapid-repeat guard, warning-sensitive presentation and 38-voice ceiling. It attempts one flat valid `effect.rogueBellBasic` or `effect.rogueBellEmphatic` recording and *returns without synthesis when warm*. When cold, failed or unready, the original short procedural shell/rattle runs; no delayed catch-up sound or duplicate enemy-by-enemy impact.
2. **Lifebinder cold wave:** `audio-enemy.js:enemyExpression` attempts `effect.lifebinderAlgorRelease` only when `family=crypt`, `skillId=boss/crypt/4`, `stage=release`. A warm recording replaces the matching material *release*, not the warning, a damaging impact or phase. On cold/failure, original `effect.enemyRelease` and generic material/noise fallback remain. The 30 Phase 1 assets and original material binding indices are preserved.
3. **Earned quest payout:** Existing `effect.questComplete` gets the short new sound; when warm it replaces its synthetic note. For paired `quest(automatic=true)` within 0.18 seconds, `audio-effects.js` suppresses the second routine note even on cold playback. Unrelated quest UI and earned state remain unchanged.
4. **Genuine Charmed and construction receipts:** `audio-effects.js` substitutes the warmed `effect.charmedWraithHeritage` only for an authentic Charmed Wraith `spell(kind=charmHeritage)`, without adding sound to the paired visual `charmHeritage`. The original generic spell synth remains the cold fallback. The existing `effect.construction` now binds `barracks-complete` only on the actual Barracks finalized receipt from `party.js`, with fallback to its prior synthesized sound.
5. **Mage physical orb:** The `heroProjectileFlight` for `class=mage,slot=1,style=magic,!charged` is emitted only once the projectile actually exists. The drafted `audio-hero.js` warm recording replaces the one launch voice; early cast and manual Skill-1 beam stay out. Cold or warning-priority contexts keep the old softer synth.
6. **TRUE Thornpup Tripwire:** After one or more genuine contacts, `party-pets.js` now emits the preexisting `spell` enriched with only observation metadata `petFamily,slot,hits,skill`. `audio-effects.js` swaps `effect.thornTripwire` for that *one* generic pet `magicCast`, preserving cold fallback. The `petSkill` visual release/impact remains silent; blocked/wrong-slot/wrong-family cases never get a recorded success.
7. **Audio registration:** Exactly **38 effect recordings**, 84 music, 34 ambience; **101 director event keys** (95 original + six valid flat names). Every registered audio file is hash-addressed, offline-package eligible, and the generated runtime catalog + service worker are refreshed using the existing build contract. All eight recordings are draft-only playable assets; the original preview source folder itself stays outside the PWA package. No save-version change, new skill, target logic, boss damage, Charm probability, pet behavior or gameplay RNG change.

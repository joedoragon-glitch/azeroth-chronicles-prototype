# Awakening and the Crown Peace — beta conclusion (issue #248)

## Story decision: there was peace; it was not freedom

The Dark Lord was once a player-controlled hero. When that controlling player left, he and the five adventurers who followed him became their own people. Their campaign had also left casualties, rival species and devastated communities behind. He brought the five regions under one rule, stopped generations of violence, protected trade and bridges, and made a common currency circulate. Those achievements were genuine. So were the tribute, forced work, detention and restrictions used to keep that order.

The five dungeon bosses are **the old party**, not five arbitrary strong enemies. Each holds a different means of imposing the same peace after the Dark Lord is defeated:

| Dungeon / persistent family | Successor's remaining foundation | Meaning of a TRUE victory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Crypt / `crypt`: **Lifebinder** (oathwright identity proposed in draft #258) | Allowing fallen people to continue as self-aware, free-willed skeletons, with memory and personality intact; some may follow their preserver out of gratitude, but none is bound to obey | His possible following cannot be treated as an obedient undead army; TRUE defeat prevents him from becoming its unquestioned sovereign, not from allowing skeletons to live |
| Sunken Archive / `archive`: **Drowned Keeper** | Recorded knowledge, counsel and the capacity to shape what later rulers believe and administer | No keeper can monopolize the realm's memory as grounds for a new Crown |
| Colossus Mine / `mine`: **Stone Colossus** (**Garun the Firstborn**, owner-corrected #253) | The Dark Lord's loyal Ogre Bastion, council architect of roads, relocated buildings and monster settlements; his Mine-security wardens, engineers and built infrastructure could impose *compulsory shelter*. The Ridge Tyrant, **not Garun**, runs the currency and accounts. | A loyal architect can still turn protective infrastructure into compulsory confinement, even without ever plotting betrayal |
| Abyss Bastion / `abyss`: **Abyss Dragon** (**Veyrith**, proposed #254) | Voluntary aerial pathfinder and old friend, still the purple personal flown mount; his instructors, nest routes, landing permissions and **unfinished** air-force could become coercive flight clearance | No dragon can make safe return routes and access to the skies conditional on obedience to a new sovereign |
| Citadel of Ashes / `citadel`: **Ash Sentinel** (**Marshal Veyra**, proposed #255) | A loyal coastal road warden, Crown drillmaster and practicing cosmetologist who professionalized goblin, ogre, human and other irregulars into the standing army; her Citadel officers, doctrine and garrisons could militarize everyday life | Her trained soldiers must not automatically inherit military rule simply because the Dark Lord is gone; she has the capacity, not the desire, to become a dictator |

This is a **bounded synthesis for #248**, not an override of character-ticket authorship. The Lifebinder's life-binding, the Keeper's Archive, the Dragon's purple personal-mount/unfinished-air-program canon and all five family IDs are established *directions*. The newly opened, still **unmerged/unapproved** character PRs provide sharper *candidates*: [#258](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/258) proposes the Lifebinder's **Brook-like conscious skeletons**, retaining their memories, personality and free will with no necessary memorial-anchor upkeep; [#253](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/253) records the owner's correction to **Garun the Firstborn**: loyal friend and council architect, collector of geometric art and useless machines, with fairly paid but still captive Dara; the formerly proposed Kharun resentment narrative is rejected; [#254](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/254) proposes Veyrith's air-navigation convictions; [#255](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/255) records the owner's correction to **Marshal Veyra**, professional military drillmaster and practicing cosmetologist, loyal to the Dark Lord and friendly with Garun; the previous refugee-rescuer/cinder-scarred idealist origin is rejected. [#252](https://github.com/joedoragon-glitch/azeroth-chronicles-prototype/pull/252) proposes the Dark Lord's six-signature Victory ledger and Archive clue. These sketches support five **different** succession claims, but none is treated as already shipped or owner-accepted by this draft. The Dark Lord's historical class, previous government and the present hero's ancestry remain open. The original party numbered **six total**—the former hero plus exactly five companions; there is no sixth dungeon companion.

## What defeating the five changes

The TRUE victories break *personal succession*: each former companion has the power to rally or inherit a distinctive coalition around the old coercive bargain, whether or not they personally wanted rulership, whether through grateful bound people, records, protected passages, landing clearances or a standing army. Defeat breaks **that claimant's personal ability to enforce universal obedience**; it does not erase their people's agency, demolish roads, destroy the Archive, unmint the crowns, ban all flight or settle every local dispute. The Lifebinder's conscious skeletons remain people in their own right, and the defeat of their maker does not revoke their choices. Garun and Veyra need not turn against their old friend: institutional continuity can become dangerous after his death without advance treachery.

The old peace is not an illusion: generations of neighbors have already learned to use the same ferries, markets, repair roads, refugee sites and common currency. When no warlord can claim the whole infrastructure, local inhabitants have an immediate practical reason to keep those shared services working. They now continue them by ordinary cooperation, not because the fortress ordered it. The postgame neutral residents in the Citadel, the Archive, Mine, Bastion, Crypt, side interiors and recovered Treasuries make this visible without a new diplomacy or government mechanic. Such peace remains **chosen and fallible**, not a permanent magical pacification of their wills.

The abandoned-player conceit receives one last quiet echo: the present hero may someday be tempted by the empty throne, but no option, compulsory coronation or sixth boss is added. The player is trusted to notice the resemblance.


## Character and encounter integration caution — 11 October owner revisions

- **Lifebinder (#258):** The owner's Brook comparison supersedes maintenance-anchor speculation. The undead are people continuing as skeletons, not controlled summons in the political story. Existing enemy summoning remains a combat abstraction until any wider roster is explicitly implemented.
- **Garun (#253):** His loyalty, council work, odd private taste and Dara's paid captivity are character commitments. The owner has **locked a seven-move combat specification**, with genuine structural effects rather than engineering labels alone: Punching Shear, Progressive Collapse, Dynamic Compaction, Pile Driving, Lateral Load Transfer, Passive Earth Pressure and Mass Underpinning. The draft explicitly says the full intended mechanics are **not yet implemented or validated**. #248 must not claim the redesign is already active or try to absorb it into the peace engine.
- **Marshal Veyra (#255):** Her coastal origins, genuine cosmetology, military professionalization, loyalty and friendship with Garun replace the earlier burn-survivor/voluntary-muster narrative. An owner-directed five-move encounter redesign proposes a canister fan, soldier formation, five-leg charge, independent 4×4 resurfacing board and Volumize summon. The PR reports partial mechanical work and failing shared VFX/audio coverage; the exact head and full gameplay/browser equivalence require independent verification. These are not peace-mechanic prerequisites.
- **Veyrith (#254):** The voluntary aerial navigator, personal purple mount and future flight-clearance threat remain consistent with #248. His air arm is still not deployed, and his concrete biography remains an unmerged proposal.
- **Drowned Keeper:** Still the knowledge/Archive pillar. No new owner correction has been found in the reviewed character drafts; do not invent one to balance the others.

These changes refine **who** the five are and **what could go wrong**, without changing which five `true` flags gate peace or requiring the former companions to seek power deliberately.

## Player-facing conclusion

> The Dark Lord ended the old wars. He also left five former companions able to enforce his peace after his death. Their claims are broken now.
>
> The bridges stand; ferries still run. Crowns pass between people and creatures who once fought one another. They keep the roads open without a command from the Citadel.
>
> At the Dark Fortress, someone has placed a plain chair beside the empty throne, for any traveler who wishes to speak.
>
> You could sit in either. No voice tells you which.

The existing `Peace for everyone` dialog and its continue/Nightmare/export actions remain. The Awakening checklist states that **only missing TRUE victories** remain. No branch, quest or level system was added.

## Engine contract and narrowly targeted fix

- Source: `src/prototype/engine.js`, `src/prototype/world.js`, `src/prototype/save.js`, and `src/prototype/app.js`.
- `s.true.darklord && dungeonIds.every(id => s.true[id])` remains the **only** peace gate. `dungeonIds` is derived from the five `kind: 'dungeon'` records, not the field bosses. The five prior TRUE results survive across saves; `s.late` only records TRUE wins earned during Awakening. No five-new-fights requirement.
- `awaken()` still fixes the remaining guardian/boss level to the captured victorious tier and grants pending TRUE access for defeated normal dungeons. It now reports the **actual remaining** companion count instead of falsely announcing that all five rose again. All five pre-earned TRUE flags transition immediately to peace.
- `checkEnding()` still clears pending fights, traps/projectiles, harmful quest objectives and instantiated enemies. `makeHabitat()` still retains the neutral outdoor field bosses. Outdoor areas preserve their established populations; major dungeons receive six nonhostile occupants, side rooms five and Treasuries four. Interior visitors deliberately include species from neighboring regions. Traps remain inactive in peace. These are low-stat, non-rewarding neutral scene actors, not new AI or trade systems.
- **Confirmed gap repaired:** new Treasury and side-dungeon `zone()` creation returned early before calling `makeHabitat()`. Both now populate as peaceful on their first post-ending visit. Historical peaceful Treasury saves with `habitat: true` but empty occupants gain residents on reentry. No rescue, cache, trap-coordinate, travel, player-class, combat, save-schema or boss-family migration was rewritten.
- The `checkEnding()` and `makeHabitat()` path is in the existing campaign; the conclusion changes only player-facing prose. Field enemies in peace remain neutral and cannot attack or be attacked under the existing targeting rules. Post-ending scavenging and already-freed specialists remain intact.
